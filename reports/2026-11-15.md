# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-11-15

*Weekend edition. Few new deals came out in the past 24 hours, so this brief focuses on updates to live deals that set up a big week ahead.*

- **Anthropic IPO could price above its $70–$78 range:** The FT reports that underwriters are considering a higher price after orders reached about 6x the 825M shares on offer. That could push the valuation to $1.5T+ and the raise past $100B. Nothing is confirmed yet. Pricing is expected Wed Nov 18, with Nasdaq trading as "ANT" on Thu Nov 19. ([BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/c3d7c432-77e7-4c65-a4dd-e4d7bad34795), [TradingKey](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/262304476-anthropic-ipo-ant-price-above-78-orders-six-times-november-14-2026-tradingkey), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-ipo-price-range-of-70-to-78-per-share-to-reach-an-implied-valuation-of-1-404-trillion/))
- **Big Tech's $26B Anthropic anchor order is now a retail talking point:** Nvidia and Alphabet ($10B each), Microsoft ($1B), Micron, Broadcom, AMD and TSMC are all committed to buy at the IPO price. A higher price means these companies pay more for stakes in a major customer. ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/11/14/nvidia-alphabet-microsoft-and-micron-are-buying-anthropic-ipo/))
- **Nvidia reports Tuesday (Nov 17), its first earnings under interim CEO Colette Kress:** The company guided to $108B in revenue (±2%) against a consensus of about $109B. Analysts will press on whether the $10B Anthropic pledge still stands after Jensen Huang's exit. NVDA fell 12.75% last week, and several analysts have cut their targets. ([Invezz](https://invezz.com/news/2026/11/13/nvidia-earnings-preview-q3-kress-huang/), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/nvidia-earnings-along-baggage-top-053700154.html), [JournalArta](https://journalarta.com/en/2026/11/15/nvidia-first-post-jensen-earnings-kress-anthropic-ipo-week/))
- **Samsung–Modine is still only talks:** MOD closed Friday up 28% at $135.66, about $7.2B in market value. Seoul Economic Daily estimates a $7.5–8B price, which would be Samsung's largest deal ever. There's no signed deal, and a CFIUS review is a real risk because Modine supplies US AI infrastructure. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-12/samsung-is-in-talks-to-acquire-data-center-cooling-firm-modine), [Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/business/2026/11/13/samsung-in-talks-to-buy-us-cooling-maker-modine-for-ai-data), [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/samsung-modine-talks-data-centre-cooling))
- **Fractile raises $720M at a $7B valuation:** The UK AI-inference chip startup's Series C was led by investors including Thrive, Founders Fund and Lightspeed. It's another large private bet on challengers to Nvidia in inference. ([Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/11/12/startup-funding-news-today-november-12-2026-fractile-uforce-agile-robots-imprint-more/))
- **AMD–Meta $1B equity tie-up is back in focus:** Coverage this weekend re-ran AMD's plan to buy $1B of Meta's equity offering alongside Nvidia's $3B, with AMD Helios racks now in testing at Meta. Read it as a reminder that chipmakers are funding their own customers, not as a new deal. ([NewsBytes](https://newsbytes.ph/2026/11/14/amd-follows-nvidias-ai-investment-strategy-with-1-b-meta-deal/), [HPCwire](https://www.hpcwire.com/aiwire/2026/10/27/amd-details-meta-ai-infrastructure-collaboration-plans-1b-investment/))

**Synthesis:** No new deals this weekend, but next week is the biggest test of the AI trade this year. Nvidia reports Tuesday, Anthropic prices Wednesday and trades Thursday. If Anthropic prices above its range and Nvidia beats with a clear answer on its $10B pledge, the sell-off in AI chips (NVDA, AMD, AVGO, MU, TSM) could reverse quickly. A weak Nvidia print would put more weight on the "circular money" critique, where chipmakers fund their own customers. Outside chips, data-center cooling and power is the clearest M&A theme: watch MOD for a signed Samsung deal and VRT, NVT and TT for read-through. GOOGL and AMZN carry the largest paper gains from a strong Anthropic debut.
