# 2026-10-18

*Sunday: markets closed and few new announcements. Several items below are carryovers still driving Monday's trading.*

- **Replit / OpenAI vs. Anthropic:** Replit's CEO said at TechCrunch Disrupt (Oct 17) that Replit has stopped working with Anthropic and now defaults to OpenAI, citing cost and performance. A source close to the deal disputes this, and Replit has made no formal announcement.
- **Amazon / Decart (reported, unconfirmed):** Amazon (AMZN) is reportedly in talks to buy AI startup Decart for about $7B. Nothing is signed.
- **Zen Technologies / Cogknit:** Zen Technologies' board approved a 51.98% stake in defence-AI startup Cogknit for up to about $14M (Oct 17). It is small and Indian-listed, so there is little US market impact.
- **Proofpoint / Varonis (carryover):** Thoma Bravo-backed Proofpoint agreed to buy Varonis (VRNS) for $49.75 per share in cash (~$6.3B, 52% premium to its unaffected price).
- **IonQ / Elisity (carryover):** IonQ (IONQ) is buying security firm Elisity in an all-stock deal of up to ~$1.3B, and investors worry about dilution.
- **Schneider Electric / PTC (carryover):** Schneider agreed on Oct 5 to buy industrial software maker PTC (PTC) for $205 per share in cash (~$22.6B equity value, ~42% premium). Close is expected in Q3 2027.
- **Anthropic IPO (unconfirmed timing):** Reports still point to an upcoming Anthropic listing, but there is no public S-1 or price range that I could verify.

**Takeaway:** It was a quiet weekend, so Monday will mostly trade on the earlier software take-outs (VRNS, PTC, CCCS) as merger-arb names. The theme is unchanged: private equity and strategic buyers are paying large premiums for software and security assets, while AI model vendors compete hard for partners. Watch AMZN for confirmation of the Decart deal, IONQ for share-issuance pressure, and any Anthropic S-1, which would be the biggest tech listing catalyst.
