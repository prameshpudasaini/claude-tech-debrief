# 2026-09-19

## Tech & Market-Moving Deals

- **Generac struck an $8 billion long-term supply deal with Amazon** to provide backup generators for Amazon's data centers, with $2.4B of deliveries locked in for 2027–2028; the agreement also hands Amazon warrants on up to ~1.7M Generac shares (~$200/share), and GNRC stock has surged from roughly $175 to near $250 since the news broke — the latest sign that hyperscalers are locking up physical power-equipment supply chains to keep pace with AI data-center buildout.
- **SpaceX's weighting in the Nasdaq-100 more than doubled** in Friday's quarterly index rebalance, an event expected to trigger an estimated $15.5–22 billion in programmatic/index-fund buying — a mechanical but market-moving flow investors are watching into the close.
- **Crusoe Energy closed a $3.9 billion Series F at a $30.9 billion post-money valuation**, co-led by Atreides Management and Valor Equity Partners, underscoring investors' continued willingness to pay up for AI data-center and compute-infrastructure capacity even outside the public markets.
- **Oura's IPO is heating up**: the smart-ring maker's SEC S-1 shows revenue up 74% to $1.21B for the nine months through June, with 5 million paid members; Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan, Allen & Co. and Jefferies are leading the Nasdaq listing (ticker OURA) — one of the more anticipated consumer-tech IPOs of the year.
- **Anthropic is reportedly moving toward filing its IPO paperwork this fall**, with reports pointing to a valuation target as high as $2 trillion following its $965B private round in May — a listing of that size would be one of the largest tech IPOs ever and a bellwether for AI-sector public-market appetite.
- **Qualcomm confirmed its $3.9 billion acquisition of Modular**, a startup whose software lets AI workloads run efficiently across different chip architectures, reinforcing Qualcomm's broader data-center AI push alongside its recently announced customer relationships with Microsoft and Meta.
- **Crypto-adjacent tech stocks rallied in premarket trading** after bitcoin crossed $78,000, with Coinbase and Robinhood each up roughly 1–2.5% — a reminder that crypto-linked equities remain a fast-moving swing factor for tech-sector sentiment.

## What It Means

The dominant theme remains the AI infrastructure buildout spilling beyond chips and cloud contracts into physical supply chains — Generac's warrant-linked power deal with Amazon mirrors a pattern (also seen with Qualcomm's hyperscaler tie-ups) of Big Tech locking in capacity from smaller, specialized suppliers in exchange for equity upside, which is worth watching for other power/cooling/grid names. Meanwhile, capital continues to flood AI compute providers privately (Crusoe's $3.9B round) even as the IPO pipeline reopens for both AI labs (Anthropic's reported $2T listing target) and adjacent consumer-tech names (Oura). Near-term, GNRC, AMZN, and QCOM are the tickers most directly in play from confirmed deals, while SpaceX's Nasdaq-100 rebalance and bitcoin's move above $78K add mechanical, sentiment-driven volatility to watch into the close. The overall signal: AI-driven capital expenditure keeps widening its footprint across power, semiconductors, and infrastructure software, and public-market investors are being offered more ways to get direct exposure to it.
