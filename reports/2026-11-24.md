# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-11-24

- **Tesla and SpaceX are reportedly negotiating a ~$3.5T all-stock merger:** The Financial Times reports that each company has formed a special committee (three Tesla directors, two SpaceX directors). Morgan Stanley and Goldman Sachs are advising SpaceX, and Evercore is advising Tesla. Insiders say a deal is not close, the exchange ratio and Musk's voting control are the main sticking points, and neither company has confirmed the talks. ([Yahoo Finance/Reuters](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/spacex-tesla-directors-negotiate-potential-062339987.html), [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/24/tesla-spacex-merger-year-end-deadline-new-congress/), [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/tesla-spacex-directors-merger-talks-valuation-gap-1834454))
- **The sides are reportedly aiming to sign by year-end:** Any deal would need a majority of *all* eligible Tesla shares to vote yes, so abstaining counts as a no. Regulators could also object, given Tesla's business in China and SpaceX's rocket and Starlink work. ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/spacex-tesla-merger-five-directors-special-committees), [Stocktwits](https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/tesla-spacex-5-directors-lead-merger-discussions-investor-talks-warp-speed/cNbsbrxR9ke))
- **Chip stocks gave back some of Monday's rally before the open:** Monday's rally followed Nvidia reinstating Jensen Huang as CEO. On Tuesday, NVDA fell about 3.5–6% before the open after jumping 31.5% Monday to $165.05. Micron fell about 6.7%, Intel about 6%, and AMD, Applied Materials and Lam Research about 3–4% each. ([Yahoo Finance UK](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/u-chip-stocks-fall-premarket-122411709.html), [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4676053-chip-ai-stocks-retreat-after-record-breaking-rally-on-huangs-return?feed_item_type=news), [Bloomingbit](https://en.bloomingbit.io/feed/news/125129))
- **Sierra raised $1.75B at a $30B valuation:** Sierra is Bret Taylor's company that builds AI agents for customer service. Greenoaks and Lightspeed led the round, and the valuation nearly doubled from about $15.8B in May. Big valuations for private AI-agent companies put pressure on publicly traded customer-service and CRM software makers (CRM, NOW, HUBS, FIVN). ([Ventureburn](https://ventureburn.com/sierra-raises-1-75b-series-f-30b-valuation/), [MLQ](https://mlq.ai/news/sierra-says-it-is-finalizing-a-funding-round-led-by-lightspeed-and-greenoaks-at-a-30-billion-valuation/))
- **Workday completed its purchase of C1.ai:** The deal closed Nov. 23 and adds AI-agent features to Workday's HR and finance software. It is a small deal, but it shows established software companies are buying AI companies rather than building everything themselves. ([Business Wire M&A](https://www.businesswire.com/newsroom/subject/merger-acquisition))
- **Cybersecurity deals are still in focus:** The market is still digesting CrowdStrike's $43.9B all-stock purchase of Zscaler, announced Monday and the biggest cybersecurity deal ever. It comes just after Palo Alto Networks agreed to buy Applied Compute for $4.5B. In other deals, Sanofi is buying Insmed for $41B, and industrial company Terex is selling its Genie aerial-lift business to Linamar for $1.4B. Neither is a tech deal, but both show deal-making is busy overall. ([rallies.ai](https://rallies.ai/news/deals-of-the-day-mergers-and-acquisitions-3840611e23373635), [Benzinga M&A calendar](https://www.benzinga.com/calendars/m-a), [Terex 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000097216/000110465926131293/tm2630439d1_ex99-1.htm))
- **The IPO calendar is still quiet:** No US tech IPOs are pricing in Thanksgiving week. Anthropic (ANT) is still trading choppily after its record $81.3B IPO on Nov. 19, and Nscale (NSCL) is still waiting to price. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-ipo-ai-offering-price.html), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/anthropics-ipo-puts-ais-circular-financing-boom-to-the-test/))

**Synthesis:** The big story today is the reported Tesla–SpaceX merger talks. Expect TSLA to swing on every headline about the exchange ratio, because that number decides whether Tesla holders get a good or bad deal. Because Musk controls SpaceX but not Tesla, the shareholder vote will be harder to win than the headlines suggest. The chip stocks' pullback before the open looks like profit-taking after Monday's huge move rather than a change in the story. Still, this week's inflation report and the Fed's December decision could move NVDA, MU and AMD more than deal news will. AI-agent software companies keep raising money at higher valuations (Sierra, Cognition's reported $75B talks), which is a growing threat to customer-service and enterprise software stocks. Cybersecurity is still the hottest area for deals among publicly traded companies.

*Note: Today had few new tech deals. Some items above were announced Nov. 23 or are follow-ups to earlier deals. The Tesla–SpaceX talks are based on an FT report that the companies have not confirmed. Premarket figures differ from source to source, so confirm against live quotes before trading.*
