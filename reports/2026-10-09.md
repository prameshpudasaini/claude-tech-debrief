# 2026-10-09

- **Anthropic IPO nears:** Reuters-sourced reports say Anthropic (confidential filing June 1, Nasdaq chosen) could start roadshows as early as mid-October, with a listing before the November midterms and a raise of roughly $60B at a valuation near $1–2T. Not yet confirmed by the company; a public S-1 is the next trigger.
- **Oura pulls its IPO:** The smart-ring maker postponed its ~$2.2B Nasdaq IPO (priced to value it near $15B) on Sept. 29, citing market uncertainty, with most shares coming from existing holders. Holtec Nuclear and Bamboo Insurance also delayed listings.
- **SpaceX vs. carriers:** SpaceX's push into mobile (EchoStar spectrum, plus a reported spectrum deal on Oct. 8 per CNBC's tech page) keeps hitting AT&T, Verizon and T-Mobile shares. SpaceX says it will build out terrestrial Starlink mobile service. Terms of the Oct. 8 deal were not confirmed.
- **AMD explores a Korean foundry:** A report dated Oct. 8 says AMD is still exploring a foundry partnership with a Korean tech giant. TSMC remains its primary supplier, so this is talks only, not a deal.
- **OpenAI funding talks:** OpenAI is reportedly in early talks to raise about $30B at a ~$1.4T valuation, with ~$70B in annualized revenue. Nvidia and SoftBank recently funded their final $10B each from the March round.
- **Hexagon to buy Rocscience:** Hexagon agreed to acquire Rocscience (geotechnical engineering software) from TA, announced Oct. 8. Small, with limited US market impact.

**Take:** The IPO window is the story. Oura's pullback shows investors are wary (AI spending worries, high bond yields), yet Anthropic is pushing ahead with a possible mega-listing, which could pull cash away from other tech names. Watch AT&T, Verizon and T-Mobile for Starlink headlines, AMD and Samsung on foundry news, and Nvidia and SoftBank for OpenAI-linked moves. Note: few confirmed big-cap deals surfaced in the last 24 hours, so some items are developing reports rather than finished deals.
