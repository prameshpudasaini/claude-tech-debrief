# 2026-09-05

## Tech & Market-Moving Deals

- **AI cloud infrastructure firm Nscale is seeking up to $3.5 billion in pre-IPO financing**, including roughly $2 billion from Nvidia and $1.5 billion in convertible notes anchored by Daniel Loeb's Third Point, ahead of a planned U.S. listing that could raise another $3 billion. Nscale says it already has $103 billion in signed contracts (including a $45 billion Anthropic supply deal) and is targeting a valuation north of $30 billion — another sign Nvidia is bankrolling the data-center buildout it also sells into.
- **Blue Owl Capital is planning to roll up its data-center holdings into a new publicly traded REIT**, seeding it with about $6.5 billion of assets before taking it public, joining Blackstone and Brookfield in racing to give public investors direct exposure to AI infrastructure real estate.
- **Salesforce led a $166 million investment in Israeli HR-tech company HiBob, valuing it at $3.5 billion** — the latest in a string of strategic corporate bets by Salesforce into fast-growing enterprise software rather than building competing tools in-house.
- **Cloud security startup Upwind raised a $300 million Series C led by Bessemer and TCV, more than doubling its valuation to $3.8 billion in five months**, with Salesforce Ventures, Greylock, and Craft Ventures also participating — cybersecurity funding is staying hot even as broader VC activity concentrates in fewer, larger checks.
- **Autonomous-perception startup Lyte raised $165 million at a $1.6 billion valuation** to build sensing technology for self-driving and robotic systems, part of a broader wave of over $1.4 billion raised by Israeli and Israeli-founded startups in the first days of September alone.

## What It Means

Capital is flooding into AI infrastructure through every available channel — direct equity (Nvidia backing Nscale), public real estate vehicles (Blue Owl's REIT), and late-stage private rounds — which points to more IPO and listing activity in the coming weeks as firms race to convert private AI bets into public liquidity; watch NVDA for continued strategic-investment headlines and any new data-center REIT filings for a tradable proxy on the buildout. Salesforce's back-to-back strategic checks into HiBob and Upwind show large SaaS incumbents leaning on corporate VC to stay close to AI-native winners rather than building competitors from scratch, a pattern worth tracking for future M&A targets (CRM). On the macro side, Friday's hotter-than-expected August jobs report pushed Fed rate-hike odds higher and pulled the Dow, S&P 500, and Nasdaq lower to close last week — a reminder that even with deal flow this strong, rate expectations remain the near-term swing factor for high-multiple tech and AI names heading into next week.
