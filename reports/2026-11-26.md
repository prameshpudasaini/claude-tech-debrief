# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-11-26

- **Expedia is buying Kiwi.com, the Czech flight-booking site:** Expedia (EXPE) agreed on Nov. 25 to buy Kiwi.com from General Atlantic, which owns about two-thirds of it. Kiwi.com had €227M in revenue in 2025 and is known for combining flights from different airlines, including low-cost carriers like Ryanair and easyJet, into a single trip. The price was not disclosed, and the deal should close in 2027. It is Expedia's fourth recent purchase, after Tiqets, CarTrawler and Layla. ([Skift](https://skift.com/2026/11/25/expedia-to-buy-kiwi-com-extending-its-acquisition-spree-into-flights/), [MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/expedia-group-inc-entered-into-an-agreement-to-acquire-kiwi-com-s-r-o-from-general-atlantic-servi-ce7851d8da8ef420), [Travelextra](https://www.travelextra.ie/expedia-group-agrees-to-acquire-kiwi-com/))
- **Samsung Display is becoming ams OSRAM's largest shareholder:** It agreed to buy a 9.1% stake for about €210M (~$230M), along with a joint project to develop micro-LED displays. The deal should close in early 2027 if regulators approve. It is a small deal, but ams OSRAM is also adapting its micro-LED technology for the optical links that connect AI data-center hardware. ([Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/business/2026/11/25/samsung-display-becomes-top-shareholder-in-ams-osram-in), [Korea Herald](https://koreaherald.com/article/10949386), [OLED-Info](https://www.oled-info.com/samsung-display-makes-its-biggest-microled-move-yet-acquire-91-stake-ams-osram))
- **SpaceX (SPCX) won FCC approval for up to 1 million AI data-center satellites:** Half of the network must be working by 2038. The stock fell about 8% early in the day, as the cost of insuring SpaceX's debt hit a record and investors worried a Tesla merger would dilute their shares, then closed up 1.3% at $127.44. SpaceX also said it will give one new share to every active "Trump Account." ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/11/25/stock-market-today-nov-25-spacex-gets-historic-fcc-approval-for-1-million-satellites-is-this-its-next-growth-engine/), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/spacex-stock-pares-losses-following-fcc-approval-for-data-centers-in-space-183757039.html), [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/11/25/why-spacex-stock-is-down-today/), [24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/26/spacex-and-elon-musk-will-each-give-a-share-to-every-active-trump-account/))
- **ElevenLabs is reportedly seeking funding at a $33B valuation:** Bloomberg says the voice-AI startup, last valued at $22B, is in early talks and aims to close the round in Q1 2027. It is a private deal, but it shows investors are still paying more for AI app companies even as AI-chip stocks fall. ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/25/voice-ai-startup-elevenlabs-reportedly-seeking-new-funding-at-33b-valuation/))
- **SoftBank and ABB may split off ABB's China robotics business to save their deal:** Chinese regulators are worried that sensitive robotics data could flow to the US. The China spin-off is meant to rescue SoftBank's planned purchase of ABB's robotics unit. It is another case of US–China tension slowing tech deals. ([DIGITIMES](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20261123PD216/abb-business-data-acquisition-openai.html))
- **Tech stocks recovered most of a steep drop but still closed lower:** The Nasdaq ended down 0.62% after falling as much as 4.6% during the day, while the Dow rose 0.33%. NVDA fell 3.9%, MU 4.5%, SNDK 5.6% and AMZN 3.1%. The causes were a hot inflation reading, new tariff proposals and continued selling of AI stocks. US markets are closed today (Nov. 26) for Thanksgiving, and Friday is a half-day. ([Portfolio Terminal](https://portfolio-terminal.com/markets/stock-market-close), [Swingfolio](https://swingfolio.com/daily/us/2026-11-25/afternoon), [Stock Market Watch](https://stockmarketwatch.com/digest/2026-11-25))
- **No IPOs are scheduled this week:** Renaissance Capital lists no tech IPOs for the holiday week. The only new filing was a small one, Malaysia-based Algobizz for $20M. Anthropic's record $81B IPO (Nov. 18) remains the event that sets the tone for tech listings. ([Renaissance Capital](https://www.renaissancecapital.com/IPO-Center/News), [Stock Analysis](https://stockanalysis.com/ipos/news/))

**Synthesis:** It was a quiet holiday day for deals. The new deals were mid-sized purchases (Expedia–Kiwi, Samsung–ams OSRAM), not blockbusters. The theme is still the same: companies are paying up for AI, both in private funding (ElevenLabs at $33B) and in projects (SpaceX's satellite data centers), while public AI-chip stocks fall on worries about tariffs, inflation and AI spending. Watch for big swings in NVDA, MU and AMD on Friday's half-day, when thin trading can exaggerate moves. Also watch EXPE for its growing flight business and SPCX/TSLA for merger headlines. Deals involving China, like SoftBank–ABB, are now openly facing regulatory risk, so treat pending cross-border tech deals with caution.

*Note: Announcements were light because of Thanksgiving. Few outlets have reported the SpaceX FCC approval so far, so confirm it with the FCC before trading. Deals covered earlier in the week, including CrowdStrike–Zscaler ($44B, signed Nov. 23) and Amazon–Decart, are still pending.*
