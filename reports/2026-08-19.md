# 2026-08-19

## Tech & Market-Moving Deals

- **Marvell grants Google a warrant to buy up to $12.2B in shares as part of an expanded custom AI chip deal.** Google can purchase up to 58.97 million Marvell shares at $206.58 each, vesting in tranches tied to future custom-silicon revenue (AI inference accelerators, storage, networking and memory-interface controllers for the TPU ecosystem) through fiscal 2033. The stake would make Google one of Marvell's largest shareholders.
- **Broadcom shares fall ~5% as the Marvell-Google deal threatens its custom-chip franchise.** Broadcom has been Google's primary custom silicon partner for TPUs, and the market read Marvell's expanded scope as Google diversifying its supplier base — even though Broadcom retains a long-term agreement with Google through 2031. Marvell shares jumped 6-13% on the news.
- **Nvidia leans further into its role as AI infrastructure financier.** Following this week's dividend hike (to $0.25/share from a penny) and new $80B buyback, Nvidia's playbook — up to $105B in financing for OpenAI's Ohio data-center campus, a $1.5B stake in SB Energy, and a $500B+ third-party capital platform with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR — is drawing fresh scrutiny as its moat shifts from chip supply toward balance-sheet muscle.
- **Nvidia keeps writing checks into AI infrastructure startups.** The chipmaker participated in Together AI's $800M Series C (at an $8.3B valuation, led by Aramco Ventures) and Fireworks AI's $1.5B Series D (at a $17.5B valuation), continuing a pattern of taking equity stakes in the same AI-infra ecosystem that buys its GPUs.
- **Chip stocks broadly underperform even as the major indices grind higher.** The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index (SOX) plunged roughly 5.6% with all 30 components lower, while the Dow (+0.2%), S&P 500 (+0.4%) and Nasdaq (+0.3%) advanced on a Treasury buyback announcement and a pause on Canada tariffs — a split that shows AI-hardware names getting hit by supplier-diversification and rate jitters even as the broader tape rallies.
- **International chip names took an even harder hit.** Samsung Electronics closed down 7.8% and SK Hynix fell 9.75% as rising global bond yields pressured Korean tech shares alongside the domestic semiconductor selloff.

## What it means

The Marvell-Google warrant deal is the clearest signal yet that Google is deliberately building out a second (and third) custom-silicon supplier beyond Broadcom, and the stock market reacted instantly — Marvell up double digits, Broadcom down mid-single digits — making both names ones to watch for follow-through as more contract details emerge. Layered on top is Nvidia's continuing evolution from chip vendor to AI-infrastructure financier, funding data centers, startups and even its own customers' buildouts, which cuts both ways: it deepens Nvidia's ecosystem lock-in but keeps "circular financing" concerns alive for investors. Net effect for near-term trading: watch MRVL and AVGO for the custom-silicon share battle, NVDA and the SOX index for whether chip weakness persists despite a firm broader tape, and hyperscaler capex commentary (Google, Meta, Microsoft) for signs of how much appetite remains for multi-sourcing AI silicon.
