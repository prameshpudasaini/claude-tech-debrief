# 2026-08-20

## Tech & Market-Moving Deals

- **Marvell hands Google a warrant worth up to $12.2B in an expanded custom-AI-chip deal.** Google gets the right to buy 58.97M Marvell shares at $206.58 each — vesting in tranches tied to every $500M of chips it purchases through fiscal 2033, a program that could be worth roughly $120B in revenue to Marvell. The scope covers AI accelerators, storage and networking silicon around Google's TPU stack, and would make Google Marvell's fifth-largest shareholder.
- **Broadcom is the collateral damage.** AVGO fell about 5.4% Wednesday to ~$362 and is down roughly 14% on the week, as investors reprice a TPU franchise that was ~95% Broadcom-supplied. Analysts are pushing back that custom-AI demand still exceeds foundry capacity, so both suppliers can grow — but a credible second source at Google is a real structural change.
- **Stripe finalized its acquisition of OpenRouter for about $7.5B.** OpenRouter routes and meters token spend across 400+ models from 80+ providers; the price is a 5.4x markup on the $1.3B valuation it raised at in May. Roughly $1.5B goes to founders, $6B to investors including a16z, Sequoia, Nvidia and Google's venture arm.
- **Chinese humanoid-robot maker Unitree exploded on debut in Shanghai.** Shares rose as much as 629% off a 150.8 yuan IPO price after a $904M raise, closing at a ~342B yuan (~$48B) market cap. The retail tranche was 5,526x subscribed — a sentiment read-through for US-listed robotics and humanoid-exposed names.
- **LivePerson stockholders vote today on the SoundHound AI takeover.** The all-stock deal values LPSN at roughly $3.33/share (a 22% premium to its 30-day VWAP at the April announcement), and both ISS and Glass Lewis back it. A yes vote gives SoundHound a large enterprise conversational-AI installed base; SOUN and LPSN both trade on the outcome.
- **Veeda AI raised over $90M in seed funding, led by Khosla and Radical.** Founded weeks ago by Sanja Fidler, Nvidia's former VP of AI research, the Toronto company is building world models and simulation for robot training — one of the largest seed rounds ever for a Canadian startup and another data point on capital rotating from LLMs toward physical AI.
- **The AI-infrastructure IPO pipeline keeps filling.** Vantage Data Centers is exploring a potential $10B listing, DayOne has confidentially filed for a ~$5B offering, and Crusoe Cloud is reportedly in talks with banks — while OpenAI's own confidential S-1 (filed June 8, Goldman and Morgan Stanley leading) keeps a late-2026/2027 debut on the clock.

## What it means

Today's theme is second-sourcing: Google paying Marvell in equity to build a rival to its Broadcom TPU supply line is the clearest sign yet that hyperscalers want no single vendor holding their custom-silicon roadmap hostage. That's a positive re-rating for MRVL and a multi-year overhang for AVGO, and it rhymes with the Meta-Google TPU story pressuring Nvidia — the whole AI-chip supply chain is being deliberately diversified. Second, money is moving down the stack from models to the plumbing around them: Stripe paying 5.4x a three-month-old valuation for AI spend routing, and a $90M seed for robot world models, both say the premium is now on metering, orchestrating and embodying AI rather than training it. Watch MRVL and AVGO for follow-through, SOUN/LPSN on today's vote result, and robotics names for spillover from Unitree's debut.
