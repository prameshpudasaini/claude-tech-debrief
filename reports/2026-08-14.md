- **OpenAI and Anthropic cut API prices while DeepSeek raises them.** Divergent pricing moves among the top model providers signal a shift toward competing on inference cost/speed rather than just capability — a dynamic likely to pressure margins at smaller AI labs and benefit cost-efficient infrastructure providers.
- **Google ships a new, cheaper flagship AI model overnight.** Continues the rapid model-release cadence among Alphabet, OpenAI and Anthropic, keeping competitive and pricing pressure on the frontier-model race.

## What it means

Today's news is less about single mega-deals and more about the AI infrastructure arms race broadening: Apple's Alibaba tie-up shows even AI laggards are now doing strategic partnerships to catch up, while Anthropic's still-unclosed Decart deal and Big Tech's $1.5T+ spending pile keep compute and chip supply center stage. Watch Alibaba (BABA) and Apple (AAPL) on the China AI partnership, plus semiconductor names (SMIC-linked suppliers, Nvidia, AMD) as tightening chip capacity feeds into pricing. The broader signal for traders: capital commitments keep growing even as providers start competing more aggressively on price, meaning near-term volatility is likely to concentrate in infrastructure and compute-supply stocks rather than the model layer itself.
