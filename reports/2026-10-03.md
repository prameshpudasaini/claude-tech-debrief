# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-10-03

- **onsemi rewrites Synaptics deal:** After an unsolicited rival bid, onsemi (ON) amended its June agreement to buy Synaptics (SYNA) for $123/share in all cash, about $5.7B (down from roughly $7B in the original all-stock deal). ON rose about 6.5% pre-market; close is expected by mid-2027.
- **Oura IPO:** The smart-ring maker (OURA) was set to price on Nasdaq around Oct 1 in a ~$2.2B offering at a ~$14B valuation. About 73% of the shares come from existing holders, so demand and the first-day trade are a read on consumer-hardware appetite.
- **Anthropic IPO still the big overhang:** Reports point to a listing as early as October on Nasdaq at a roughly $965B valuation. It could pull money from, or lift, other AI names depending on how it trades.
- **Inworld buys Ultravox:** The AI company is combining real-time voice AI with model inference. Private deal, but another sign of consolidation in AI infrastructure.
- **Zhone acquires Tellabs Access:** The deal expands fiber access and Optical LAN products. Small and mostly a telecom-equipment niche story.
- **Meta's multi-vendor AI chip strategy (ongoing):** Its AMD (~$60B, 6 GW) and Nvidia supply deals, plus Google TPU leasing, keep shaping chip-demand expectations. This is not new today, but it frames AMD/NVDA trading.

**Synthesis:** Chip and AI-infrastructure consolidation continues, and the onsemi/Synaptics bidding contest shows buyers will pay up for analog and edge-AI assets. IPO supply is building (Oura now, Anthropic next), which can strain liquidity in richly valued AI stocks. Watch ON, SYNA (a rival bid could still emerge), OURA's debut, and AMD/NVDA around Anthropic IPO news. Note: few brand-new large deals surfaced in the past 24 hours, so some items are recent context.
