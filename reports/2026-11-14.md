# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-11-14

- **Samsung in talks to buy Modine (MOD), a data-center cooling maker:** Bloomberg reports early-stage talks, and a deal could come together within weeks. Modine jumped ~29% to about $7.3B in market value. It would be Samsung's largest acquisition ever. Neither company commented, and the talks could still fall apart. ([DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/samsung-in-talks-to-acquire-data-center-cooling-company-modine-manufacturing-report/), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/samsung-talks-buy-modine-data-172540580.html), [KED Global](https://www.kedglobal.com/mergers-acquisitions/newsView/ked202611130006))
- **Anthropic IPO is about 6x oversubscribed:** Bloomberg says orders are roughly six times the 825M shares on offer at $70–$78. Existing backers will get priority, and up to 20% of the deal is set aside for retail investors. Pricing is still expected Nov 18, with Nasdaq trading as "ANT" on Nov 19. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-12/anthropic-s-ipo-is-said-to-be-about-six-times-oversubscribed), [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/stocks/anthropic-ipo-six-times-oversubscribed-what-retail-investors-can-realistically-expect/), [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-ipo-oversubscribed-founders-sell-retail-europe))
- **Nvidia ends the week down 12.75% with its Anthropic pledge in question:** NVDA fell ~2% Friday after Jensen Huang's exit. Its ~$10B share of the $26B Anthropic placement is still unconfirmed under interim CEO Colette Kress, and earnings come Tuesday (Nov 17). ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-12/nvidia-s-10-billion-anthropic-ipo-pledge-in-focus-with-ceo-exit), [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-weekly-roundup-14-nov-2026-nvidia-fell-anthropic-ipo-book-6x/))
- **Big Tech's $26B Anthropic placement is drawing "circular money" criticism:** Alphabet, Nvidia, Microsoft, Broadcom, AMD, Micron and TSMC are all buying Anthropic shares at the IPO price. Critics note these are companies Anthropic already pays for compute, which adds headline risk for GOOGL, NVDA, MSFT and AVGO. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/google-nvidia-microsoft-literally-feeding-182134309.html), [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/11/11/nvidia-and-alphabet-just-agreed-to-buy-usd10-billion-each-of-anthropic-stock-at-its-ipo-price/))
- **Analog Devices closes its $1.35B Alif Semiconductor buy:** The all-cash deal, announced in September, closed Nov 13. It adds low-power edge-AI microcontrollers to ADI's industrial and consumer business. ([Evertiq](https://evertiq.com/news/2026-11-13-analog-devices-completes-acquisition-of-alif-semiconductor))
- **Cognizant completes its Wizeline acquisition:** CTSH adds digital product engineering and AI services, part of the IT-services push to sell AI implementation work. The deal is small, but it's another sign of the services sector consolidating around AI. ([Solutions Review](https://solutionsreview.com/ai-news-for-the-week-of-november-13-updates-from-cisco-coreweave-red-hat-more/))

**Synthesis:** Fresh deals were light again. The theme is spending on the "picks and shovels" of AI. Samsung's move on Modine shows that cooling and power for data centers are now acquisition targets, so watch MOD, VRT, nVent (NVT) and Trane (TT) for read-through. The AI-chip trade hinges on next week: Nvidia's earnings Tuesday and Anthropic's pricing Wednesday. Strong IPO demand supports AI sentiment, but the open question over Nvidia's $10B pledge could keep NVDA, AMD, AVGO, MU and TSM volatile. A reported $2.3B Intel–Classiq quantum deal and OpenAI IPO chatter are left out because their timing and status couldn't be confirmed.
