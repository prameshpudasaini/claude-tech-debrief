# 2026-10-08

- **GlobalFoundries (GFS) / TSMC:** GlobalFoundries signed a $2B, five-year deal to make silicon interposers (a key AI chip-packaging part) for TSMC at its Malta, New York plant; volume production starts in 1H 2028. GFS hit a one-month high on the news.
- **Alphabet / Constellation Energy (CEG):** Alphabet signed a $4.3B, 20-year deal for 890 MW of nuclear power; CEG jumped about 12%.
- **Vistra (VST):** The US Department of Energy committed $4.2B to upgrade Vistra's nuclear plants in Pennsylvania and Ohio; shares rose about 11%.
- **Uber / ezCater:** Uber agreed to buy corporate food-ordering platform ezCater for $2.3B.
- **TD SYNNEX (SNX) / BlueStar:** TD SYNNEX signed a definitive agreement to buy IT distributor BlueStar; terms not disclosed.
- **SAP / TechWolf:** SAP agreed to acquire AI work-intelligence startup TechWolf, expected to close in Q4 2026.
- **Oura IPO pulled:** The smart-ring maker cancelled its roughly $2.2B Nasdaq IPO a day before debut, citing IPO-market uncertainty. Anthropic's IPO is reportedly being lined up for November (Bloomberg), with no public S-1 yet; OpenAI reportedly waits until 2027.
- **AMD:** Reported plan to buy World Labs (3D/spatial AI) for $8.2B in stock, closing by end of 2026 (single-source, date unconfirmed). Separately, AMD rose about 3% after Lisa Su said chip output will rise significantly in 2027.

**Takeaway:** AI's bottlenecks are the story: packaging (GFS, TSMC) and power (CEG, VST, Alphabet) are drawing real money, so watch GFS, TSM, CEG and VST. The IPO window looks shaky, since Oura pulled its deal and rates are rising, which makes the Anthropic listing a key test for AI sentiment. Software and AI M&A (SAP, Uber, AMD) continues, but mostly at small to mid size, so impact is limited outside the names involved. Note: some of today's items come from thin or single sources, so verify before trading.
