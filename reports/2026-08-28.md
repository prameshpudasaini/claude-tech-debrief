# 2026-08-28

- **Nvidia agrees to buy Hugging Face for $12.9B.** Reports from The Information and CNBC say Nvidia has reached a deal to acquire the open-source AI model repository, a huge jump from Hugging Face's $4.5B valuation in 2023. The deal isn't formally signed yet and could still fall apart, but it would deepen Nvidia's grip on the AI software/model ecosystem, not just the chips underneath it.
- **Nvidia posts a blowout quarter and stuns Wall Street with guidance.** Revenue hit $96.2B (+106% YoY) and management guided to ~70% revenue growth for fiscal 2027, nearly double analyst expectations. The print triggered a broad AI-trade rally: Nasdaq +1.6%, S&P 500 +0.7%, with Salesforce (+22%), CrowdStrike (+20%), Okta (+28%), and Palo Alto Networks (+12%) all surging alongside it.
- **Amazon (AWS) to acquire DuckLabs**, the Amsterdam-based team behind the fast-growing open-source database DuckDB. Amazon says the core DuckDB open-source project stays independent under a nonprofit foundation, but the acquisition pulls the founding engineers into AWS to build out cloud analytics on S3 — a direct shot at Snowflake and Databricks.
- **Diodes Incorporated (NASDAQ: DIOD) completed its $250M acquisition of ElevATE Semiconductor**, a fabless analog/mixed-signal chipmaker focused on automated test equipment. Diodes says the deal is immediately accretive and adds roughly $50M of revenue in the first year.
- **Accenture (NYSE: ACN) agreed to acquire Comware**, a Tokyo-based technology services provider, adding 180+ consultants as Accenture continues rolling up regional IT services firms to bulk up its AI and cloud consulting footprint in Japan.
- **Crusoe reportedly in IPO talks with banks.** Axios reports the AI cloud/data-center infrastructure company is exploring a public listing, the latest sign that AI infrastructure players are looking to cash in while investor appetite for the theme stays hot.
- **Anthropic reportedly preparing an October IPO** that people familiar with the plans say could aim to match or exceed SpaceX's record-setting $1.77T listing — a signal of just how much capital is chasing frontier AI labs right now.

Nvidia is the story of the day on two fronts at once: a blowout earnings beat that reignited the AI trade across software and security stocks, and a reported $12.9B move to buy Hugging Face that would extend its reach from chips into the open-source model layer. Underneath that, hyperscalers keep buying data infrastructure talent outright (Amazon/DuckLabs) rather than just partnering, and the IPO pipeline for AI-adjacent companies (Crusoe, Anthropic) is thickening fast. Watch NVDA, AMZN, and software names with AI exposure (CRM, CRWD, OKTA, PANW) for continued momentum, and keep an eye on DIOD and ACN on their bolt-on deals — none of these are earth-shaking on their own, but they show consolidation accelerating across chips, IT services, and AI infrastructure at the same time.
