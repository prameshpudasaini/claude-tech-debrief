# Tech Deals Morning Brief — 2026-09-29

- **AMD to buy World Labs (~$8.2B, all stock):** AMD agreed to acquire Fei-Fei Li's AI research lab, and Li becomes AMD's EVP and chief scientist reporting to CEO Lisa Su. AMD rose ~1.3% to ~$616 on the news; the deal dilutes shareholders by roughly 0.8%.
- **BRC Group (RILY) to acquire Sangoma Technologies (SANG) for ~$204M:** $5.225 per share, a ~47% premium to the Sept 28 close. SANG jumped ~44% after hours; the deal is expected to close by early 2027.
- **Nscale files for NYSE IPO (ticker NSCL):** The Nvidia-backed AI cloud provider is reportedly targeting a valuation of up to ~$35B. It posted $140.6M in revenue and a $1.02B net loss in H1 2026, and closed a $3.36B pre-IPO convertible round (Third Point, Nvidia, Apollo, Citadel; ~$1B from Nvidia still to come). Filing news is from Sept 18; treat as a live IPO catalyst rather than a fresh announcement.
- **Nvidia–Hugging Face ($12.9B) and Nvidia–MediaTek (up to $3.5B convertible):** Earlier September/August deals still driving coverage of Nvidia's push to own more of the AI stack. Not new in the last 24 hours.
- **Small AI tuck-ins:** Mitratech bought BotDojo and Mark43 bought ForceMetrics (both private-startup deals announced Sept 28), with little direct market impact.

**Takeaway:** AI talent and infrastructure remain the main deal theme. AMD is using its high stock price to buy AI-model expertise and go after Nvidia in robotics and "spatial" AI. Watch AMD (dilution and execution), NVDA (competitive response and Nscale exposure), and the AI-cloud IPO pipeline (NSCL) for signs of investor appetite. Small-cap communications software (SANG) is seeing takeover premiums. Note: search coverage this morning was thin, so only the AMD and Sangoma deals are confirmed as fresh within 24 hours.
