# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-11-12

- **Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO:** Nvidia's board said Huang left as president, CEO and director, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO, and former Synopsys CEO Harvey Jones becomes the company's first board chair and will lead the search for a permanent CEO. Nvidia's release gave no reason, but press reports link the exit to personal scandals, including an alleged relationship with a suspected Chinese intelligence asset. NVDA fell more than 13% at the open (on pace for its worst day since Jan 2025), then cut the loss to about 7%. ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000082/nvidiaannouncesceotransiti.htm), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/nvidias-jensen-huang-resigns-chipmaker-names-cfo-colette-kress-as-interim-ceo/), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-thursday-november-12-sp-500-nasdaq-dow-nvidia-invest-110027430.html))
- **Nvidia's $10B Anthropic IPO pledge under scrutiny:** Nvidia committed ~$10B (Alphabet also ~$10B) to the $26B private placement that runs alongside Anthropic's IPO, and Bloomberg says the CEO exit puts that pledge in focus. No report so far says the commitment has changed. Anthropic's book is reported to be several times oversubscribed at $70–$78, with pricing expected Nov 18 and Nasdaq trading (ANT) Nov 19. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-12/nvidia-s-10-billion-anthropic-ipo-pledge-in-focus-with-ceo-exit), [CNBC live](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-jensen-huang-ceo-live-updates.html), [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/roadshow-launch))
- **Money rotates toward Nvidia's rivals:** AMD rose about 3% and Broadcom was flagged as a possible winner. Intel, Marvell and Micron each fell more than 2%, and AI stocks sold off worldwide. Analysts call the AMD/AVGO move a sentiment trade, not yet a change in market share. ([Investing.com](https://investing.com/news/stock-market-news/broadcom-amd-stock-outlook-after-huangs-exit-from-nvidia-93CH-5022420), [Reuters via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/global-ai-stocks-sink-as-nvidia-crisis-deepens-sector-selloff-5021776))
- **Siemens buys chip-debug specialist Lauterbach:** Announced Nov 10 with undisclosed terms (Lauterbach makes ~€60M in revenue a year). The deal adds TRACE32 embedded debug/trace tools to Siemens' EDA arm and targets closing in H1 2027. It's a small deal, but it raises the competitive stakes for Synopsys (SNPS) and Cadence (CDNS) in chip-verification software. ([Siemens](https://press.siemens.com/global/en/pressrelease/siemens-expand-portfolio-semiconductor-design-and-verification-planned-acquisition), [Quartz](https://qz.com/siemens-lauterbach-acquisition-chip-debugging-software-111026))
- **JD.com closes its CECONOMY takeover:** JD completed its public offer for the parent of MediaMarkt/Saturn, ending with 59.8% and consolidating the business from Nov 12. This gives JD (Nasdaq: JD) a large European electronics-retail footprint. ([Manila Times/GlobeNewswire](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/11/12/tmt-newswire/globenewswire/jdcom-completes-voluntary-public-takeover-offer-of-ceconomy/2468418))

**Synthesis:** The day's story is not a deal but a shock to the market's most important AI stock. Nvidia losing its founder-CEO adds governance and key-person risk, just as the AI trade was already under pressure. Watch NVDA into its Nov 17 earnings for any sign that Kress changes strategy, and watch Anthropic's Nov 18 pricing: a clean deal, with Nvidia's $10B commitment kept, would steady the group, while any wobble would deepen the sell-off. Short term, money is rotating toward AMD and Broadcom as the "other" AI chip names, while memory and networking names (MU, MRVL) trade down with Nvidia. Apart from that, new M&A was light. Serco's $292M purchase of QinetiQ's US defense-tech unit and small private AI deals (e.g., Worker.ai/DeltaGen) are left out because they have little US stock impact.
