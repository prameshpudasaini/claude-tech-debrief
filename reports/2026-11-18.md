# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-11-18

- **Nvidia beats and guides above consensus, but the rally fades:** Fiscal Q3 revenue was $115.8B (up 103% from a year ago) against about $109B expected, and Q4 guidance of $127.4–132.6B came in above the ~$125B consensus. Nvidia also returned $28.1B to shareholders. Yahoo Finance reports the board said it won't bring Jensen Huang back as CEO. NVDA rose more than 2% after hours and jumped premarket, then gave up the gain after Wednesday's open. ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000088/q3fy27pr.htm), [Yahoo Finance live](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/live/nvidia-earnings-live-nvidia-posts-q3-beat-board-says-it-will-not-reinstate-jensen-huang-as-ceo-221428433.html), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-18/us-stock-futures-rally-as-nvidia-results-ease-ai-trade-worries), [StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/articles/stock-market-today-live-november-18-2026))
- **Anthropic prices its IPO tonight and trades Thursday as "ANT":** The official range is still $70–$78 for 825M shares, valuing the company at up to about $1.4T. The WSJ reports pricing could top $80, and the order book is already covered. Up to 20% of shares go to retail investors. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/anthropic-ipo-date-price-range.html), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/anthropic-ipo-price-could-top-141901520.html), [Finwire](https://finwire.io/news/stock-markets-news/anthropic-set-to-price-ipo-tonight-reports-point-above-80))
- **AppLovin reportedly in talks to buy Criteo:** Reuters reports AppLovin is discussing a deal for the Paris-based ad-tech firm. Criteo has hired bankers to run a sale process, and Vista Equity and Quinti Capital are also interested. These are talks, not a signed deal, but CRTO is now a live takeover target and APP would add a web-ad business. ([TradingView/Reuters](https://www.tradingview.com/news/binance_news:4ead9f026094b:0-applovin-reportedly-in-talks-to-acquire-ad-tech-firm-criteo/), [StreetInsider/Reuters](https://www.streetinsider.com/General+News/Criteo+S.A.+%28CRTO%29+initiates+a+new+sale+process+-+Reuters/21174849.html))
- **Synaptics shareholders approve the onsemi merger:** 98.3% of votes cast backed the deal, which was signed on October 1. That clears a key hurdle toward closing, so the SYNA spread should narrow and ON moves closer to adding edge-AI and connectivity chips. ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/synaptics-stockholders-approve-on-semiconductor-merger-deal-93CH-5036481))
- **EasyPost to go public via SPAC:** The shipping-software company agreed to merge with American Drive Acquisition Co. (ADAC) at a $479M pre-money equity value, about $576M in enterprise value. It will list on Nasdaq as "EZPO" and expects about $211M of trust cash if no shareholders redeem. It's small, but it's another tech listing using the reopened IPO window. ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002083002/000110465926129352/tm2629912d1_ex99-1.htm), [TokenPost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/business/60432))
- **Transom Capital completes its SoundThinking take-private:** Holders get $8 per share in cash plus a CVR worth up to $3 more. SSTI stopped trading on Nasdaq today. It's one more small-cap software name leaving public markets for private equity, following GTCR/Elliott's CCC buyout. ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/18/3405653/0/en/transom-capital-completes-acquisition-of-soundthinking.html))

**Synthesis:** Nvidia's beat-and-raise ended the "AI demand is cracking" fear for now, but the faded rally shows investors still worry about valuations and Nvidia's leadership turmoil. The real test of AI appetite is tonight's Anthropic pricing. A print above $80 and a strong Thursday debut would likely lift NVDA, AMD and AVGO plus Anthropic stakeholders GOOGL and AMZN, and a weak debut would add to this month's chip sell-off. Outside AI, consolidation keeps going in ad tech (APP/CRTO) and chips (ON/SYNA), and private equity keeps taking out small-cap software. Watch CRTO for a bid, the ANT open Thursday, and whether NVDA can hold its post-earnings level.
