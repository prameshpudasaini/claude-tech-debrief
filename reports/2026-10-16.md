# 2026-10-16

- **Proofpoint / Varonis:** Thoma Bravo-backed Proofpoint agreed to buy Varonis (VRNS) for $49.75/share in cash (~$6.3B), a 52% premium to the pre-rumor price; Ontario Teachers' is co-investing and close is expected in 1H 2027. VRNS traded around $47 after the news, a normal merger-arb spread.
- **DA Davidson on VRNS:** The firm cut Varonis to Neutral since the stock is now pinned to the offer price, so upside is capped unless a rival bidder appears.
- **onsemi / Synaptics (ongoing):** onsemi's revised all-cash ~$5.7B ($123/share) bid for Synaptics (SYNA) still stands against a reported rival proposal; close is expected by mid-2027.
- **Microchip / Hailo (ongoing):** Microchip (MCHP) is buying AI-chip startup Hailo (terms undisclosed), adding edge-AI silicon.
- **Anthropic / OpenAI IPOs (ongoing):** Both have confidentially filed for IPOs. No public S-1 or pricing date yet; they remain the biggest pending catalysts for tech listings.
- **Workday / Silver Lake (reported, unconfirmed):** Silver Lake is reportedly exploring a $50B+ take-private of Workday (WDAY); talks were described as preliminary.

**Takeaway:** The one clear new deal in the last 24 hours is Proofpoint buying Varonis, another sign that private equity is taking out beaten-down software and security companies at big premiums. That keeps the spotlight on similar mid-cap names (cybersecurity, data software, WDAY) as possible targets, and on chips (SYNA, MCHP, ON) where consolidation continues. Watch VRNS for a rival bid and SYNA for a competing offer. Most other items are carry-overs, and I found no major new IPO pricing in US tech today.
