# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-11-13

- **Nvidia slides for a second day as analysts cut targets:** NVDA fell again on Friday, a day after Jensen Huang's resignation, briefly trading below a $4T market cap for the first time since July 2025. Barclays cut its target to $210 from $275, and Bernstein to $200 from $400. Retail traders bought the dip at a record pace, and the company reports earnings Tuesday (Nov 17) with no permanent CEO. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-nvda-stock-jensen-huang-transition.html), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-friday-november-13-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-nvidia-ppi-141451558.html))
- **Nvidia's ~$10B Anthropic pledge is still unconfirmed:** Nvidia hasn't said whether it will keep its ~$10B share of the $26B private placement alongside Anthropic's IPO. A Bloomberg Intelligence analyst says the new leadership could rethink its size or timing, but neither company has announced a change. ([Quartz](https://qz.com/nvidia-huang-resigns-anthropic-ipo-pledge-111226), [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/11/63229612/nvidia-ai-boom-jensen-huang-gary-marcus-colette-kress-funding-concerns))
- **Anthropic IPO still on schedule:** Anthropic is selling 825M shares at $70–$78 (raising ~$58–64B), with pricing expected Nov 18 and Nasdaq trading as "ANT" on Nov 19. Microsoft, Nvidia, Alphabet and Micron are named in the strategic placement. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-price-range-of-70-to-78-in-upcoming-ipo.html), [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/analyst-stock-ratings/analyst-color/26/11/63119292/anthropic-ipo-pricing-impresses-dan-ives-a-deal-of-this-size-gets-one-shot-at-its-debut))
- **TSMC buys into both leading AI labs:** An SEC filing earlier this week shows TSMC's board approved up to $500M in Anthropic shares at the IPO price and up to $500M in OpenAI preferred stock. TSM rose ~2% on the news. A supplier owning part of its biggest customers ties chip demand even more closely to the AI-lab funding cycle. ([The Star/Reuters](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/11/10/tsmc-to-invest-up-to-1-billion-in-anthropic-openai))
- **The rotation into Nvidia's rivals partly reverses:** AMD (+5.9%) and Broadcom (+5.5%) rallied Thursday on hopes that AI chip demand spreads beyond Nvidia. On Friday they gave part of that back: AMD fell ~3%, AVGO ~2% and Micron ~2%. Arista (ANET) was a standout, up ~5%. ([TradingKey](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/262303536-nvda-huang-resigns-shares-fall-amd-avgo-rise-tradingkey), [Invezz](https://invezz.com/news/2026/11/13/amd-broadcom-stocks-fall-2-as-nvidia-rebounds-is-wall-street-switching-sides/))
- **Marvell–Lumentum ($115B, all-stock) still pending:** No new regulatory or shareholder update. Nvidia put $2B into each company this year, and press reports say Huang was consulted before the deal. That makes the CEO change a small extra unknown for MRVL/LITE arbitrage traders. ([Light Reading](https://lightreading.com/ai-machine-learning/marvell-strikes-115b-deal-for-lumentum), [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-marvell-to-buy-nvidia-backed-lumentum-for-115b-in-ai-deal/))

**Synthesis:** New deal flow was very light in the last 24 hours. The market is still digesting Nvidia's CEO exit, not pricing new M&A. The key theme is how much the AI build-out depends on Nvidia's checkbook, from Anthropic's placement to its stakes in Marvell and Lumentum. With fresh deals scarce, the near-term trade rests on two events next week: Nvidia's earnings Nov 17 (watch for comments on its AI-lab investments) and Anthropic's pricing Nov 18. Expect NVDA, AMD, AVGO, MU and TSM to keep moving together and to swing hard on any news about the $10B pledge. Small private deals, such as the reported IBM–AuthMind buy, which couldn't be confirmed, are left out.
