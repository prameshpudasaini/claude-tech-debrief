# 2026-11-08

*Sunday: markets are closed and deal flow is light. Items below are the most recent significant moves from the past several days, with dates noted where they fall outside the last 24 hours.*

- **Anthropic IPO:** Its latest S-1/A (Nov 2) still has no price range or share count. Reports say the roadshow starts the week of Nov 9 and pricing could come the week of Nov 16. Valuation talk runs $1T–$1.3T, but none of it is filed, and prediction-market odds of a pre-December IPO have slipped.
- **Shield AI to buy Echodyne (Nov 5):** The defense-tech company will pay up to $5.3B for the radar maker, which is used in counter-drone systems. It is also raising $2.5B (a $1B Series H at a $28.8B valuation, $1B from Blackstone, $500M of JPMorgan-led debt). Close is expected in early 2027.
- **Fox completes Roku acquisition (Nov 6):** Roku holders get $95.30 in cash and stock per share (0.9799 Fox Class A shares plus cash). Roku is now out of the public market.
- **Nvidia and AMD invest in Meta (Oct 27):** Nvidia put $3B and AMD $1B into Meta's equity raise, which is now $97.5B. The deals come with bigger Meta orders for Nvidia's Vera Rubin and Vera CPUs and for AMD's AI compute.
- **Rezera (RZRA) and vVardis (VVVV) IPOs:** Rezera is expected to price near $200M at $11–13 and trade around Nov 12. Dental-products company vVardis (Apollo-backed) is looking to raise about $214M at $21–24 the week of Nov 9. Neither is a core tech name.
- **Cisco closes Astrix Security (~$400M):** Cisco is adding AI-agent and non-human-identity security to its portfolio. The dollar size is small for Cisco.

**Take:** Anthropic's price range and roadshow are the main event for next week, and they could set the tone for AI-linked names (Nvidia, AMD, Alphabet, Amazon, Microsoft, which are investors or suppliers). Big chip and cloud companies keep putting money into their own customers, a circular financing pattern that supports their stocks but raises concern if AI demand slows. Defense tech is drawing heavy private money (Shield AI, Echodyne); watch public drone and radar names for a sympathy move. Note: coverage for the past 24 hours was thin, so confirm the Anthropic timing on EDGAR before trading.
