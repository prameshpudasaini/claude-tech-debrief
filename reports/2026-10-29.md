# 2026-10-29

- **Meta equity raise with chip suppliers:** Meta is raising roughly $95B in equity (about $35B in underwritten offerings, up to $60B via an at-the-market program). Nvidia committed $3B and AMD $1B, turning two top suppliers into shareholders and expanding Vera Rubin and Helios deployments; Broadcom was also reported as interested.
- **Marvell–Lumentum (reported, unconfirmed):** Marvell is reportedly in advanced talks to buy optical component maker Lumentum. No definitive agreement has been announced, but Lumentum shares jumped on the report.
- **Anthropic IPO filing:** Anthropic's IPO paperwork is reported to have moved forward this week, with terms undisclosed and a raise of up to ~$75B estimated. Sources disagree on the filing's status, so treat timing as unsettled. Optical stocks rallied on the news (Lumentum +8.2%, Coherent +5.8%, Ciena +4.9%).
- **Nscale IPO pricing:** Nvidia-backed AI infrastructure firm Nscale is reportedly targeting a $30B valuation for its IPO despite investor pushback toward $16–19B.
- **Guidewire buys Hyperexponential:** Guidewire (GWRE) is acquiring AI underwriting platform Hyperexponential for up to $2.1B, extending its AI push in insurance software.
- **CoStar buys Vexcel:** CoStar (CSGP) agreed to buy aerial imaging firm Vexcel for $730M in cash, announced with earnings; the stock has been choppy since.
- **Amazon Leo–Globalstar cleared:** The FCC approved Amazon Leo's $11.5B purchase of Globalstar spectrum, ending the regulatory review.
- **TPG–Ark Data Centres (reported):** TPG is reportedly in talks to buy Ark Data Centres for about $5B, another sign of capital chasing AI data center capacity.

**Takeaway:** AI infrastructure is still where the money is going. Suppliers like Nvidia and AMD are now taking equity stakes in their biggest customers, and optical names (Lumentum, Coherent, Ciena, Marvell) are in play on M&A chatter and Anthropic IPO momentum. Watch NVDA, AMD, META, AVGO and the optical group for volatility, and note that a huge Meta share sale plus mega-IPOs (Anthropic, Nscale) could soak up investor cash. Several items above are reports, not signed deals, so expect headline swings.
