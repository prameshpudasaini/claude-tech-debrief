# 2026-10-27

- **Nvidia → Meta ($3B):** Nvidia will invest $3 billion in Meta's new equity offering and expand the Muse AI partnership, with Meta rolling out more Vera Rubin systems and Vera CPUs.
- **AMD → Meta ($1B):** AMD is putting in $1 billion; its Helios racks already run at Meta and EPYC chips power Muse. Broadcom was also named, and Meta said its top chip suppliers signaled interest in up to $5B total (non-binding).
- **Meta's mega-raise:** Meta plans $35B in underwritten stock offerings, then up to $60B more via an at-the-market program (~$95–97.5B total) to fund AI infrastructure. Expect dilution and supply-chain-financing headlines.
- **Anthropic IPO:** Reports say Anthropic's S-1 went public Oct. 26, with a possible raise of ~$75B+. Sources conflict (it first filed confidentially in June), and no price range or date is confirmed.
- **OpenAI → Orchestra Bio:** OpenAI is reportedly buying Orchestra Bio, a startup building AI agents for drug development. Single-source and unconfirmed; the stock impact is indirect.
- **Stagwell → Freestar:** Stagwell (STGW) bought publisher ad-monetization firm Freestar to feed its "AI Media Machine." Small deal, terms undisclosed.
- **SpaceX chip financing (earlier report):** SpaceX is reportedly seeking ~$40B in debt to buy Nvidia GPUs. Not confirmed, early stage, and from Oct. 6–7, but it is still circulating.

**Takeaway:** The theme is AI buildout financed circularly: chip makers (Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom) are investing in their own biggest customers, who spend the money back on chips. That supports NVDA, AMD and AVGO demand, but raises concentration risk and means Meta share supply (dilution) is worth watching. The Anthropic S-1 could pull investor money toward AI IPOs and set valuation marks for peers, so also watch MSFT, GOOGL and AMZN as holders and partners. Several items here are single-source, so verify before trading.
