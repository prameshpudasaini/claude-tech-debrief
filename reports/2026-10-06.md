# 2026-10-06

> Note: only a few new deals could be verified from the past 24 hours, so this brief has fewer than the usual 5–8 bullets. Older items are labeled as context, not padded as news.

- **Schneider Electric / PTC (confirmed):** Schneider Electric signed a definitive deal to buy PTC for $205/share in cash (~$22.6B equity, ~$23.7B enterprise value), and PTC stock reportedly jumped ~35%. Close is expected by Q3 2027, pending PTC shareholder and regulatory approvals; confirmed via PTC's SEC 8-K filings.
- **C.H. Robinson / RXO (reported):** C.H. Robinson (CHRW) agreed to buy truck-brokerage firm RXO in a ~$5.8B stock-and-cash deal. This is logistics rather than pure tech, so the market impact is mainly on freight and logistics stocks.
- **Zeta Global / Senso (small):** Zeta Global (ZETA) plans to acquire Senso to add AI engineering and frontier-model connectivity. Terms were not found and the impact on the stock is likely small.
- **Anthropic IPO (upcoming):** Anthropic has picked Nasdaq for a listing that may value it near $2T, which could be the largest IPO ever. Reports conflict on timing: one calendar says Oct. 23, while Reuters-sourced reports say marketing starts mid-October and pricing lands just before the November midterms.
- **Context, not new today:** AMD agreed in July to sell Anthropic tens of billions in MI450 servers and invest up to $5B. onsemi's ~$7B Synaptics deal and Qualcomm's Modular purchase also remain in play.

## Synthesis

The one confirmed big deal, Schneider–PTC, shows industrial and "physical AI" software still getting bought at rich premiums. Watch PTC (likely to trade near the $205 offer) and peers such as Autodesk, Dassault, Bentley and Siemens for sympathy moves. The bigger market event is Anthropic's coming IPO. It could pull cash from other tech names, and it makes AMD (investor and supplier) and Nvidia worth watching as the date firms up.
