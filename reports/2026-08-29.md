# 2026-08-29

- **Nvidia's $12.9B Hugging Face deal is still the overhang heading into next week.** The agreement to buy the open-source AI model repository isn't formally signed yet, but reporting from CNBC and The Information suggests it's close — a move that would push Nvidia deeper into the software/model layer, not just chips, and has analysts split on whether it's good for the broader open-source ecosystem.
- **Nvidia's blowout Q2 print keeps rippling through the tape.** $96.2B in revenue (+106% YoY) and guidance for ~70% growth in fiscal 2027 sparked the tech sector's best single-day showing since March 2001 (XLK +3.2%), lifting AI-adjacent software and security names (Salesforce, CrowdStrike, Okta, Palo Alto Networks) alongside chip stocks — a rally that's likely to carry into Monday's session.
- **Amazon/AWS is acquiring DuckLabs**, the Amsterdam team behind the open-source analytics database DuckDB, expected to close in early September. The core DuckDB project stays independent and open-source, but AWS gains the founding engineers to build out S3-based analytics — a direct challenge to Snowflake and Databricks.
- **Databricks closed a $5B raise at a $190B valuation** (after investors initially pushed for $15B) and has also been rolling up smaller acquisitions, including Electric, maker of the lightweight Postgres engine PGlite — reinforcing Databricks as a serious counterweight to Snowflake in the AI-data infrastructure land grab.
- **Anthropic's IPO buildup keeps accelerating.** Investors and bankers are now circling a possible $2 trillion valuation for an October Nasdaq listing, which would eclipse SpaceX as the largest IPO ever; Anthropic's own annualized revenue run-rate has reportedly surpassed $65B, up more than 7x since the end of 2025.
- **TSMC and Amkor's 10-year Arizona packaging partnership continues to pay off for both stocks**, with Amkor up sharply since the deal was announced and TSMC holding gains as the tie-up expands U.S.-based advanced chip packaging capacity — a name to watch as reshoring-driven semiconductor deals keep landing.
- **OpenAI's CFO downplayed an imminent IPO** but didn't rule out a Q4 listing, adding OpenAI to the same fall watch-list as Anthropic, Crusoe Cloud, and General Atlantic as AI-infrastructure and model companies line up to tap public markets.

It's a quiet Saturday for new dealmaking, but Friday's momentum is what matters for Monday's open: Nvidia is dominating the tape from two directions — a record quarter that reignited the entire AI trade, and a reported $13B move into the open-source model ecosystem via Hugging Face. Layered on top, hyperscalers (Amazon, Databricks) are buying data-infrastructure teams outright rather than just partnering, and the AI IPO pipeline (Anthropic, OpenAI, Crusoe) is thickening into what could be a historic fall listing season. Watch NVDA, AMZN, and AI-exposed software names (CRM, CRWD, OKTA, PANW) for continued momentum, plus AMKR/TSM on the semiconductor-reshoring theme — none of Saturday's headlines are new shocks, but they confirm consolidation is accelerating across chips, cloud data, and AI infrastructure all at once.
