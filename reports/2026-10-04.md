# 2026-10-04

- **ON Semiconductor / Synaptics:** ON switched its Synaptics (SYNA) takeover from a ~$7B stock swap to a ~$5.7B all-cash deal at $123 per share, after a rival proposal surfaced. SYNA jumped about 14% and ON about 5%; closing is expected by mid-2027.
- **Anthropic IPO:** Reports say the public prospectus is due in early-to-mid October, with the roadshow in mid-October and the Nasdaq listing now targeted for November so Q3 results can be included. Talk of a ~$2T valuation and up to $100B raised is unconfirmed.
- **Cerebras (CBRS):** The stock has slipped below its IPO price as insiders filed to sell and a report claimed OpenAI's newest fast model runs on Nvidia GPUs, putting Cerebras' ~$20B anchor contract in question. Altman has tried to calm the worry.
- **Oura IPO:** The smart-ring maker postponed its planned pricing, citing "market uncertainty."
- **OpenAI IPO:** OpenAI, which reportedly filed confidentially in June, is now said to be looking at early 2027.
- **Dynatrace / Arize:** Dynatrace (DT) completed its acquisition of AI observability startup Arize, adding agent monitoring to its software platform.

**Takeaway:** It is a quiet weekend for new deals, so the focus is on what is coming. The Anthropic prospectus and roadshow in the next two weeks are the big event, and they will test how much appetite there is for huge AI listings, while Oura's delay and Cerebras' slide show the IPO window is choppy. Watch chip M&A names (ON, SYNA), Cerebras versus Nvidia for any sign that OpenAI is moving workloads, and AI-linked IPO names around the Anthropic filing. Note that this scan found few fresh deals in the past 24 hours, so some items are ongoing stories rather than brand-new announcements.
