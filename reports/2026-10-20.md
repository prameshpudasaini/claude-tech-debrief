# 2026-10-20

- **Nvidia + Germany:** Chancellor Merz and Jensen Huang were set to unveil an industrial-AI partnership in Berlin on Oct 19. Reports say it is backed by a three-digit-million-euro federal contribution, mostly for compute on Deutsche Telekom's industrial AI cloud, with Siemens, Audi and Black Forest Labs participating. Final terms were not confirmed in sources.
- **Proofpoint to buy Varonis:** Thoma Bravo-owned Proofpoint agreed (Oct 15) to acquire Varonis (VRNS) for about $6.3B in cash, $49.75 per share, a 52% premium to the pre-rumor price. Close is expected in H1 2027.
- **IonQ + Elisity:** IonQ agreed (Oct 12) to an all-stock deal worth up to $1.3B for cybersecurity firm Elisity, pushing the quantum player into security software.
- **Anthropic IPO watch:** Anthropic filed a confidential draft S-1 in June at a roughly $965B valuation. A Renaissance Capital note from Oct 16 says a public filing could come as soon as this week, though that is unconfirmed.
- **Jio Platforms IPO (India):** Reliance-backed Jio Platforms opens its ~₹30,213 crore IPO on Oct 21, with listing expected Oct 28. It is a large global tech offering that may draw EM and telecom flows.
- **Nebius buys Inferize:** Nebius (NBIS) acquired AI inference-optimization startup Inferize to strengthen its Token Factory inference stack.
- **Dynatrace closes Arize:** Dynatrace (DT) completed its acquisition of AI observability firm Arize.

**Takeaway:** Cybersecurity and AI-infrastructure software are where the deals are. Private equity is paying large premiums for security names, which could lift Varonis and peers (CrowdStrike, Palo Alto, Zscaler, Rubrik). Nvidia's Germany tie-up shows sovereign AI spending is still growing, which helps NVDA and European industrials like Siemens. The big swing factor is a public Anthropic S-1, which could shift flows among AI-linked stocks. Note that the US IPO calendar is empty this week and many items above are a few days old, so the past 24 hours were quiet.
