# 2026-11-07

*Note: little brand-new deal flow surfaced in the last 24 hours, so this brief includes the most market-relevant deals from the past week, each dated. Dates and terms come from press reports; I did not check every filing.*

- **Anthropic IPO (ticker ANT, Nasdaq):** The amended S-1 filed Nov 2 still has no price range. Reports put the roadshow in the week of Nov 9, earliest pricing around Nov 13 and first trade the week of Nov 16, though none of this is confirmed.
- **Marvell to buy Lumentum (announced Nov 2):** About $115B all-stock deal, 4.5 Marvell shares per Lumentum share, to add AI optical interconnect to Marvell's chips. Close is expected in 1H 2028 and needs US and Chinese approval. Analysts flag antitrust and customer-conflict risk.
- **Meta buys Conductor (reported Nov 4):** Meta acquired the AI coding startup that runs several coding agents in parallel, with terms undisclosed. The team reportedly joins Superintelligence Labs, and Conductor's cloud service is being wound down.
- **Nvidia and AMD invest in Meta (Oct 27):** Nvidia is putting in $3B and AMD $1B of a Meta equity raise that funds its AI buildout. It ties Meta's chip purchases (Vera Rubin, Helios racks) to both suppliers.
- **Five9 to buy Level AI ($325M cash) and CDW to buy Lovelytics ($525M), early Nov:** Small AI add-ons for contact-center software and IT services. Both come from single-source reports, so treat them as unverified.
- **Fox / Roku (watch, not closed):** One headline said Fox had completed its $22B Roku purchase, but other sources show the June deal still pending, with close expected in 1H 2027. Treat the "completed" claim as unconfirmed.

**Takeaway:** Money is flowing to AI infrastructure. Chip makers are buying stakes in their own customers (Meta), and Marvell is paying up for optical links that connect AI data centers. Watch MRVL and LITE for deal-spread moves, NVDA, AMD and META on the circular-financing story, and the optical group (Broadcom, Coherent) as a read-through. The biggest near-term catalyst is the Anthropic IPO, which could pull money from other AI names and set the tone for later AI listings. Expect volatility around the pricing date.
