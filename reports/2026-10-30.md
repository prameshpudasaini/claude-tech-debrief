# 2026-10-30

- **DoorDash to buy Instacart:** DoorDash (DASH) signed a deal to acquire Instacart (CART) at $65/share, about $17B in equity value, at least 75% cash and up to 25% DoorDash stock. Instacart's board approved it; no closing timeline or regulatory clearance has been reported.
- **Thrive Holdings takes ADT private (reported):** Thrive Holdings agreed to buy ADT in an all-cash deal valued at about $7.6B, and ADT shares jumped to a 5-year high.
- **Marvell–Lumentum talks continue (unconfirmed):** Marvell (MRVL) is reportedly in advanced talks to buy optical maker Lumentum (LITE), with a deal possibly announced as soon as next week. Marvell fell sharply on the report while Lumentum rallied; Lumentum reports earnings Nov 5.
- **Alphabet slides after earnings:** Alphabet (GOOGL) sank on a Q3 miss (EPS $2.12 vs $3.03 expected, negative free cash flow), pulling the Dow, S&P 500 and Nasdaq lower and reviving AI-spending worries. Management said search query growth was the fastest in five years.
- **Deepgram buys Daily:** Voice-AI firm Deepgram is acquiring Daily, maker of the open-source Pipecat framework, and setting up an independent Pipecat Foundation. Price undisclosed; private companies, so limited direct stock impact.
- **OPay IPO prices:** Nigerian digital bank OPay priced its US IPO at $19 (low end of range), raising $304M, with NYSE trading expected Oct 30. Fintech rather than pure tech, and the low-end pricing hints at a cautious IPO market.
- **Still in the background:** Nvidia ($3B) and AMD ($1B) are buying Meta shares in its roughly $95B equity raise, and the Anthropic IPO filing remains unconfirmed.

**Takeaway:** Big-ticket consolidation is heating up (DoorDash–Instacart, Marvell–Lumentum), but Alphabet's miss and OPay's low-end pricing show investors are jittery about AI spending and new issues. Watch DASH and CART for deal-spread and antitrust headlines, MRVL and LITE for a formal announcement, and GOOGL, META and the chip suppliers for AI-capex sentiment. Several items are reports, not signed deals, so expect headline swings.
