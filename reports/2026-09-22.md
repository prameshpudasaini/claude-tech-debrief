# 2026-09-22

## Tech & Market-Moving Deals

- **AMD's blockbuster AI chip pact with OpenAI pushed AMD's market cap past $1 trillion for the first time**, with shares up as much as 37% around the news that OpenAI will deploy up to 6 gigawatts of AMD GPUs over several hardware generations, while AMD hands OpenAI warrants for up to ~160 million shares (roughly a 10% stake) tied to milestones.
- **Chipmakers rallied broadly Monday on easing US-China trade tensions ahead of Thursday's Trump-Xi summit** — Intel jumped ~12%, Qualcomm gained ~9%, Arm rose ~17%, and Meta gained over 11%, as investors bet on reduced export-control risk heading into a state visit where AI is expected to be a central topic (with Sam Altman and Jensen Huang reportedly attending).
- **SoftBank launched an $11+ billion bond offering** (its largest-ever non-financial Asia-Pacific corporate bond deal) to fund the next $10 billion tranche of its OpenAI investment, part of a $30 billion staged commitment that will bring SoftBank's cumulative OpenAI stake to about 13% ($64.6 billion invested).
- **Paramount Skydance cleared the last major hurdle for its $110.9 billion acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery**, reaching an out-of-court settlement that imposes a five-year consent decree (content investment commitments, distributor restrictions, an independent editorial oversight board for CBS News/CNN); the companies expect the deal to close in about two weeks.
- **Alibaba unveiled its new Zhenwu V900 AI chip and a plan to expand Alibaba Cloud's global data-center capacity to more than 20 gigawatts by 2032**, tripling the performance of its prior chip generation; Alibaba shares rose about 3% in Hong Kong on the announcement.
- **Qualcomm kicked off its Snapdragon Summit (Sept 22–24) with dual flagship Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 / Gen 6 Pro chips** built on a 2nm process with on-device AI agent support up to 30 billion parameters — positioning Qualcomm's mobile silicon more directly in the "agentic AI" race alongside its recent $60 billion AWS custom-silicon warrant deal.

## What It Means

The dominant theme is that AI infrastructure commitments are now directly repricing entire sectors in a single session: the AMD-OpenAI compute deal alone added roughly $1 trillion in market value context and dragged the whole chip complex (Intel, Qualcomm, Arm) higher, while easing US-China trade rhetoric ahead of the Trump-Xi summit amplified the move — watch AMD, INTC, QCOM, and ARM into Thursday's summit for further volatility on any tariff or export-control headlines. SoftBank's giant bond raise and Alibaba's aggressive chip/data-center buildout show the AI capex race is now a global, multi-bank-financed arms race rather than a US-only story, which should keep pressure on power, memory, and networking suppliers tied to hyperscale build-outs. Meanwhile Paramount-Warner Bros. Discovery clearing its last regulatory hurdle removes a multi-month overhang for both stocks and signals regulators are willing to approve big media consolidation with governance conditions attached rather than blocking outright — a precedent worth watching for the next large tech/media tie-up.
