# 2026-10-07

- **Schneider Electric to buy PTC (PTC)** for $205/share in cash (~$22.6B equity value, a ~42% premium). PTC jumped ~33% to $192.26, still below the offer price; close expected by Q3 2027 pending shareholder and regulatory approval.
- **ON Semiconductor revised its Synaptics bid** to an all-cash $123/share (~$5.7B), replacing a ~$7B stock deal. ON rose ~8% and Synaptics ~14% on the news.
- **C.H. Robinson to acquire RXO** for $17.25 cash plus 0.0856 CHRW shares per RXO share (~$5.8B). RXO jumped ~22%; close expected in H1 2027 with $300M of targeted synergies.
- **Qualcomm (QCOM) rallied after Apple renewed its patent licensing agreement**, removing a long-running overhang on Qualcomm's licensing revenue.
- **Constellation Energy (CEG) signed a 20-year nuclear power deal to supply Google's AI buildout**, sending the stock up ~15%.
- **SAP agreed to acquire Belgian AI work-intelligence startup TechWolf**, expected to close in Q4 2026; a small deal, but another sign of enterprise-software incumbents buying AI capability.
- **Anthropic's IPO is reported to be slipping to mid-October**, with a Nasdaq listing targeted before the November midterms at a valuation reported as high as ~$2T. This is not yet priced; timing is based on press reports.

**Takeaway:** Cash buyouts are the theme: Schneider/PTC, ON/Synaptics and CHRW/RXO all show buyers paying real premiums, so watch for merger-arb spreads and for other mid-cap industrial-software and analog/edge-chip names that could become targets. AI's power demand is the other thread (Constellation/Google), which supports the nuclear and utilities trade. Anthropic's pending IPO is the big event risk: a record-size listing could pull money from existing AI names like Nvidia and Microsoft in the short term. The S&P 500 closed at a record on Oct 6, so the mood is risk-on.

Note: Nvidia's $500B AI-financing partnership with Apollo, BlackRock, KKR and others dates from August, so it is not counted as a new deal. Qualcomm/Apple and Constellation/Google dates come from search snippets and were not independently confirmed.
