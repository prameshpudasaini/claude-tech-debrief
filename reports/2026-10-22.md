# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-10-22

- **EQT to buy Qiagen (~$10.8B):** The Swedish private equity firm agreed to acquire the diagnostics company for about €9.6B at €46.50 per share, announced Oct 21. Tech-adjacent (life-science tools), but a big take-private that could lift diagnostics and tools peers.
- **CBRE to buy Onnec Group:** CBRE agreed to pay £460M upfront (plus up to £300M in earn-outs) for the UK data-center and network infrastructure services firm. It shows real-estate services players chasing AI data-center buildouts.
- **Anthropic IPO still the big one to watch:** Anthropic has reportedly filed confidentially and picked Nasdaq, with a roughly $1.8–2T valuation floated and a raise of $60–100B. No public S-1, price range or confirmed date yet, so the Oct 23 date on some calendars is unverified.
- **Jio Platforms IPO in India is live:** Reliance's roughly ₹30,213 crore offering opened Oct 21 (price band ₹1,065–1,119) and is reported to close Oct 23. Meta and Google are existing investors.
- **Nvidia pushes its partner ecosystem at GTC Berlin:** Nvidia pointed to nearly $10B of European investment and tie-ups with Google Cloud, Dell and HPE. Separately, SemiAnalysis reports Nvidia turned away neoclouds that added AMD or Google TPU chips; unconfirmed by Nvidia.
- **Small deals:** APEX Tech's SPAC merger with Korai Flix ($3.5B, Q1 2027 close) and a muted US IPO tape, with Gravitics cutting its deal size and a Nasdaq listing withdrawn.

**Take:** No new mega-deal from the big US tech names in the past 24 hours; the story is the AI IPO window. Anthropic's listing, if it comes, would be a major test of appetite for AI valuations and could pull money from other AI names (NVDA, GOOGL, AMZN as backers). Watch NVDA, DELL and HPE for the Nvidia ecosystem push, and data-center infrastructure names after the CBRE deal. Salesforce–Braze (from Oct 21) still frames software M&A.
