# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-11-16

- **Blackstone's new data-center REIT buys into $5B of AWS campuses:** Blackstone Digital Infrastructure Trust (BXDC) agreed to buy a 75.1% controlling stake in two AWS joint ventures valued at about $5B. The ventures hold 13 new data centers in northern Indiana and Mississippi. Amazon keeps 24.9% and leases everything back for at least 12 years. It's a classic sale-leaseback: Amazon frees up capital for AI capex, and BXDC gets its first big deal right after its IPO. Expected to close in Q4. ([MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/blackstone-reit-to-buy-majority-stake-in-5-billion-amazon-data-center-portfolio-ce785edfd88af02c), [WebProNews](https://www.webpronews.com/blackstone-bxdc-aws-data-centers-5-billion-leaseback), [Finimize](https://finimize.com/content/blackstones-new-data-center-reit-starts-with-a-5-billion-aws-deal))
- **AMD closes its ~$8.2B all-stock purchase of World Labs:** Fei-Fei Li's "world model" startup works on 3D environments and robot learning. Li joins AMD as EVP and chief scientist. It's AMD's second-largest deal ever, after Xilinx, and a push from selling chips into owning AI models. ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/16/3403615/0/en/amd-completes-acquisition-of-world-labs.html), [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/amd-world-labs-8-2bn-fei-fei-li-chief-scientist))
- **Anthropic IPO week begins:** Pricing is expected Wednesday (Nov 18), with Nasdaq trading as "ANT" on Thursday. Bloomberg says the share sale plus the $26B private placement could raise up to about $90.4B. The book is reported to be about 6x oversubscribed at the $70–$78 range, which implies a valuation of about $1.26–1.41T. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/anthropic-ipo-date-price-range.html), [Macau Business](https://macaubusiness.com/tech-shares-slide-ahead-of-nvidia-results-anthropic-ipo/), [Morningstar](https://www.morningstar.com/stocks/anthropic-we-see-stock-undervalued-ipo-pricing-range))
- **Nvidia slides on reports that Jensen Huang may try to return as CEO:** The FT reports that Huang has privately discussed coming back, and an open letter from tech executives backs his return. No plan is formal. NVDA fell about 2% intraday. The Nasdaq was down roughly 1.5–2% at points, with AVGO and AMD also weaker. Earnings are Tuesday (Nov 17). ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/nvidia-stock-price-jensen-huang.html), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/zacharyfolk/2026/11/16/nvidia-shares-slip-in-premarket-as-jensen-huang-reportedly-weighs-ceo-comeback/), [24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/16/stock-market-today-nasdaq-100-sinks-1-as-nvidia-faces-its-first-earnings-without-jensen-huang/))
- **SAP completes its acquisition of TechWolf:** TechWolf's AI work-intelligence platform becomes the core of SuccessFactors. It's a small deal, but it adds to SAP's bolt-on AI buying in HR software, an area where it competes with WDAY. ([SAP News](https://news.sap.com/2026/11/sap-completes-acquisition-of-techwolf))
- **Marvell–Lumentum spread widens to about 18% (deal update):** MRVL has fallen about 38% since talks emerged on Oct 28. That leaves Lumentum trading well below its implied deal value, so the market doubts the $115B all-stock deal will close on its 2028 timeline. Marvell added three directors last week ahead of the approval fight. ([GuruFocus](https://gurufocus.com/news/9198147/marvell-stocks-fall-66-as-lumentum-deal-spread-widens-to-18), [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/marvell-buying-lumentum-waiting-list-new-directors))

**Synthesis:** Monday's new deals were about physical AI infrastructure. Amazon is selling data centers to Blackstone and leasing them back, a sign that hyperscalers want to free up cash for spending without slowing the build-out. That's bullish for data-center REITs and suppliers (BXDC, DLR, EQIX, VRT). AMD closing World Labs shows chipmakers moving into AI models and robotics. The near-term market, though, depends on two events. The first is Nvidia's earnings Tuesday, now with added CEO uncertainty. The second is Anthropic's pricing Wednesday and its debut Thursday. A clean Nvidia beat plus a strong ANT debut could spark a sharp rebound in NVDA, AMD, AVGO, MU and TSM, and lift GOOGL and AMZN on their Anthropic stakes. A miss or a messy leadership answer would likely extend the AI-chip sell-off. Keep an eye on MRVL/LITE as a gauge of confidence in big AI M&A, and on MOD for any signed Samsung deal (no update today).
