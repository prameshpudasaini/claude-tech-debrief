# 2026-08-11

- **Intel launches its first stock sale since 1971, upsized to $20B.** Intel priced a $15 billion common stock offering (with a $2.25B greenshoe pushing net proceeds to ~$19.7B) at $95/share on August 10 to fund AI-driven capex, foundry expansion, and its 14A manufacturing process — including new chip deals tied to Tesla. The stock dipped on the dilution news despite the bullish AI framing.
- **Teledyne to acquire Varex Imaging for $1.1B.** Teledyne Technologies agreed to buy the X-ray/CT-detector maker for $18.90/share in cash, an 88% premium; Varex shares soared nearly 50% on the news. Deal expected to close in early 2027, pending regulatory and shareholder approval.
- **AMD bought AI chip startup Taalas this week.** AMD acquired Toronto-based Taalas, which builds chips that hardwire trained AI models directly into silicon to cut inference costs — another move in AMD's push to challenge Nvidia in the inference market.
- **OpenAI's IPO is closing in.** OpenAI's public S-1 prospectus is expected on EDGAR within days, targeting a September listing that could value the ChatGPT maker above $1 trillion — potentially the largest tech IPO ever, and the first full look at its financials (still unprofitable despite ~$2B/month in revenue).
- **A Delaware judge forced Verisk Analytics to proceed with its $2.35B AccuLynx deal**, rejecting the insurance-data company's attempt to back out of the acquisition — removing a key overhang for the stock.
- **SoundHound AI's acquisition of LivePerson nears the finish line.** Proxy advisor Glass Lewis and LivePerson's board are urging shareholders to approve the all-stock-and-cash deal (~$3.33/LivePerson share) ahead of the August 20 special vote; SOUN options market is already pricing in volatility around the outcome.

Today's tape is dominated by the AI capex arms race spilling into the equity markets: Intel tapping public investors directly for AI/foundry funding is a notable shift from debt-and-subsidy financing, and AMD's Taalas buy shows the chip fight moving from raw GPU power to inference efficiency. Watch INTC and AMD for continued volatility around AI capex headlines, SOUN/LPSN into the August 20 merger vote, and OpenAI-adjacent names (MSFT, ORCL, NVDA) for any S-1 leaks as the IPO timeline firms up — a filing this month would be one of the biggest market catalysts of the year. Broader tone: dealmaking is still brisk in mid-cap tech/industrial tech (Teledyne-Varex), suggesting M&A appetite remains healthy even as mega-cap AI stocks wobble on valuation jitters.
