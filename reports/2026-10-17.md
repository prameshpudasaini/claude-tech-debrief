# 2026-10-17

- **IonQ / Elisity:** IonQ (IONQ) agreed to buy zero-trust security firm Elisity in an all-stock deal worth $1.15B at close, plus up to $150M more on revenue milestones (up to ~$1.3B). Close is expected in Q4 2026; IONQ fell as much as 5% on dilution worries and doubts about fitting security software into a quantum business.
- **Amazon / Decart (reported, unconfirmed):** The WSJ reports Amazon (AMZN) is in talks to buy Israeli AI startup Decart for about $7B, up from a ~$4B valuation in May. Anthropic reportedly walked from a ~$6B bid in September; nothing is signed.
- **GTCR + Elliott / CCC Intelligent Solutions:** The two investors agreed to take insurance-software provider CCC (CCCS) private at $7.75/share (~$6.1B), a 44% premium. Another PE take-out of a beaten-down software name.
- **Intuit / Sequence:** Intuit (INTU) is reportedly buying fintech startup Sequence for roughly $200M, a small tuck-in for its finance tools.
- **Cloudflare / Deno:** Cloudflare (NET) is acquiring Deno to strengthen its Workers developer platform (announced Oct 10; terms not disclosed), a modest add to its edge-computing push.
- **Intel Foundry (analyst claim):** KeyBanc says Intel (INTC) has won foundry contracts from Broadcom, Google, Apple, AMD, Microsoft and Nvidia. This is an analyst note, not a company announcement, so treat it with caution.
- **Anthropic IPO (unconfirmed timing):** One calendar lists Anthropic for Oct 23, but there is no price range or public S-1, and other reports point to November. Reuters says investors are weighing a lower valuation. This week's US IPOs were all biotech, with no tech pricings.

**Takeaway:** Software and security keep getting bought up, either by private equity at big premiums (CCC, and Varonis earlier this week) or by strategic buyers adding AI and security features (Amazon, IonQ, Cloudflare). Watch CCCS for merger-arb trading, IONQ for pressure from share issuance, and AMZN on whether the Decart deal is confirmed. Also keep an eye on INTC if the foundry wins are confirmed, and on any Anthropic S-1 filing, which would be the biggest tech listing catalyst.
