# 2026-08-12

## Tech & Market-Moving Deals (last 24 hours)

- **Intel closes its upsized $20B stock offering today.** Priced at $95/share (210.5M shares), the deal was upsized from $15B amid heavy demand, with proceeds earmarked for capex as Intel leans further into its AI-chip and foundry turnaround — a major dilution event for INTC holders but a signal the market is willing to fund Intel's AI bet.
- **Anthropic signs a 20-year, $9.1B compute deal with Riot Platforms.** Riot will supply roughly 191 megawatts of capacity from its Rockdale, Texas facility, the latest sign that Bitcoin miners are repurposing power infrastructure for AI workloads — bullish for RIOT and the broader crypto-miner-to-AI-datacenter trade.
- **Archer Aviation to acquire Boeing's Wisk Aero, Insitu and SkyGrid units for a near-20% equity stake.** The deal bundles Boeing's autonomous-flight and eVTOL assets into Archer to build an end-to-end "physical AI" aerospace platform; ACHR shares jumped about 10% on the news.
- **Teledyne Technologies to buy Varex Imaging for $1.1B in cash.** The $18.90/share offer (an ~88% premium) adds X-ray sources and digital-detector technology to Teledyne's imaging portfolio; VREX shares spiked nearly 50%.
- **Nielsen to acquire ad-verification firm DoubleVerify for $2.15B.** DV shareholders get $13.60/share (a 30% premium) as Nielsen combines traditional audience measurement with DoubleVerify's ad-fraud and brand-safety tech, aiming for a combined $4B+ revenue business — notable for DV holders and the ad-tech sector broadly.
- **Nvidia and partners push the Stargate AI buildout toward its $500B goal.** Oracle and SoftBank added fresh commitments for five new US data centers, building on Nvidia's up-to-$100B staged investment in OpenAI (tied to at least 10 gigawatts of Nvidia systems) — keeps NVDA, ORCL and the AI-infrastructure trade in focus.
- **Chinese copper-foil maker Londian Wason (NYSE: FOIL) prices its US IPO.** Priced at $22/ADS for a $94.3M raise (~$1.7B valuation), the company is the world's largest LiB copper-foil supplier by volume, serving CATL, LG Energy Solution, Samsung SDI and BYD — a read on investor appetite for EV-battery supply-chain listings.

## What it means

The dominant theme is still AI infrastructure financing: Intel is tapping equity markets to fund its buildout, Anthropic is locking up power capacity from a crypto miner, and Nvidia/Oracle/SoftBank keep inflating the Stargate commitment toward half a trillion dollars — all signs that capex funding, not just chip design, is becoming the bottleneck to watch. Elsewhere, decent-premium cash M&A (Teledyne/Varex, Nielsen/DoubleVerify) shows strategic buyers are still willing to pay up for niche, profitable tech assets even as broader deal volume stays selective. Watch INTC for offering-related pressure today, RIOT and other miners for follow-through on the AI-power pivot, and ACHR/VREX for continued momentum after their pop; FOIL's debut is worth watching as a proxy for demand in China-linked EV supply-chain listings.
