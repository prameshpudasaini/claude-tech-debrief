# 2026-10-25

*Coverage note: today is a Sunday and web search surfaced few deals dated strictly within the past 24 hours. Items carry their best-known dates; reported talks are labeled as such.*

- **Salesforce (CRM) to buy Braze (BRZE) for ~$5.66B cash ($47.50/share), announced Oct 20:** It is the largest recent US software deal, and closing is expected in fiscal 2028. Watch the spread between Braze's price and the offer.
- **Onsemi (ON) to buy Synaptics (SYNA) in a ~$7B all-stock deal for physical AI and edge chips (date unconfirmed):** Onsemi reportedly fell about 9% and Synaptics rose more than 11% after hours. Closing is expected around mid-2027.
- **Visa (V) reportedly in talks to buy crypto-custody firm Fireblocks for about $2.5B:** This is a report of talks, not an announced deal, and I could not confirm it independently. It would extend Visa's push into stablecoin and crypto payments.
- **CBRE (CBRE) to buy data-centre infrastructure firm Onnec for an initial £460M (~$610M), up to £300M more in earn-outs (Oct 20):** It is a small deal for CBRE, but it shows buyers paying up for AI data-centre buildout assets.
- **SpaceX is reportedly in early talks to raise ~$40B of debt to buy Nvidia (NVDA) chips (FT, about two weeks ago):** This is still a report, not a closed deal. It points to huge, debt-funded AI chip demand.
- **Microsoft (MSFT) said it will lean more on its own Maia chips for AI inference (CNBC, Oct 12):** It still partners with Nvidia and AMD for training. This is a slow-moving risk for Nvidia's share of hyperscaler spending.
- **Amazon (AMZN) is reportedly buying billions of Nvidia GB300 racks that it cannot yet power on (Business Insider, Oct 20):** This is a purchase, not a partnership, but it shows power and data-center readiness are now the bottleneck.

**Takeaway:** Buyers keep paying for AI-ready software, edge chips, and data-center infrastructure, and acquirers' stocks often dip while targets jump. The real constraint on AI spending is shifting from chip supply to power and financing, which matters for NVDA, AMD, and the data-center builders. Watch CRM/BRZE and ON/SYNA for deal spreads, and watch for news on the Visa/Fireblocks talks and on the OpenAI and Anthropic funding and listing plans from last week's reports. Expect a quiet weekend, with Monday's open as the first test of these stories.
