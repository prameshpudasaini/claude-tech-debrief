# 2026-09-25

## Tech & Market-Moving Deals

- **Akamai signed a seven-year, $11.6 billion cloud-services agreement with Anthropic**, with an option for up to $9 billion more (roughly $20 billion total) — the deal centers on CPU-based compute rather than GPUs, Akamai issued Anthropic a warrant for up to ~5% of its shares, and AKAM stock surged over 20% premarket, with CoreWeave and Cloudflare also ticking up on the read-through.
- **PayPal shares jumped about 4% after reports surfaced that an unnamed West Coast tech company is evaluating an all-stock takeover bid**, reviving M&A speculation around the fintech just weeks after Stripe and Advent International's $53 billion offer collapsed in August.
- **Microsoft and OpenAI signed a new agreement restructuring and extending their long-term partnership**, formalizing the "next chapter" of the relationship around continued Azure infrastructure access and governance terms — a key overhang for MSFT and for OpenAI's path toward an eventual IPO.
- **Qualcomm and Amazon struck a custom AI chip partnership tied to warrants worth up to $60 billion**, with Qualcomm building next-generation ASICs for AWS's AI inference data centers — a direct shot at Nvidia's dominance in AI silicon and a new growth line for QCOM.
- **AMD's agreement to sell Meta up to $60 billion of AI chips over five years also gives Meta the right to acquire as much as a 10% stake in AMD**, tightening the two companies' ties as Meta diversifies its chip supply away from Nvidia.
- **Anthropic's IPO timeline is reportedly shifting toward mid-October, with backers pointing to a valuation target as high as $2 trillion** — a listing at that size would eclipse SpaceX's $1.77 trillion IPO as the largest in history and is a major catalyst to watch for AI-sector sentiment.
- **Samsung Electronics led Mistral AI's Series D funding round and announced a strategic partnership** to embed Mistral's models across Samsung's semiconductor engineering and manufacturing operations, deepening Samsung's bet on AI-driven chip design.
- **Broadcom and TSMC are reportedly separately weighing deals that would split up Intel**, with Broadcom eyeing Intel's chip design and marketing business and TSMC studying a stake in Intel's fabs — a potential breakup of one of the industry's most storied chipmakers that the Trump administration is watching closely.

## What It Means

The standout mover today is Akamai, which jumped over 20% on its record $11.6–20 billion Anthropic deal — a reminder that "AI infrastructure" trades now extend well beyond GPU makers into CPU cloud, networking, and edge providers (watch AKAM, and the read-through to CoreWeave and Cloudflare). PayPal's fresh takeover chatter (PYPL) shows fintech consolidation speculation hasn't gone away even after the Stripe deal fell apart. The bigger structural story is chip supply chains realigning around AI: Qualcomm-Amazon and AMD-Meta are both multibillion-dollar deals where the buyer gets an equity stake in the chipmaker, a pattern that ties hyperscaler capex directly to semiconductor stock performance (QCOM, AMD, AMZN, META). Layer on Anthropic's possible record-breaking IPO and Broadcom/TSMC's interest in carving up Intel, and the picture for the next few weeks is clear: AI capital keeps flowing into compute, chips, and cloud infrastructure, with INTC, AMD, QCOM, AKAM, and the eventual Anthropic listing among the names most likely to see outsized volatility.
