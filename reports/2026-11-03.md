# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-11-03

- **Marvell (MRVL) / Lumentum (LITE) follow-through:** The roughly $115B all-stock deal announced Monday (Nov 2) is still the biggest tech deal in play. Lumentum fell about 10% and Marvell also dropped, so the market is not cheering the price paid.
- **Anthropic IPO (ticker ANT, Nasdaq):** Its public S-1 was filed Oct 26, and an amendment was reportedly filed Nov 2. Price range and share count are still blank in coverage I found (not verified on EDGAR). Reports point to a roadshow no earlier than about Nov 10 and a listing in the second half of November, with valuation talk of $1.5–2T (unconfirmed).
- **Oracle (ORCL) $10B common stock sale:** Oracle is selling new stock alongside bonds to fund AI build-out. It is a follow-on offering, not an IPO, but it adds share supply and dilution.
- **SpaceX / EchoStar (SATS) report:** SpaceX shares reportedly rose on reports of talks to acquire EchoStar's Boost Mobile service or secure access to its network. These are unconfirmed talks, not a signed deal. SpaceX already agreed to buy EchoStar spectrum for about $17B in 2025.
- **Still in focus: Nvidia (NVDA) and AMD invest in Meta (META):** Nvidia put $3B and AMD $1B into Meta's roughly $97.5B equity raise (announced Oct 27). Older news, but it shapes AI-chip demand expectations.

**Takeaway:** Capital is flowing into AI: companies are issuing stock (Meta, Oracle), consolidating AI networking (Marvell/Lumentum), and lining up the Anthropic IPO. The Anthropic listing could pull money from other big tech names, so watch Nasdaq mega-caps and AI-linked stocks like NVDA, AMD, MRVL, LITE and ORCL for supply and dilution pressure. Midterm elections and a weak Nasdaq are delaying other IPOs, so few new listings are expected this week. Coverage was thin this morning and no major new big-tech acquisition surfaced in the past 24 hours.
