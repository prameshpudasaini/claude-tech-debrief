# 2026-08-27

## Tech & Market-Moving Deals

- **Nvidia has agreed to buy Hugging Face for roughly $12.9 billion**, confirming reports that first surfaced this week. The deal hands Nvidia control of the leading open-source AI model and dataset repository — the "GitHub of AI" — pushing its reach well beyond chips into the software/distribution layer that thousands of AI developers rely on. Hugging Face's revenue is only ~$150M/year, underscoring how much strategic premium Nvidia is paying.
- **Nvidia posted blockbuster fiscal Q2 results Wednesday**: revenue of $96.2B (+106% YoY), with Data Center revenue surging 117% to $89B and guidance pointing to ~$108B next quarter. Shares dipped slightly into the print but reversed to gain roughly 4% in after-hours trading — a strong signal that AI infrastructure demand isn't slowing.
- **Anthropic is moving toward what could be the largest IPO in history.** Following its confidential S-1 filing in June, a public filing is expected as soon as late August, with a Nasdaq listing targeted for October. Investors are reportedly pricing the offering around a $2 trillion valuation — north of $60B raised — with Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan and Morgan Stanley leading.
- **Nvidia separately struck a $7B combined deal with AI coding startup Poolside** — $6B to non-exclusively license Poolside's "Model Factory" software (and hire ~109 of its engineers) plus a $1B equity investment at a roughly $12–13B valuation, deepening Nvidia's push into proprietary model-building tools.
- **AMD is acquiring Toronto-based AI inference chip startup Taalas**, adding technology that etches model weights directly into silicon to cut compute and memory overhead. The deal, expected to close in Q4 2026, is AMD's latest move to chip away at Nvidia's inference dominance.
- **Marvell's stock jumped ~10% after Google expanded its custom-silicon partnership** tied to Google's TPU program, structured as a warrant letting Google buy up to $12.2B of Marvell shares — a vote of confidence in Marvell's role in Google's AI chip supply chain.
- **Waymo disclosed an expanding bench of compute partners** — AMD, Micron, Nvidia, Samsung, SanDisk, Socionext and TSMC — as it scales the custom silicon behind its robotaxi fleet, another data point in how broadly AI infrastructure spend is spreading across the chip sector.

## What it means

The dominant theme remains AI infrastructure consolidation: Nvidia is spending aggressively to own not just chips but the software and model-distribution layers around them (Hugging Face, Poolside), while its own Q2 print shows the underlying demand still justifies that spending. Meanwhile capital keeps flowing to challengers and suppliers — AMD via the Taalas acquisition, Marvell via its expanded Google tie-up — signaling the AI buildout is broadening beyond a single winner-take-all chip story. Watch NVDA for continued momentum off earnings and the Hugging Face close, AMD and MRVL as AI-diversification plays, and keep Anthropic's IPO timeline on the radar — a $2T listing this fall would be a major test of whether public markets will underwrite private AI valuations at their current scale.
