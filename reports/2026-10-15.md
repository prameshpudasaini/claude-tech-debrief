# 2026-10-15

- **onsemi / Synaptics:** onsemi (ON) revised its Synaptics (SYNA) deal to an all-cash ~$5.7B ($123/share), down from a ~$7B all-stock plan, after an unsolicited rival proposal surfaced; SYNA jumped ~14% and ON ~5–7%. Announced Oct 1, still expected to close by mid-2027.
- **Microchip / Hailo:** Microchip Technology (MCHP) agreed to buy AI-chip startup Hailo (reported late September, terms undisclosed), extending the push into edge-AI silicon.
- **Anthropic IPO:** Anthropic filed a confidential draft S-1 on June 1 and has been reported as preparing a public filing; timing and size remain unconfirmed. A listing would be a major test of the market after SpaceX's June Nasdaq debut.
- **OpenAI IPO:** OpenAI has confidentially filed for an IPO but says timing is undecided, with reports pointing to late 2026 or 2027.
- **Bumble / Ithaca Holdings (ongoing):** Bumble (BMBL) is reportedly in advanced take-private talks at roughly $1B+; neither side has confirmed.
- **Workday / Silver Lake (ongoing):** Silver Lake is reportedly exploring a $50B+ take-private of Workday (WDAY); talks were described as preliminary.
- **Nvidia AI financing (ongoing):** Nvidia is reportedly working with Goldman Sachs and Apollo on data-center financing and may backstop up to 25% of qualifying deals.

**Takeaway:** It was a quiet day for brand-new deals; my searches found no major tech announcement dated in the last 24 hours, so several items above are carry-overs. The themes are consolidation in chips (ON/Synaptics, Microchip/Hailo, including a bidding contest) and take-privates of beaten-down software and consumer-app names. Watch SYNA for a possible rival bid, BMBL and WDAY for deal headlines, NVDA for AI-financing exposure, and any Anthropic public S-1 as the next big IPO catalyst.
