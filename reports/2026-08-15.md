# 2026-08-15

## Tech & Market-Moving Deals

- **Nasdaq agrees to acquire LeveL Markets.** Nasdaq (NDAQ) signed a definitive agreement to buy LeveL Markets, the third-largest U.S. alternative trading system by volume, as part of its "always-on markets" push to modernize equity-trading infrastructure and extend into round-the-clock/tokenized trading. LeveL will operate as a standalone unit under a new Nasdaq division; terms weren't disclosed.
- **Sony and TSMC form an image-sensor joint venture.** Sony Semiconductor Solutions and TSMC signed a binding agreement to build "Advanced Vision Semiconductor Manufacturing Corp," a JV in Kumamoto, Japan, to mass-produce next-gen smartphone image sensors starting in 2029 — deepening the Sony-TSMC manufacturing alliance and tying two major suppliers closer together.
- **SanDisk stuns Wall Street with its 2026 Investor Day outlook.** SanDisk (SNDK) shares surged roughly 12-15% (four-session gain near 26%) after laying out a long-term growth model projecting mid-to-high double-digit revenue growth through fiscal 2030, driven by AI-fueled memory demand. JPMorgan upgraded the stock to Overweight, and the news lifted Micron, SK Hynix, Lam Research and Applied Materials in sympathy.
- **Reddit is joining the S&P 500.** S&P Dow Jones Indices confirmed Reddit (RDDT) will enter the index before the open on August 18, replacing AvalonBay (being acquired by Equity Residential). Shares jumped over 10% on the news — Reddit becomes only the second pure-play social media company in the index, forcing index-fund buying ahead of the effective date.
- **River AI raises $1.1B just two months after founding.** The AI startup from xAI co-founder Igor Babuschkin closed a seed/Series A round led by General Catalyst and AMP PBC, with Nvidia, AMD Ventures, Y Combinator and Temasek participating — one of the largest and fastest mega-rounds of the year, underscoring how much capital is still chasing top AI talent pre-revenue.
- **Teledyne to buy Varex Imaging for $1.1B.** Teledyne Technologies (TDY) agreed to acquire the imaging-components maker, adding X-ray detector and imaging technology to its industrial/medical-imaging portfolio.
- **Anthropic's $6B Decart talks remain unresolved.** Anthropic is still negotiating to acquire Israeli AI-infrastructure startup Decart (real-time video/world models, GPU-efficiency tech) at roughly 50% above its ~$4B May valuation. It would be Anthropic's largest acquisition yet, aimed at cutting inference costs ahead of its targeted October IPO — but talks are early-stage and could still fall apart.

## What it means

With U.S. markets closed for the weekend, the freshest signal is how capital keeps reallocating around the AI buildout rather than a single new headline deal: Nasdaq and Sony/TSMC are both hardening infrastructure (trading rails and chip supply) that AI-era markets depend on, while SanDisk's blowout guidance shows memory/storage names are now being re-rated as AI infrastructure plays alongside Micron and SK Hynix. Watch SNDK, MU, and NDAQ when trading resumes, plus RDDT into its August 18 S&P 500 entry (index funds will need to buy). The bigger swing factor for the week ahead is Anthropic's Decart decision — a close would confirm AI labs are still willing to pay premium prices for compute-efficiency IP right before hitting public markets, while a collapse would suggest some pump-the-brakes caution creeping into AI M&A.
