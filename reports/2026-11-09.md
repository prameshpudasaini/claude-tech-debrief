# 2026-11-09

- **AMD + Google Cloud:** AMD said its 5th Gen EPYC CPUs will be the host processors for Google Cloud's new TPU 8tx, Google's first cloud TPU system with hardware-enforced confidential computing. AMD shares rose about 8.5% in morning trading, helped by an Oppenheimer upgrade to Outperform.
- **Anthropic IPO (ticker ANT, Nasdaq):** The S-1 was filed Oct 26 with the price range still blank. The roadshow could launch as early as Nov 10, with pricing possibly the week of Nov 16 and a listing before Thanksgiving. Reports say investors are pushing the valuation to about $1.5–1.6T (down from ~$2T) for a raise of up to $80–100B. Reuters reports some investors doubt the November timing.
- **vVardis (VVVV) IPO:** The Apollo-backed Swiss dental-tech company is expected to price this week at $21–24 per share, raising about $214M. This is a small deal, but it is a read on IPO appetite after the recent tech selloff.
- **Analog Devices closes Alif Semiconductor (Nov 6):** ADI completed its purchase of Alif, adding AI-capable microcontrollers for edge devices to its portfolio.
- **Fox closes Roku acquisition (Nov 6):** Roku holders receive $95.30 in cash and Fox Class A stock per share, ending Roku as a standalone public streaming-platform stock.
- **Morgan Stanley to buy Vinyl Equity:** The bank is buying the digital transfer-agent and shareholder-recordkeeping platform to expand its digital-asset infrastructure.
- **Nvidia supply-chain financing (unconfirmed):** A weekly roundup says Nvidia has started financing "billions of dollars" of factories and equipment to ease AI-chip production bottlenecks. Terms and date are not confirmed.

**Takeaway:** A quiet day for new M&A. The big story is the AI IPO pipeline, led by Anthropic. Its pricing and timing could pull money from other AI and mega-cap tech stocks and set the tone for later listings. Watch AMD, which is gaining ground in AI infrastructure alongside Google's TPUs, and Nvidia for any confirmation of the financing reports. Also watch ADI and the edge-AI chip group. Several of these items come from secondary sources and some deals closed before today, so confirm in filings (EDGAR) before trading.
