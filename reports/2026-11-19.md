# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-11-19

- **Anthropic prices the largest IPO on record:** The Claude maker priced at $83 a share, above its $70–$78 range, and sold 980M shares instead of the planned 825M. That raised $81.3B (more than SpaceX) at about a $1.5T fully diluted valuation, with orders reported near 7x the shares on offer. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-ipo-ai-offering-price.html), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-reportedly-prices-ipo-at-83-a-share-above-projected-range/), [Yahoo Finance live](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/live/anthropic-ipo-live-company-said-to-price-stock-at-83-begin-trading-thursday-212656452.html))
- **ANT debuts on Nasdaq today:** Shares open through a Nasdaq auction, not at the 9:30 bell. Early auction indications were around $104–$105, about 25% above the IPO price, but no first trade had been confirmed when this was written. ([CNN live](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/19/business/live-news/anthropic-ipo), [StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/articles/anthropic-ipo-live-ant-first-trading-day-2026))
- **Big Tech's $26B Anthropic stake purchase closes after the IPO:** Nvidia and Google are each buying $10B of shares at the IPO price. AMD ($2.5B), Broadcom, Micron and Microsoft (about $1B each) and TSMC (about $0.5B) are also in. This was disclosed in the November 10 amended S-1, so it isn't new, but the higher price means fewer shares per dollar, and a strong debut gives all seven an immediate mark-to-market gain. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/nvidia-nvda-planned-10-billion-152453901.html), [Finimize](https://finimize.com/content/anthropics-ipo-comes-with-a-26-billion-side-deal))
- **Palo Alto Networks buys Applied Compute for $4.5B in stock and raises its outlook:** Applied Compute helps enterprises train and run their own open-weight AI models securely. The deal should close in Palo Alto's fiscal Q3 2027. The acquisition and a raised FY27 revenue forecast sent PANW up about 9–10% premarket. ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001327567/000132756726000030/ex992pressrelease_november.htm), [Reuters via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/palo-altos-raised-sales-forecast-powers-cybersecurity-stock-rally-5038632))
- **Cybersecurity stocks rise with Palo Alto:** CrowdStrike, Zscaler and Fortinet were up 5–7% premarket, and Okta, Cloudflare, F5 and SentinelOne gained 2–6%. These are premarket moves, not closing prices. ([Investing.com](https://za.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/premarket-movers-palo-alto-soars-chip-stocks-rally-ahead-of-anthropic-debut-4586190))
- **Maximus closes its $660M Agile Defense deal:** Agile Defense provides cyber, data and AI services to defense and intelligence agencies, and the deal includes up to $50M in earn-outs. Maximus (MMS) says the business is already in its FY27 guidance, which it released today alongside fourth-quarter results. ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001032220/000103222026000048/mms-2026x09x30x8kxex991.htm), [GovConWire](https://govconwire.com/articles/maximus-agile-defense-acquisition-660m))
- **Nvidia says its $12.9B Hugging Face deal is still on:** Interim CEO Colette Kress said the strategy hasn't changed since Jensen Huang's exit. It's a status update, not a new deal, but it removes one source of worry for NVDA holders. Treat it cautiously, since only secondary sources reported it. ([BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/c8912e69-d4f7-4a77-9c7a-5b2e55945c06))

**Synthesis:** Anthropic's record IPO is the main event today. Pricing above range and raising the share count means investors still want AI despite this month's chip sell-off. That lifted Nasdaq futures about 2% and helped Anthropic's backers and suppliers (GOOGL, AMZN, NVDA, AMD, AVGO, MU) before the bell. Watch where ANT opens and whether it holds its first-day gain, because that will set the tone for AI stocks into year-end. A fade back toward $83 would be a warning sign. Separately, Palo Alto's deal shows established software companies buying AI startups to stay relevant, and cybersecurity (PANW, CRWD, ZS, FTNT) is today's strongest group outside AI. Keep in mind that premarket gains have faded at the open in recent sessions.
