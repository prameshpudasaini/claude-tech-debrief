# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-11-23

- **CrowdStrike agreed to buy Zscaler for $43.9B in stock:** Zscaler holders get 1.135 CRWD shares for each ZS share. That works out to about $260 per share, a 34% premium to Friday's close. Both boards approved the deal unanimously, and closing depends on regulators. Before the open, ZS rose about 13–20% and CRWD fell about 6–9%. ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001535527/000110465926130785/tm2630454d1_ex99-1.htm), [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/m-a/26/11/63524549/crowdstrike-strikes-43-9-billion-zscaler-deal-in-cybersecurity-shake-up), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/crowdstrike-acquire-zscaler-43-9b-114133750.html))
- **TD Cowen says cybersecurity consolidation has entered a "more material phase":** The deal comes days after Palo Alto Networks agreed to buy Applied Compute for $4.5B. Index funds that hold both ZS and CRWD will have to rebalance once the deal closes. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/crowdstrike-acquire-zscaler-43-9b-114133750.html), [Benzinga ETFs](https://www.benzinga.com/etfs/sector-etfs/26/11/63527504/crowdstrikes-44b-zscaler-acquisition-could-reshape-cybersecurity-etfs-heres-what-changes-for-investors), [Quartz](https://qz.com/palo-alto-networks-applied-compute-acquisition-ai-111926))
- **Amazon confirmed it is buying Decart's AI optimization technology and team for a reported ~$7.5B:** Amazon is buying Decart's software for speeding up AI work across different chips, and its founders and researchers will join Amazon. It is not buying Decart's AI models. The aim is to make Amazon's Trainium chips easier to use, which weakens the "switching cost" advantage that keeps customers on Nvidia. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/amazon-agrees-buy-decart-ai-150027613.html), [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4675596-amazon-acquires-decart-ais-optimization-tech-to-improve-chip-performance), [Longbridge](https://longbridge.com/en/news/306411757))
- **Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO and chairman, effective immediately:** The board vote was unanimous and followed input from senior US government officials, who say his return poses no national security risk. Colette Kress goes back to being CFO. NVDA rose about 30% Monday morning, recovering much of last week's roughly $900B loss. The SEC accounting probe and an independent board review are still open. ([Nvidia 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm), [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/23/tech/nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-ceo), [PBS/AP](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/nvidia-board-unanimously-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-ceo-and-chairman))
- **Other AI stocks rose with Nvidia:** CNBC lists Dell, Bloom Energy, Marvell and Lumentum among the top movers. Marvell and Lumentum still have their $115B all-stock merger pending. On Friday, Micron fell 12%, Sandisk fell 14% and newly listed Anthropic (ANT) fell about 14%. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/), [CNBC Nov. 20 movers](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/stocks-moving-the-most-today-live-updates.html), [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/marvell-agrees-to-acquire-lumentum-in-all-stock-ai-connectivity-deal/))
- **The IPO calendar is quiet:** No US tech IPOs are scheduled for the Thanksgiving week. AI cloud company Nscale (filed to list as NSCL, seeking up to a $35B valuation) is still waiting to price, and reports say investors are pushing back on that valuation. ([Renaissance Capital](https://www.renaissancecapital.com/IPO-Center/News/121767/UK-based-data-center-provider-Nscale-files-for-an-estimated-2.0-billion-US), [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/nscale-ipo-valuation-investor-pushback/))

**Synthesis:** Today has two big stories. First, Huang's return removes the biggest single worry for AI stocks, so expect a broad relief rally in NVDA and the AI supply chain (MRVL, LITE, DELL, MU, and probably ANT). Still, the SEC probe means the rally may not hold if there are new headlines. Second, deal-making is picking up. CrowdStrike–Zscaler confirms cybersecurity is consolidating into a few big platforms, so watch PANW, OKTA, S, NET and FTNT for takeover speculation, and expect ZS to trade a bit below the deal value until regulators sign off. Amazon's Decart purchase is a long-term bet against Nvidia's software lead and helps the AMZN/AWS story ahead of re:Invent on Nov. 30.
