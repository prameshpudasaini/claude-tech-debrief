# 2026-10-19

- **AMD / World Labs:** AMD agreed to buy Fei-Fei Li's AI startup World Labs (world models) for about $8.2B in stock, with Li becoming AMD's chief scientist. The deal is reported as still needing regulatory and financing clarity; exact announcement date not confirmed.
- **Anthropic IPO:** Reports say Anthropic's debut slipped from October to a possible pre-Thanksgiving November listing at a valuation above $2T. Sources conflict on whether the S-1 is public (leaked draft figures: ~$4.6B revenue, ~$42B net loss, ~$518B compute commitments).
- **Microchip / Hailo:** Microchip Technology agreed to acquire edge-AI chip maker Hailo (reported late September), adding AI inference silicon to its portfolio.
- **Boomi / Elementary Data:** Boomi is acquiring Elementary Data to add data-quality and governance tools for enterprise AI. Boomi is private, so direct stock impact is limited.
- **OpenWorld (NASDAQ: OPNW) / Blocksight:** OpenWorld signed a definitive agreement to buy AI-agent startup Blocksight to support a $1B tokenization push. A small-cap, speculative name.
- **AIOS Tech / SmartCore:** AIOS Tech agreed to acquire SmartCore Holdings in an all-stock deal worth about $100M. Small-cap only.
- **IPO calendar:** Renaissance Capital showed no IPOs scheduled for the week; Oura, Motive and Cerebras remain in the pipeline.

**Synthesis:** It was a quiet weekend for big deals, and several items above were not firmly dated to the past 24 hours, so verify before trading. The main theme is AI consolidation: chip makers (AMD, Microchip) are buying AI talent and edge-inference tech, while the mega-IPOs (Anthropic, OpenAI) loom as a large supply of new AI stock. Watch AMD (dilution versus AI upside), Microchip, and AI-exposed megacaps that may see money rotate if an Anthropic listing lands in November.
