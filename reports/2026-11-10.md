# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-11-10

- **Anthropic sets IPO terms:** An amended S-1 filed Nov 10 proposes 825M Class A shares at $70–$78 (up to ~$64.4B), with a Nasdaq listing under "ANT" and trading reportedly targeted around Nov 16–19. Pricing details are proposed and a draft circulated earlier ($70–$80, 867M shares) was withdrawn as inaccurate. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-price-range-of-70-to-78-in-upcoming-ipo.html), [Invezz](https://invezz.com/news/2026/11/10/anthropic-ipo-price-range/))
- **Chip and cloud giants line up $26B for Anthropic's placement:** Per the filing, Nvidia and Google each take $10B, AMD $2.5B, Broadcom, Micron and Microsoft $1B each, and TSMC $500M, in non-voting stock at the IPO price. This figure comes largely from one aggregator summary, so treat details as unconfirmed. ([InvestingLive](https://investinglive.com/stocks/anthropic-lines-up-26-billion-from-nvidia-google-others-as-ipo-priced-at-70-to-80/), [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-nvidia-google-buy-10b-each-of-anthropics-26b-placement/))
- **Alphabet discloses $548M Qualcomm stake:** A Nov 6 filing shows ~2.98M QCOM shares held via GV, reported Nov 7–9 and read as a sign of deeper ties as Qualcomm targets $15B of data-center revenue by 2029. Sources disagree on how the stake was acquired (one links it to Qualcomm's Modular deal). ([Digitimes](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20261109VL216/alphabet-qualcomm-partnership-data-center-investment.html), [TechCentral](https://www.techcentral.ie/alphabet-takes-548m-stake-in-qualcomm/))
- **Enghouse to buy MIND C.T.I.:** The Canadian software firm (TSX: ENGH) agreed to buy the Nasdaq-listed telecom software maker for ~$25.4M cash ($1.30/share, ~42% premium), closing expected Q1 2027. Small deal, but a clear premium for MNDO holders. ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/enghouse-systems-to-acquire-mind-cti-in-all-cash-transaction-expanding-its-telecom-and-enterprise-uc-offerings-302934816.html))
- **APEX Tech SPAC to merge with Korai Flix:** APEX Tech Acquisition (TRAD) agreed on Nov 9 to combine with Korai Flix at a $3.5B valuation. Details are thin; a speculative SPAC move. ([Panabee](https://www.panabee.com/news/apex-tech-acquisition-earnings-q4-2026))

**Synthesis:** The day is dominated by Anthropic's IPO, which would be one of the largest ever, and its chip and cloud suppliers are investing directly in it. That ties AI model demand to Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, TSMC, Google and Microsoft. Watch those names into the roadshow (as early as Nov 13) and pricing, since a strong book could lift AI hardware sentiment while a weak one could hit it. Also watch Qualcomm (QCOM) as its data-center push draws Alphabet's interest. Note a possible headwind: last week's midterm results included ballot measures that could slow the AI buildout. Fox/Roku (~$22B, announced June) was reported as closed by one source, but most coverage says closing is still expected in H1 2027, so it is excluded here.
