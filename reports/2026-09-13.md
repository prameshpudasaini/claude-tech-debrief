# 2026-09-13

## Tech & Market-Moving Deals

- **Nvidia is reportedly in talks to invest up to $10 billion as an anchor investor in Anthropic's planned IPO**, which could raise as much as $100 billion at a roughly $2 trillion valuation — a deal that, if it holds, would make it the largest IPO in history and cements Nvidia's strategy of backing the AI labs that buy its chips.
- **Silver Lake-backed, Intel-spun-off Altera is preparing a confidential IPO filing that could raise more than $2 billion**, with Barclays, Citi, JPMorgan and Morgan Stanley lined up as underwriters; Intel retains a 49% stake in the FPGA maker, so a strong listing would be a tangible cash-out catalyst for Intel's own balance sheet and AI-adjacent chip story.
- **OpenAI deepened its chip partnership with Samsung**, with the two companies confirming at a Seoul press conference that they're co-developing next-generation custom inference silicon and HBM memory — another step in OpenAI's push to reduce its reliance on Nvidia hardware, alongside its existing Broadcom silicon program.
- **AI inference chip startup Positron raised $875 million in a two-tranche Series C at a $5 billion valuation** (5x its February mark), with backers including NEA, Qatar Investment Authority, Cisco Investments and Dylan Patel's SemiAnalysis Capital — fresh evidence that big money is still chasing alternatives to Nvidia GPUs for AI inference workloads.
- **AMD raised the top end of its long-term AI chip market forecast to $3 trillion by 2030** and previewed its rack-scale "Helios" AI system, which ships its first units this fall — reinforcing bullish sentiment across the chip sector even without a new transaction attached.

## What It Means

With markets closed for the weekend, today's flow is less about closed deals and more about capital lining up for the next leg of the AI trade: Nvidia angling for a strategic stake in Anthropic's blockbuster IPO, Intel-linked Altera testing IPO demand, and a fresh $875 million bet on inference-chip upstart Positron all point to money still chasing both public listings and private alternatives to Nvidia's GPU dominance. Watch NVDA and ANTHROPIC-adjacent headlines as the IPO process advances toward a filing expected before the U.S. midterms, INTC for read-through from the Altera listing, and AMD/custom-silicon names (Samsung, Broadcom, Qualcomm) as the "diversify away from Nvidia" theme keeps building. None of this is closed yet — the Nvidia-Anthropic and Altera items are both still "in talks" or "preparing to file" — so confirmation in the coming days is the next catalyst to track.
