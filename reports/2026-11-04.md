# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-11-04

- **Marvell (MRVL) / Lumentum (LITE):** The roughly $115B Marvell deal for Lumentum, announced Nov 2, is still the biggest tech deal in play. It was first reported Oct 28 and Marvell fell about 12–13% on dilution and financing worries; it also needs US and Chinese approval.
- **Salesforce (CRM) buys Cresta:** Salesforce signed a deal on Nov 2 to acquire Cresta, a voice-first AI platform for customer service. The price was not found; it adds to Salesforce's push into AI agents.
- **Anthropic IPO (ANT, Nasdaq):** The public S-1 was filed Oct 26 with the price range still blank. The roadshow cannot start before about Nov 10, and a listing in the second half of November is expected (timing and the $1.5T+ valuation talk are unconfirmed).
- **Oracle (ORCL) stock and bond sale:** Oracle priced a roughly $20B stock-and-notes offering, with stock at $116 per share, to fund AI build-out. It adds share supply and dilution (an earlier note said $10B for the stock piece; sources differ).
- **SpaceX / EchoStar (SATS):** Reports say SpaceX is exploring a purchase of EchoStar's Boost Mobile business. These are unconfirmed talks, built on the roughly $17B spectrum deal from 2025.
- **IPO calendar is empty this week:** IPOScoop shows no IPOs set to price, tied to the late-October selloff and the Nov 3 midterm elections.

**Takeaway:** Money is moving into AI through share sales (Oracle, Meta), consolidation (Marvell/Lumentum), and the coming Anthropic listing. Investors are punishing buyers who pay with stock, so watch MRVL, LITE, ORCL and AI-networking rivals like Broadcom and Coherent for dilution and deal-spread moves. A huge Anthropic offering could pull cash from Nasdaq mega-caps like NVDA, MSFT and GOOGL in mid-to-late November. Coverage was thin this morning and no major new big-tech deal surfaced in the past 24 hours; election results may also drive the market today.
