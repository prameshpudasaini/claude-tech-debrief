# 2026-08-24

## Tech & Market-Moving Deals

- **Nvidia is in talks to invest in Perplexity at a $30B+ valuation**, up more than 50% from the AI search startup's ~$20B round a year ago; the deal deepens commercial ties between the two as Perplexity's annualized revenue has tripled to over $750M this year. Watch NVDA, which reports earnings this Wednesday.
- **Hugging Face is exploring a sale that could value it at $13B**, nearly triple its $4.5B valuation from 2023. The "GitHub of AI" model hub has reportedly hired a bank to gauge buyer interest; talks are preliminary and no bidder has been named.
- **XPeng's robotics unit (Dogotix) raised over $900M at a $6.3B post-money valuation**, the largest private funding round yet in China's humanoid-robot sector. IDG Capital led, with Alibaba and Tencent among the strategic backers; mass production of its IRON humanoid is targeted for late 2026. Watch XPEV, BABA and TCEHY.
- **Alibaba priced a $10.2B (HK$80B) share placement** — the largest-ever primary follow-on by a Hong Kong-listed company — to fund its AI buildout, then launched its Wan3.0 video-generation model the next day, which has already climbed to the #2 spot in global rankings ahead of OpenAI's Sora. The capital raise follows a 75% plunge in Alibaba's quarterly profit tied to AI capex.
- **Qualcomm's pending acquisition of AI-infrastructure startup Modular Inc.** remains on track to close in H2 2026 pending regulatory approval, part of Qualcomm's broader push to diversify beyond mobile chips into AI/data-center silicon. Watch QCOM.
- **AMD's ~$219M-plus acquisition of inference-chip startup Taalas** continues moving toward its expected Q4 2026 close; the Toronto startup hardwires trained AI models directly into silicon, and AMD plans to pair its chips with Instinct GPUs and Epyc processors to compete with Nvidia on inference efficiency.

## What it means

The dominant theme remains AI infrastructure consolidation: chipmakers (Nvidia, AMD, Qualcomm) are locking up specialized inference and model talent through investment, licensing or outright acquisition rather than waiting for organic buildout, while capital-hungry AI platforms (Perplexity, Hugging Face, Alibaba, XPeng's robotics arm) are racing to raise at sharply higher valuations to keep pace. The Hugging Face sale exploration and Nvidia-Perplexity talks both point to consolidation among AI infrastructure/tooling players even as valuations keep climbing, which could pressure smaller, less-capitalized AI startups to seek buyers too. Near-term, NVDA's Wednesday earnings and MRVL's Thursday report are the key catalysts to watch, alongside any confirmation of the Nvidia-Perplexity round or a named Hugging Face bidder — both would be read as bullish signals for AI infrastructure spending broadly.
