# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-11-21

- **Nvidia falls 14% after Reuters reports an SEC probe into its accounting:** Two people briefed on the matter say SEC enforcement staff are looking at possible accounting issues at Nvidia. It is not clear which practices are involved or when the inquiry started, and neither the SEC nor Nvidia commented. The stock was already under pressure from Jensen Huang's Nov. 12 resignation and from a DOJ review of Nvidia's $12.9B Hugging Face acquisition. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-20/wall-street-regulator-probing-nvidia-s-accounting-reuters-says), [The Daily Star/Reuters](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/world/news/nvidias-accounting-focus-us-sec-investigation-sources-say-4329306), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/12/nx-s1-6038865/ceo-jensen-huang-resigns-from-chip-giant-nvidia))
- **Broadcom drops about 11–12% on a report that Google cut 2027 TPU orders:** Analyst Ming-Chi Kuo says Google cut about 1M units (around 20%) from its planned 2027 shipments of the Broadcom-designed TPU 8i, mostly the pricier "8i Max," and is shifting some volume to MediaTek's TPU 8t. Google and Broadcom have not confirmed it. Marvell, another custom AI chip designer, fell about 10% on the same news. ([StockAnalysis](https://stockanalysis.com/news/broadcom-falls-google-tpu-cut-mediatek/), [24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/20/broadcom-plunges-11-as-google-reportedly-shifts-tpu-orders-to-mediatek-overshadowing-goldmans-460-target-marvell-sinks-10/), [TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/broadcom-stock-avgo-sinks-on-report-google-is-shaking-up-its-ai-chip-plans))

The S&P 500 fell 3.7% to 6,910.39, putting it in a correction, and the Dow lost 950 points. The Nasdaq-100 was down 20% from its high during the day, which is bear-market territory. The Nasdaq lost about 10% for the week, its worst week in more than six years, while energy and utilities rose. ([NPR](https://npr.org/2026/11/20/g-s1-154797/nasdaq-sp500-stock-market-ai-sink-negative), [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/11/20/stock-market-today-nov-20-stocks-plunge-as-ai-trade-unwinds/), [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/equities/26/11/63492512/stock-market-today-nasdaq-100-nvidia-seventh-day-chipmakers-smh))

Big Tech agreed to buy about $26B of ANT at the IPO price, led by NVDA and GOOGL at $10B each, so those paper gains are shrinking too. The talks began before Huang resigned, and Microsoft was also reportedly interested. The deal is small for Nvidia, but it would be a sign that its deal-making continues while it is under investigation. ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/nvidia-bets-at-least-500-million-on-inceptions-diffusion-models-to-strengthen-its-open-source-nemotron-lineup/), [TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/nvidia-nears-500m-deal-for-ai-startup-inception-to-strengthen-ai-models))

Separately, Tesla and SpaceX are still reported to be negotiating price and control for a merger that could be announced in December. Tesla's Dec. 3 annual meeting has no merger item on its agenda. ([Lightwave](https://www.lightwaveonline.com/home/news/55416101/marvell-shores-up-ai-capabilities-with-115b), [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/12/spacex-tesla-merger-announcement-december-report/), [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-spacex-tesla-merger-talks-december-2026/))

**Synthesis:** Friday's moves came from bad news about the AI chip trade, not from new deals. There is now a regulatory question over Nvidia's accounting, and a report suggests Google is ordering fewer custom chips from Broadcom. Together they hit the two main bets on AI hardware, Nvidia's GPUs and Broadcom/Marvell's custom chips, both at once. The fall in Nasdaq-100 stocks is turning into a broad unwinding of AI bets, which hurts all-stock deals (MRVL/LITE) and recent IPOs (ANT) and favors defensive stocks (energy, utilities, staples). Heading into the short Thanksgiving week, watch NVDA for a response from the company or the SEC, AVGO and MediaTek for confirmation from Google, ANT's $83 IPO price, and whether SMH stops falling. Until that happens, expect fewer new tech deals and IPOs, and expect companies with cash to buy only small AI startups.
