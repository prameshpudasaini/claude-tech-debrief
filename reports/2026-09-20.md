# 2026-09-20

## Tech & Market-Moving Deals

- **Nvidia-backed AI cloud firm Nscale filed for a NYSE IPO** (ticker NSCL) targeting a valuation of up to $35 billion, disclosing a $103 billion total contracted backlog and revenue growth of over 1,250% in the first half of 2026; Nvidia is buying $1 billion of the $3.1 billion in convertible bonds Nscale is selling alongside the listing, deepening the chipmaker's direct stake in the AI infrastructure buildout.
- **Nvidia is reportedly weighing investing up to $10 billion into Anthropic's IPO**, which could be the largest public offering ever — Anthropic is said to be targeting roughly $100 billion raised at a valuation near $2 trillion, with pricing eyed before the November midterm elections.
- **Anthropic and Accenture launched a joint AI-safety partnership**, with each company committing at least $1 billion over five years; Accenture — leveraging its Faculty acquisition — becomes Anthropic's first "embedded evaluator," red-teaming models and testing safeguards as part of CEO Dario Amodei's push for third-party oversight of frontier labs.
- **Anthropic and OpenAI are both shopping for smaller AI data-center deals** (in the 20–30 MW range) to add compute capacity faster than mega-campus buildouts allow, per CNBC — a shift that could funnel new business toward regional data-center operators and power suppliers.
- **Cognition, the AI coding startup behind Devin, is raising roughly $1 billion at a $47 billion valuation**, with reported investor demand near $10 billion — the latest sign that capital keeps concentrating in a small tier of frontier AI companies even as the broader funding market tightens.
- **Tata Electronics and Nexperia signed a strategic semiconductor manufacturing partnership**, with Nexperia products set to be produced at Tata's Dholera, India fab and packaged at its Assam facility — part of the broader push to diversify chip supply chains outside China and Taiwan.

## What It Means

The AI infrastructure land-grab keeps widening: Nvidia is now financing both the cloud providers (Nscale) and, potentially, the labs themselves (Anthropic's IPO), effectively underwriting its own demand pipeline — a pattern worth watching for how it plays on NVDA sentiment and any AI-infra IPO comparables once Nscale (NSCL) starts trading. Anthropic is having a busy week on two fronts at once — courting Nvidia for its IPO while also formalizing safety commitments with Accenture — which together signal the company is trying to de-risk its public listing story on both the financial and governance side ahead of a fall filing. Meanwhile, Anthropic's and OpenAI's parallel hunt for smaller, faster-to-build data centers suggests hyperscaler-scale campuses aren't shipping compute fast enough, a theme that could lift regional colocation and power names. Add in continued mega-round AI funding (Cognition) and chip-supply diversification (Tata-Nexperia), and the throughline for traders is the same one driving markets all year: anything tied to AI compute capacity — chips, cloud, power, or capital — keeps attracting outsized money and attention.
