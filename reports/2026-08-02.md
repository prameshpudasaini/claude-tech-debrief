# August 2, 2026 — Tech & Markets Morning Debrief

- **Paramount's takeover of Warner Bros. Discovery is now frozen until a trial or June 1, 2027, whichever comes first**, after Paramount agreed late Friday not to close the deal while state antitrust suits proceed; the previously scheduled August 3 hearing has been called off and the restraining order extended through August 17. This removes the near-term catalyst investors were watching into this week and pushes WBD merger-arb further out.
- **Samsung signed a $200 billion deal with Broadcom running through 2030** to fabricate Broadcom-designed 2nm AI accelerator chips and co-develop next-generation high-bandwidth memory, deepening Broadcom's position as a second source of custom AI silicon alongside Nvidia.
- **SK Group agreed to a $750 billion multi-year memory-chip supply partnership with Nvidia and other tech giants**, the centerpiece of a broader $950 billion US-Korea semiconductor and AI-data-center pact unveiled at the San Francisco AI Summit — a long-duration demand signal for Nvidia's GPU roadmap and Korean memory suppliers.
- **Qualcomm completed its all-stock acquisition of Modular** on July 29, picking up the AI compiler startup's Mojo/MAX software stack to give its data-center chips a CUDA-independent software path as it pushes into AI inference silicon.
- **Together AI closed an $800 million Series C at an $8.3 billion valuation** on July 31, part of a broader $4.2 billion week of AI-infrastructure and energy funding — underscoring how much private capital is still chasing compute and inference-layer startups.

Today's flow keeps two threads going: chipmakers are locking in multi-year, multi-hundred-billion-dollar supply commitments (Samsung-Broadcom, SK-Nvidia) rather than doing outright M&A, which reads as a vote of confidence in sustained AI capex through the rest of the decade — watch **AVGO** and **NVDA** as the clearest beneficiaries. Meanwhile the Paramount-WBD delay removes a merger-arb catalyst traders had circled for this week, so **WBD** and **PARA** volatility around the deal likely cools until the antitrust trial track becomes clearer. Qualcomm's Modular close is worth watching as a signal that **QCOM** is serious about carving out AI inference share from Nvidia's CUDA ecosystem.
