# 2026-09-07

## Tech & Market-Moving Deals

- **Anthropic's IPO has slipped to mid-October, with marketing now expected to begin just before the U.S. midterm elections in November, per Reuters.** The AI lab is still targeting a valuation as high as $2 trillion (potentially surpassing SpaceX as the largest listing ever) and is separately lining up a $15 billion revolving credit facility with Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, and Citigroup as part of the IPO prep.
- **Samsung and SK Hynix finished-DRAM inventories have fallen below 10 days of supply, per a September 7 KB Securities report, with SK Hynix saying DRAM, NAND, and HBM are effectively sold out.** HBM4 production eats roughly triple the wafer capacity of conventional DRAM, and analysts estimate suppliers are meeting only 75–80% of second-half demand — a shortage that sent SK Hynix and Samsung shares sharply higher in Asia trading and keeps the squeeze on U.S. memory play Micron (MU).
- **SoundHound AI completed its acquisition of LivePerson**, combining SoundHound's voice-AI stack with LivePerson's enterprise conversational-AI customer base to create what the companies call an "omnichannel conversational AI powerhouse" — consolidation in a crowded conversational-AI software field.
- **LG Electronics is running a pre-IPO funding round for its robotics unit Bear Robotics, seeking roughly $297 million at a ~$1.5 billion valuation ahead of a planned Nasdaq listing**, a more-than-3x markup from Bear's 2022 round; LG shares jumped over 8% in Seoul trading on the news.
- **Flex agreed to acquire power-electronics maker EPC Power for $4.4 billion** to add 800-volt power-conversion and grid-forming technology for AI data centers, with the combined business slated to be spun off as an independent public company (within Flex's Cloud and Power Infrastructure segment) in Q1 2027.

## What It Means

The AI memory shortage keeps deepening rather than easing — sub-10-day inventories at Samsung and SK Hynix, layered on Micron's already-sold-out 2026 HBM capacity reported earlier this week, point to continued pricing power and margin upside for memory names (MU, and the Korean suppliers) well into 2027. Anthropic's IPO delay to mid-October is the story to watch most closely: pushing marketing to just before the midterms adds political and macro uncertainty to what would be the largest-ever tech listing, and any further slippage would be a signal worth tracking for OpenAI and xAI's own public-market ambitions. Elsewhere, the Flex-EPC Power and SoundHound-LivePerson deals show consolidation continuing in two very different corners of the AI trade — data-center power infrastructure and enterprise conversational software — while LG's Bear Robotics pre-IPO round adds another humanoid/service-robotics name to the pipeline. Note that U.S. markets were closed for Labor Day; watch MU, SOUN, FLEX and Anthropic-IPO headlines when trading resumes Tuesday.
