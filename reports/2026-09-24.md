# 2026-09-24

## Tech & Market-Moving Deals

- **Gen Digital (Norton/LifeLock) made an early-stage takeover offer for GoDaddy worth roughly $12 billion**, per the Financial Times — GoDaddy (GDDY) shares jumped as much as 14% on the report while Gen Digital (GEN) fell about 7.6%, as the market weighed a cybersecurity player buying access to GoDaddy's 20+ million small-business customers and ~81 million domains.
- **Cognex (CGNX) agreed to acquire RealSense, the depth-sensing camera and 3D vision unit Intel spun out in 2025, for $500 million in cash**, pushing the machine-vision company into robotic perception; RealSense is growing revenue over 50% year-over-year toward $80–90 million in 2026, and the deal closes in Q4.
- **Cybersecurity startup Island raised $400 million at a $6.4 billion valuation**, more than doubling from its prior round, as enterprises pour money into defending against AI-driven attacks — another data point for the "AI security" trade that's been lifting names like CrowdStrike and Palo Alto Networks.
- **AI infrastructure startups Modal Labs and Baseten are separately in talks to raise new funding at roughly $15 billion and $26 billion valuations**, both far above their rounds from just months ago, underscoring how fast private capital is chasing the "AI inference/serving" layer beneath the model providers.
- **Microsoft pledged more than $10 billion in new Gulf-region cloud and AI investment through 2030**, formalizing a technology framework with the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and Kuwait that builds on last year's $7.9 billion UAE commitment — the latest sign hyperscalers are racing to lock up international data-center capacity and government AI partnerships.
- **DeepSeek's revenue run rate reportedly crossed $1 billion in the past 24 hours**, showing the low-cost Chinese AI lab can also be a real commercial business — a competitive-pricing overhang worth watching for US model providers (OpenAI, Anthropic) and the chipmakers that supply them.
- **Australian data-center operator Firmus Grid, backed by Blackstone with Nvidia, Meta, and OpenAI as customers, is pressing ahead with a $5 billion-plus IPO** set to price in October — one of the largest listings ever in Australia and a fresh signal of how much capital is still flowing into AI data-center capacity globally.

## What It Means

Today's biggest single mover is the GoDaddy/Gen Digital takeover chatter — GDDY and GEN are the pair to watch intraday, and any confirmation or denial from either company will swing both. Beneath that, the theme is unmistakably AI-infrastructure and AI-security capital deployment: Microsoft's Gulf commitment, the Modal/Baseten mega-rounds, and Island's cybersecurity raise all point to money continuing to flow into the "picks and shovels" layer of AI even as headline model competition (DeepSeek hitting $1B run-rate) raises questions about pricing power for US labs. Cognex's move into robotics vision is a smaller but notable signal that traditional industrial-tech names are repositioning around "physical AI." Net-net: watch GDDY/GEN for M&A-driven volatility, MSFT and cybersecurity names (CRWD, PANW, GEN) for AI-security tailwinds, and keep an eye on US AI-model-provider sentiment (and their chip suppliers) as DeepSeek's commercial traction adds a new competitive data point.
