# August 3, 2026 — Tech & Markets Morning Debrief

- **Amazon became the fifth company ever to top a $3 trillion market cap**, hitting a record $287.16 a share Monday as its post-earnings rally extended into a third session — the payoff from last week's 36.7%-growth AWS print and raised $220B capex guidance continuing to pull in buyers. **AMZN** now sits alongside Nvidia, Alphabet, Microsoft, and Apple in the $3T club.
- **Chip stocks sold off sharply even as the broader market rallied**, with AMD down about 4.5%, Micron off 4.2%, and Nvidia down nearly 3%; chip-equipment names got hit harder still, with Applied Materials down almost 5% and KLA Corp down over 5%. The drop extends a pattern from late July and reflects investors rotating out of richly-valued AI hardware plays even as software and non-chip mega-caps (Amazon) keep climbing.
- **Okta agreed to acquire AI identity-security startup Permiso Security for just under $200M**, an almost all-cash deal expected to close in Okta's fiscal Q3 2027. The move bets that securing AI agents and other non-human identities — a fast-growing attack surface as enterprises deploy autonomous software — becomes core to Okta's platform.
- **Wall Street is now drawing a hard line between winners and losers of last week's Big Tech earnings/AI-capex week**: Microsoft and Amazon both rallied (up 4.3% and 5.3% respectively over the week) on evidence that cloud spend is converting into revenue, while Alphabet, Meta, and Oracle all sold off on capex-guidance and free-cash-flow concerns. That divergence is shaping which hyperscaler names funds are rotating into today.
- **Nvidia's reported talks to backstop up to $250B of OpenAI's Ohio data-center financing remain unconfirmed by either company**, keeping "circular financing" concerns alive as a background risk for chip-sector sentiment — a likely contributor to today's semiconductor weakness even without new news on the deal itself.

Today's tape is really a story of two AI trades pulling apart: capital keeps flowing into the hyperscalers proving AI spend converts into cloud revenue (Amazon's $3T milestone, Microsoft's strength), while the chip and equipment names that supply that buildout are getting sold on valuation and circular-financing worries. Watch **AMZN** and **MSFT** for continued strength versus **AMD, NVDA, MU, AMAT, and LRCX** for whether today's chip pullback deepens or stabilizes. Okta's Permiso deal is a smaller but useful read on where security spending is heading next — protecting the AI agents everyone is racing to deploy.
