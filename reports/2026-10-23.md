# 2026-10-23

*Coverage note: web search surfaced few deals dated strictly within the past 24 hours. Items below are labeled with their announcement dates; older items are included only where they still drive trading.*

- **Salesforce (CRM) to buy Braze (BRZE) for $5.66B cash (announced Oct 20):** $47.50/share, about a 58% premium. Braze jumped 50%+ while Salesforce dipped; it adds real-time customer messaging to Agentforce and Marketing Cloud. Closing is expected in Salesforce's fiscal 2028, pending HSR and shareholder votes.
- **Schneider Electric to buy PTC (PTC) for $22.6B cash (announced Oct 5):** $205/share, a 42% premium, funded by about €16-17B of new debt plus a €5-6B equity raise. Closing is expected by Q3 2027. PTC is likely pinned near the offer price, and Schneider's leverage is the thing to watch.
- **Samsung Electro-Mechanics wins a ~$272M memory substrate supply deal (Oct 21):** Another sign that AI memory demand is pulling the supply chain along. Small, but supportive for memory and packaging names such as Micron.
- **Anthropic IPO still pending:** It confidentially filed an S-1 on June 1 at a ~$965B valuation. Reports vary on timing, with some pointing to an October listing, but no public filing or pricing was found. SpaceX and OpenAI have also filed confidentially.
- **Jio Platforms IPO (India) closes for subscriptions today (Oct 23):** A ~₹30,213 crore issue. It is not a US listing, but it is a large global tech/telecom offering that could pull on emerging-market tech flows.

**Takeaway:** Software buyers are paying big premiums for AI-ready platforms (Braze, PTC), while the market punishes the acquirers' stock slightly. Watch CRM, BRZE and PTC for merger-arb spreads. The US mega-IPO pipeline (Anthropic, OpenAI, SpaceX) is the bigger swing factor. Any public S-1 flip would move AI-exposed names such as NVDA, MSFT, GOOGL and AMZN, which hold stakes or supply these companies.
