# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-11-11

- **Anthropic locks in its IPO timeline:** After setting a $70–$78 range (825M shares, up to ~$64.4B raised, ~$1.4T fully diluted valuation, Nasdaq: ANT), reports now point to pricing on Nov 18 and trading on Nov 19, with an unusually large ~20% retail allocation. The valuation is down from earlier ~$1.5–2T talk after the recent AI-stock sell-off. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-price-range-of-70-to-78-in-upcoming-ipo.html), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-ipo-price-range-of-70-to-78-per-share-to-reach-an-implied-valuation-of-1-404-trillion/), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-ipo-price-range-ai-stock-sell-off/))
- **Suppliers buying in, now confirmed in more outlets:** Seven strategic partners will buy ~$26B of non-voting Anthropic stock at the IPO price, led by Nvidia and Alphabet ($10B each), with AMD ($2.5B) and Microsoft, Micron and Broadcom (~$1B each). This ties NVDA, GOOGL, AMD, MU and AVGO directly to how the deal trades. ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/11/10/nvidia-alphabet-and-micron-just-agreed-to-buy-26-billion-of-stock-in-one-of-their-biggest-customers/), [Open](https://openthemagazine.com/business/google-nvidia-and-microsoft-join-26-billion-anthropic-deal-ahead-of-ipo))
- **CoreWeave shows a $132.5B contract backlog:** CRWV's Q3 (reported Nov 10) showed revenue of $3.66B (+168% YoY), backlog up 138% YoY, a raised 2026 outlook of $13–13.3B, and ~$9B of customer prepayments expected within three months. The stock jumped ~15% premarket and lifted AI names, though short seller Jim Chanos flagged weak returns on capital. ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001769628/000176962826000461/coreweave3q26earningspress.htm), [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/earnings/26/11/63134315/crwv-stock-surges-15-pre-market-as-coreweave-touts-133-billion-backlog-but-short-seller-jim-chanos-warns-of-weak-returns-on-capital-2-3-pre-tax-roic))
- **Lumentum bounces as the Marvell deal settles in:** LITE rose ~9% on Nov 11, recovering part of its steep drop after Marvell's ~$115B all-stock offer (Nov 2; 4.5 MRVL shares per LITE share, close expected 2028). The spread to the offer value is worth watching as an arbitrage signal. ([Finimize](https://finimize.com/content/tech-stocks-slip-as-marvell-lines-up-a-115-billion-deal), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-02/marvell-reaches-115-billion-deal-for-lumentum-holdings))
- **Mitsui Chemicals to buy chip-materials maker JSR for ~$11.65B:** The cash deal (with DBJ) buys JSR, a major photoresist supplier, from JIC Capital funds. This consolidates a key part of the semiconductor supply chain that US chipmakers and equipment makers rely on. ([IBTimes Japan](https://jp.ibtimes.com/mitsui-chemicals-agrees-acquire-jsr-1165-billion-deal-105165))
- **AI startups keep consolidating:** OpenAI reportedly bought AI travel-agent startup Odessia (terms undisclosed, not confirmed by OpenAI), and Cogent agreed to buy Google-backed AI security firm Specular. Both are private, so the direct stock impact is limited. They matter more as signals for online travel names (BKNG, EXPE) and cybersecurity peers. ([BetaKit](https://betakit.com/openai-acquires-sonder-co-founders-new-travel-tech-company-odessia/), [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/newsroom/subject/merger-acquisition))

**Synthesis:** Anthropic's IPO is the story that will drive tech trading for the next week. A strong book into Nov 18 pricing would likely lift the chip and cloud names that are buying into it (NVDA, GOOGL, AMD, MU, AVGO), while a cut range or a weak debut would add to the recent AI sell-off. CoreWeave's huge backlog gave AI infrastructure some relief, but the doubts about returns on capital and AI debt haven't gone away, so expect choppy trading in CRWV and other neocloud and data-center names. Deals in optics (Marvell/Lumentum) and chip materials (Mitsui/JSR) show buyers are still paying up for the parts of the supply chain that limit how fast AI can grow. It was otherwise a quiet day for new large deals: Shield AI/Echodyne ($5.3B) and Salesforce/Cresta (reportedly $2.7B) were announced earlier, and a reported Oura/Counsel Health deal could not be confirmed, so all three are excluded.
