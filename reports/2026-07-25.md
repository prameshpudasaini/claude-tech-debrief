# July 25, 2026 — Tech & Markets Morning Debrief

- **Nvidia- and Microsoft-backed consortium closes record $40B acquisition of Aligned Data Centers.** The AIP/MGX/BlackRock GIP-led group (with Nvidia, Microsoft, xAI, and Kuwait Investment Authority as backers) completed 100% acquisition of the 51-campus, 6.4GW data-center operator from Macquarie — the largest data-center deal ever, and a signal that AI infrastructure capex shows no sign of slowing.
- **Samsung and SK Hynix unveil $950B in US chip-supply partnerships** with Nvidia, Broadcom, and other American tech giants, unveiled at a San Francisco AI summit tied to South Korea's presidential visit. Samsung signed a ~$200B deal to fab Broadcom-designed chips on its 2nm process, while SK Group and Nvidia agreed a $500B+ tie-up spanning AI-factory buildout and next-gen memory supply.
- **Anthropic confidentially filed for an IPO**, targeting an October 2026 Nasdaq listing that could raise more than $60B with Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, and Morgan Stanley leading — putting it ahead of rival OpenAI, which filed confidentially in June, in the race to be the first frontier AI lab to go public.
- **Uber shares fell 4.3% (~$6.1B in market cap wiped out)** after reports that Waymo is weighing an exit from its robotaxi partnership with the company, casting doubt on Uber's autonomous-vehicle strategy just as competition in the space intensifies.
- **Tesla shed a record $214.5B in market cap on Thursday**, closing out a week where the stock lost roughly 19%, dragging on broader tech-index sentiment ahead of this week's Fed rate decision.
- **Chip stocks broadly under pressure**: Micron fell 7% and the VanEck Semiconductor ETF (SMH) pulled back 3%, as the Nasdaq booked its second straight weekly loss (-2.1%) amid investor unease over AI spending outpacing operating cash flow.

Today's headline theme is AI infrastructure consolidation at unprecedented scale: the $40B Aligned Data Centers buyout and the $950B US-Korea chip supply pact both show Nvidia, Microsoft, Samsung, and SK Hynix locking in capacity and equity stakes years in advance, even as the broader market grows nervous that AI capex is outrunning returns. Anthropic's IPO filing adds a fresh watch item — expect volatility in AI-adjacent names as the October listing timeline firms up. Near-term, watch **NVDA** and **MU/SMH** (chip-supply deal follow-through vs. capex-fatigue selling), **UBER** (Waymo partnership risk), and **TSLA** (continued momentum after its steep weekly decline) for the most tradable setups.
