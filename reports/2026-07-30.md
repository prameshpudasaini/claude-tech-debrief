# July 30, 2026 — Tech & Markets Morning Debrief

- **Microsoft's Q4 earnings blew past expectations**, with Azure cloud revenue growing at its fastest clip in four years — shares jumped as much as 15% on the print, the single biggest tech mover of the morning and a strong signal that AI infrastructure spend is starting to convert into cloud revenue.
- **Meta fell as much as 10% despite beating earnings**, after raising its 2026 AI capex guidance to $130B–$145B while free cash flow collapsed to $784M from $8.5B a year ago — investors punished the spending outlook even as the underlying quarter beat estimates, extending the market's "show me the returns" scrutiny of hyperscaler AI budgets.
- **Intercontinental Exchange (ICE) agreed to acquire MarketAxess Holdings in a $5.7B all-cash deal**, consolidating electronic fixed-income trading infrastructure — MarketAxess shares soared nearly 30% on the announcement.
- **Lam Research surged 14%** after the semiconductor-equipment maker posted record quarterly revenue and earnings, underscoring continued strength in chip-fab capital spending even as AI-capex anxiety weighs on other parts of the sector.
- **Amazon is moving to acquire satellite operator Globalstar for roughly $11.6B** and has filed with the FCC for a ~5,000-satellite direct-to-phone constellation, escalating its space-based connectivity push into direct competition with SpaceX's Starlink.
- **Apple and Amazon both report earnings after today's close**, the final leg of this week's Big Tech earnings gauntlet (Microsoft and Meta already reported) — the market's read on Apple's and Amazon's own AI capex commitments will likely set the tone for tech trading into next week.

The dominant theme this morning is a market sharply bifurcating "good" AI spending from "bad" AI spending: Microsoft's Azure beat proved infrastructure investment can show up as revenue, while Meta's ballooning capex with shrinking free cash flow triggered a sell-off — a split investors will keep applying to Apple and Amazon tonight. Watch **MSFT, META, AAPL, and AMZN** for the rest of today's session, **MKTX** on deal-premium follow-through, and semiconductor-equipment names like **LRCX** as a read on whether fab spending keeps outrunning capex fatigue elsewhere in the chip supply chain. Amazon's Globalstar move is also worth tracking as a sign hyperscalers are looking beyond data centers for their next big infrastructure bet.
