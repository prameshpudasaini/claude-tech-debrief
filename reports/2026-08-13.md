# 2026-08-13

## Tech & Market-Moving Deals (last 24 hours)

- **Anthropic in talks to buy Israeli AI startup Decart for ~$6B.** Per Bloomberg, this would be Anthropic's largest acquisition to date (its fifth this year) and a roughly 50% premium to Decart's ~$4B May valuation. Decart's tech helps chips run AI training/inference more efficiently and includes real-time video models — a fit for Anthropic's compute crunch as it preps a reported October IPO.
- **Vantage Data Centers explores a $100B IPO or sale.** The Silver Lake/DigitalBridge-backed hyperscale data-center operator is in early talks with advisers on an offering that could raise ~$10B and would be the largest data-center IPO ever, underscoring how much capital is chasing AI compute buildout.
- **Switch files for a US IPO targeting a $50B valuation (~$80B including debt).** The data-center operator is aiming to raise $10B with an earliest listing in Q4 2026, adding to a fast-growing pipeline of data-center listings competing for the same investor dollars as Vantage.
- **OpenAI acquires presentation-software startup NextSlide.** A small bolt-on that extends OpenAI's consumer/enterprise productivity tooling around ChatGPT, arriving as OpenAI reportedly prepares to file its S-1 within weeks ahead of a targeted September IPO.
- **Teledyne Technologies to acquire Varex Imaging for $1.1B.** Extends Teledyne's imaging/sensor portfolio into medical and industrial X-ray components.
- **xAI (SpaceXAI) launches Grok 4.6.** The new flagship model targets long-running agentic and coding workloads and is roughly on par with GPT-5.6 on independent benchmarks, keeping competitive pressure on OpenAI and Google in the frontier-model race.
- **S&P 500 sets a fresh record above 7,800**, led by tech and semiconductor strength on cooling inflation data — but the rally is uneven: Cerebras shares fell over 15% on mixed Q2 results, a reminder that AI-hardware earnings are being scrutinized closely even as index-level sentiment stays bullish.

## What it means

The dominant theme is capital formation around AI infrastructure: model labs (Anthropic, OpenAI) are doing bolt-on acquisitions to shore up compute and product breadth ahead of their own blockbuster IPOs, while data-center operators (Vantage, Switch) race to tap public markets for the tens of billions needed to build out capacity. For traders, that means watching the IPO pipeline (Vantage, Switch, OpenAI, Anthropic) as a gauge of investor appetite for AI infrastructure risk, and treating individual AI-hardware earnings (Cerebras today) as potential volatility triggers even while the broader Nasdaq/S&P trend stays firmly up. Near-term, expect continued strength in mega-cap tech and semis on the record-setting tape, but with growing dispersion between AI winners with proven revenue (Anthropic, OpenAI) and hardware/compute plays still working out margins.
