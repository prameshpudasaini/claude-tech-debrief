# 2026-09-16

## Tech & Market-Moving Deals

- **Intel and SK Hynix are in talks over a first-ever U.S. memory-chip manufacturing tie-up**, with options including SK Hynix leasing space at Intel's planned Ohio fab or a joint venture with major cloud providers to secure memory supply; Reuters' report sent Intel up as much as 5.3% and SK Hynix's ADR up over 3% in Wednesday trading, though SK Hynix cautioned nothing is finalized.
- **OpenAI is reportedly in talks for a new funding round valuing the company at up to $1.5 trillion** (more than double its last $730B mark), even as Sam Altman confirmed the company's IPO won't happen in 2026 and is being pushed toward 2027, citing AI-safety concerns as the reason for the delay rather than market conditions.
- **A chip-sector selloff triggered by Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's public call to slow frontier AI development** (echoed by Sam Altman and Elon Musk) knocked nearly 6% off the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index on Monday — Intel fell ~6%, Marvell dropped 7.4%, AMD slid over 5%, and Nvidia lost 3.4% — as investors weighed the risk of AI capex deceleration.
- **Chip and AI names have since rebounded into Wednesday's Fed decision**, with Qualcomm up 4% and AMD up 2.3% on Tuesday, while cybersecurity stocks (CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks, Fortinet) rallied 9–14% as capital rotated toward "AI-risk-mitigation" plays — a rotation worth watching if AI-safety headlines keep recurring.
- **Qualcomm shares hit a two-month high**, extending gains tied to its multi-generational AI chip partnership with Amazon Web Services (warrants for a ~$4B stake, up to $60B in total payments), as StoneX reiterated a Buy rating citing improving leverage in Qualcomm's nascent data-center business.
- **Markets are also digesting the Fed's rate decision due today**, with futures pricing in roughly 93% odds of a move; tech and chip stocks led broader index gains into the announcement, with the Nasdaq outperforming the Dow and S&P 500 in early trading.

## What It Means

The dominant thread this week is a tug-of-war between AI-infrastructure optimism and AI-safety anxiety: Amodei's slowdown call briefly wiped billions off chip valuations, but the sector has already clawed most of it back, and today's Intel/SK Hynix report shows the underlying AI-driven demand story (more fabs, more memory, more custom silicon) is still intact. Watch INTC and SKHY for confirmation of an actual deal, QCOM as its AWS partnership continues to re-rate the stock, and any of the major chip names (NVDA, AMD, MRVL) for a repeat selloff if more AI labs join Amodei's call to slow development. OpenAI's swelling private valuation ($1.2–1.5T) alongside its IPO delay is also worth tracking — it signals investors are still willing to pay up for AI leaders privately even as public-market entry gets pushed further out, which keeps pressure on Microsoft (its largest backer) and rival labs' own fundraising math.
