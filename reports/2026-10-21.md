# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-10-21

- **Salesforce to buy Braze (CRM, Braze):** Salesforce agreed to acquire the customer-engagement platform Braze for about $5.66B ($47.50/share cash, ~58% premium), pushing further into marketing to AI agents. Braze shares jumped over 50%; the deal still needs Braze shareholder and regulatory approval.
- **Jio Platforms IPO opens (Meta, Alphabet as backers):** Reliance's Jio Platforms opened a ~$3.2–3.8B IPO in India, which would be the country's largest ever, with anchor bids reportedly above $26B. Listing is expected around Oct 28; Meta and Google are existing investors.
- **Apex Tech Acquisition to merge with Korai Flix:** The SPAC agreed to merge with the South Korean AI entertainment platform at a $3.5B pre-money valuation, closing expected Q1 2027. Small-cap, speculative SPAC-style name.
- **UK neocloud (ticker NSCL) NYSE IPO pending:** The Nvidia-powered AI data-center operator filed in September and could raise roughly $2B, but has not yet priced. Watch for a pricing date.
- **Still in focus: Broadcom–Anthropic and OpenAI funding (reported Oct 1):** Broadcom is reported to lend Anthropic up to $42B to lease its chips, and OpenAI is reportedly seeking $30B at a ~$1.4T valuation. These are not new today but keep AI chip demand in play.

**Take:** A light day for big-cap deals. The one clear US mover is software M&A: Salesforce paying a hefty premium for Braze signals that AI-agent tools are driving buyouts, which could lift mid-cap software names seen as targets. Watch CRM and BRZE, plus Nvidia/Broadcom/AMD as AI funding keeps feeding chip demand. The IPO market is mixed (some deals cut in size), so the Jio debut and the NSCL pricing are the tests of risk appetite.

*Note: searches found no major new Nvidia/Apple/Microsoft/Google/Meta/AMD/Qualcomm deal in the past 24 hours; the Meta/Nvidia/AMD data-center standards effort (about Oct 12) is not an investment.*
