# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-10-11

*Sunday: markets are closed and the past 24 hours were quiet, so this covers the last several days' deals. Some figures come from press releases and newsletters.*

- **Schneider Electric to buy PTC (Oct 5):** All-cash deal at $205/share, about $22.6B equity value (reported ~42% premium). PTC (NASDAQ: PTC) is a big industrial-software name; it should trade near the offer price.
- **SAP to acquire TechWolf (Oct 6):** SAP is buying the AI work-intelligence startup; terms were not disclosed, and it should close in Q4 2026 pending approvals. A small deal, but it shows enterprise software buying AI talent.
- **Dynatrace closes Arize deal (Oct 2):** Dynatrace (DT) completed its purchase of the AI observability and evaluation platform, extending its monitoring into AI development.
- **Broadcom in talks on ~$30B AI chip financing (Oct 9, reported):** Early talks to arrange debt that would help OpenAI buy the custom chips the two are building. No formal process has begun and plans could change; this is a positive for AVGO's AI order book.
- **Nvidia-backed Firmus IPO pulled (Oct 8):** The Australian data-center firm's listing failed to draw enough demand at A$11 per share. Bloomberg frames it as a warning on AI infrastructure funding appetite.
- **SpaceX reportedly buying Grain's low-band spectrum (~$8B):** Subject to FCC approval. Worth watching for telecom and satellite names such as EchoStar and AST SpaceMobile.
- **IPO pipeline:** Nscale (London AI infrastructure) reportedly filed on Sep 18 with pricing expected mid-October; Cerebras (CBRS) listing timing is unclear across sources.

**Takeaway:** Cash buyers are paying big premiums for industrial and enterprise software (PTC, TechWolf, Arize), while AI infrastructure financing is getting pickier, as the Firmus IPO pull shows. Watch PTC for merger-arb spread, AVGO and OpenAI-linked chip names on the financing story, and the Nscale IPO this week as a test of AI IPO demand.
