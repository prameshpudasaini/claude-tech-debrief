**Synthesis:** The record Anthropic IPO did not turn the market around. ANT gained 17% on its first day but gave part of it back today, and investors are using the strength to sell chip stocks (AMD, MU, INTC, TSM). That means doubts about AI spending, not a lack of demand for AI stocks, are setting direction right now. Software companies are reshaping themselves to fit an AI market. Intuit is dumping a slow-growing unit at a big loss to fund buybacks, while Palo Alto is buying AI startups, and the market is rewarding the companies that buy growth over those that sell off what isn't working. Today, watch whether ANT holds above its $83 IPO price and whether AMD finds a floor. A break below $83 or more chip-stock selling would point to more tech weakness into Thanksgiving week. INTU and PANW are the stocks to watch for whether the market likes a given software deal.
