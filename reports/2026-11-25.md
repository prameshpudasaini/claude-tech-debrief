# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-11-25

- **Amazon is buying Decart's AI chip software for a reported ~$7.5B:** Amazon signed a deal to buy the technology behind Decart's "Optimization Stack" and hire its founders and engineers. The software helps AI models run faster on Amazon's own Trainium and Inferentia chips, which strengthens AWS against Nvidia's lock on AI computing. The price comes from a Bloomberg report, not from Amazon, and the deal still needs regulatory approval. ([GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/amazon-is-buying-decarts-ai-chip-software-for-a-reported-7-5b-and-bringing-its-founders-aboard/), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/amazon-buy-decart-ai-tech-013339837.html), [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/amazon-to-buy-israeli-startup-decart-for-a-reported-7-5-billion-tapping-ai-talent/))
- **Trump floated the US government taking a stake in Nvidia:** In a New York Post interview, he said it is "probably not too late," modeled on the government's ~10% stake in Intel. He gave no size, price or plan, and there is no term sheet. NVDA fell about 5.9% to ~$155 on Nov. 24. ([24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/25/trump-floats-an-intel-style-nvidia-stake-10-would-cost-the-government-375-billion/), [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/stocks/trump-floats-a-us-government-stake-in-nvidia-calling-the-chipmaker-vital-to-ai-leadership/), [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/trump-us-government-stake-nvidia-insider-trading-lawsuit-1834646))
- **Chip stocks are falling hard:** The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 7.88% on Tuesday, wiping out most of Monday's rally after Jensen Huang returned as CEO. Selling continued Wednesday, with NVDA, MU and AMD each down 5–6% before the open and Reuters reporting the index down another ~5.8%. The causes are doubts about AI spending, new tariff proposals and a hot inflation reading. ([BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/14f7aa3e-e4cc-4bf8-9215-c55a9c5ca196), [Investing.com/Reuters](https://ca.investing.com/news/economy-news/nasdaq-slides-4-as-ai-selloff-tariff-proposals-rattle-wall-st-4981250), [Yahoo Finance live](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-wednesday-november-25-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-pce-inflation-data-091428237.html))
- **A proposed US bill would tighten chip export controls:** It would push allies such as the Netherlands and Japan to adopt tougher limits on chip sales to China. ASML fell about 3% before the open, and Dutch lawmakers are pushing back. Chip-equipment makers (ASML, AMAT, LRCX, KLAC) are the most exposed. ([Stocktwits/Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/asml-dips-premarket-amid-chip-092535583.html))
- **Tesla–SpaceX merger talks are still moving:** Five directors are reportedly running the talks for a merger of about $3.2–3.5T, aiming to sign by year-end. Nothing has been signed, and the exchange ratio is still open. Tesla's Dec. 3 annual meeting does not include a merger vote. ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/24/tesla-spacex-merger-year-end-deadline-new-congress/), [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/tradingview:9704f4bb207dd:0-key-facts-tesla-spacex-merger-talks-distant-vote-regulatory-risks/), [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/11/24/tesla-and-spacex-just-assembled-deal-teams-for-a-usd3-2-trillion-merger-here-s-what-investors-need-to-know/))
- **OpenTable (owned by Booking Holdings) is buying me&u:** me&u is an Australian company that makes AI ordering and payment software for restaurants. The price was not disclosed and the deal should close in the first half of 2027. It is small for BKNG, but it shows travel and booking platforms buying their way into AI-powered payments. ([TradingView/PR Newswire](https://www.tradingview.com/news/prnewswire:26ad4deb4bc50:0-opentable-announces-proposed-acquisition-of-australian-restaurant-technology-company-me-u/))
- **Nokia placed more orders for AI photonics chip equipment from Aixtron:** The repeat orders for Aixtron's G10-AsP systems, which make indium phosphide photonic chips, will be delivered through 2027. They expand Nokia's capacity to make optical chips for AI data centers, a small plus for the optical-networking trade (NOK, COHR, LITE). ([Investing.com](https://ca.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/aixtron-and-nokia-expand-inp-photonics-production-for-ai-93CH-4977428))

**Synthesis:** Big economic and political news is moving markets more than deals are. AI-chip stocks are being sold on worries about AI spending, tariffs, export-control risk and talk of the government taking a stake, so expect sharp swings in NVDA, MU, AMD and ASML into the holiday-shortened week. The deal news points the other way: Amazon's Decart purchase shows cloud giants are spending heavily to depend less on Nvidia, a long-term plus for AMZN and its custom chips and a slow headwind for NVDA. TSLA will keep reacting to merger headlines, and a government stake in Nvidia is only talk until a filing appears.

*Note: It was a light day for brand-new tech deals during Thanksgiving week. Amazon announced the Decart deal on Nov. 23–24, but the market is only now reacting to it. Price figures differ by source, so check live quotes before trading.*
