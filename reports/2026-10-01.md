# Tech Deals Morning Brief — 2026-10-01

- **Oura IPO postponed:** The smart-ring maker reportedly pulled its Nasdaq IPO (OURA, ~$14B valuation, 50M shares at $40–44) one day before trading, citing market uncertainty. Four IPOs were postponed or pulled in the past week, seven for Q3.
- **SB Energy IPO delayed:** SoftBank pushed back the Nasdaq listing of its AI data-center developer (targeting $5B+ at a ~$50B valuation) after investors balked at the price and the OpenAI ties. Nvidia holds a $3B stake.
- **Anthropic IPO prospectus details surface:** Its confidential prospectus reportedly warns of "catastrophic or existential" AI risks, with bankers discussing a ~$2T valuation (up from $965B in May). OpenAI's Altman said he has no IPO timeline.
- **Accelevation lists on Nasdaq (Sept 30):** The AI data-center power-equipment maker was set to sell 30M shares at $20–24 (~$660M raise), one of the few deals that got done. Roze AI (RZAI) also listed.
- **IonQ gets regulatory approval for SkyWater Technology acquisition:** Clears the way for the quantum company to close its purchase of the US chip foundry; exact close date and terms not confirmed in this run.
- **Still in play from earlier this month:** AMD–World Labs (~$8.2B, all stock), Nvidia–Hugging Face (~$12.9B), Onsemi–Synaptics (~$7B) and ADI–Alif ($1.35B).

**Takeaway:** The deal theme is shifting from "buy AI" to "can the market pay for AI." Big AI-infrastructure IPOs (SB Energy, Oura) are being delayed on valuation and shaky markets (Dow and S&P posted September losses as Treasury yields rose), while smaller, profitable picks-and-shovels names still list. Watch Nvidia and SoftBank for SB Energy exposure, AMD for dilution from World Labs, IonQ and the quantum group on the SkyWater close, and IPO sentiment ahead of the Anthropic and OpenAI listings.
