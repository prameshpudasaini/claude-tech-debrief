# 2026-10-14

- **Bumble / Ithaca Holdings:** Reuters reports Bumble (BMBL) is in advanced talks to go private with Ithaca Holdings (founded by Ravi Gupta and John Hegeman) at roughly a $1 billion valuation (other coverage cites $1.3B); shares jumped ~50% to about $3.81. Not yet confirmed by either company.
- **Workday / Silver Lake:** Silver Lake is reportedly exploring a take-private of Workday (WDAY) at $50B+ (reported in August, talks preliminary). Workday also announced a definitive agreement to buy agentic identity platform C1.ai at Workday Rising.
- **Nvidia financing push:** Nvidia is working with Wall Street (Goldman Sachs, Apollo and others) on AI data-center financing and may backstop up to 25% of qualifying deals, a potential exposure of up to $125B.
- **Google, Meta, AMD and Nvidia on networking:** At the Open Compute Project summit, Google will support Falcon Transport on Nvidia's ConnectX-10 and BlueField DPUs, with AMD and Meta joining the announcement. Microsoft also unveiled its in-house Maia 300 inference chip, aimed at reducing reliance on Nvidia and AMD.
- **AMD momentum:** BofA raised its AMD price target to $720, citing the Anthropic-Akamai partnership, up to 2 GW of MI450 accelerators for Anthropic, and Microsoft's expanded Helios deployments in Azure.
- **IPO market:** Oura's roughly $2.1-2.2B Nasdaq IPO is still on hold after being pulled Sept 29 on valuation and market-uncertainty concerns. Elsewhere, India's tech IPOs (Fusion CX, Zetwerk, Tonbo Imaging) open this week; there are no confirmed large US tech IPOs.
- **Recent deals still in focus:** AMD's reported ~$8.2B all-stock purchase of World Labs, and CrowdStrike's ~$600M XM Cyber technology deal, both announced earlier.

**Takeaway:** It was a thin day for brand-new deals, and several items above are reports or earlier announcements, not fresh 24-hour news. The themes are the same: take-privates of cheaper software and consumer-app names (Bumble, Workday), and AI infrastructure financing and chips (Nvidia, AMD, Microsoft Maia). Watch BMBL and WDAY for deal headlines, AMD and NVDA for AI capex signals, and the stalled Oura IPO as a sign of how choosy the IPO market is.
