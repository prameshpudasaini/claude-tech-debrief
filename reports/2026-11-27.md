# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-11-27

- **AI and chip stocks are bouncing back before today's half-day session:** As of about 8:30 a.m. ET, Nasdaq-100 futures were up about 2.4% and S&P 500 futures about 1.7%. Before the open, SanDisk (SNDK) was up about 11%, Micron (MU) about 6%, AMD about 4% and Nvidia (NVDA) about 2.5%. Bloom Energy (BE), which powers data centers, was up about 9%. Many of these stocks lost more than a quarter of their value in November, so this looks like bargain-hunting rather than a reaction to new deals. ([Upstox](https://upstox.com/news/market-news/us-stocks/dow-jones-s-and-p-500-nasdaq-futures-hint-gap-up-open-on-black-friday-us-tech-and-ai-stocks-to-watch-today/article-204109/), [MLQ](https://mlq.ai/news/nasdaq-futures-rise-24-before-black-friday-half-day-as-chips-rebound/), [24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/27/stock-market-today-markets-off-to-a-strong-black-friday-start/))
- **Marvell and Lumentum both rose ahead of their pending merger:** Before the open, Marvell (MRVL) was up about 8.6% and Lumentum (LITE) about 7.9%. Marvell agreed on Nov. 3 to buy Lumentum in an all-stock deal, the biggest tech merger of 2026. Because Marvell is paying in stock, LITE's price follows MRVL's. That makes the pair a leveraged bet on demand for the optical connections that link AI hardware in data centers. ([Capital Brief](https://www.capitalbrief.com/briefing/marvell-lumentum-to-merge-in-biggest-deal-of-2026-356716c9-bd96-4f66-a928-f849d9de6357/), [Share Talk](https://www.share-talk.com/nasdaq-futures-surge-2-7-as-nvidia-micron-and-sandisk-lead-global-ai-stock-recovery/))
- **Memory chip makers see supply staying tight, but there is a warning:** Micron's CFO says memory supply should stay tight through 2028, and more than 75% of Micron's 2027 shipments are already committed to customers. Memory price increases of up to 60% are helping SNDK, MU and SK Hynix recover. However, research firm TrendForce warns that DRAM price gains could fade by late 2027. ([24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/27/sandisk-jumps-10-as-investors-return-to-beaten-down-memory-stocks-micron-and-sk-hynix-rise-6-despite-trendforce-dram-warning/), [Pomegra](https://pomegra.io/news/micron-technology-stock-sandisk-rally-as-dip-buyers-return))
- **Anthropic (ANT) is trading back above its IPO price:** Before the open, the stock was around $85, up about 3% and above its $83 IPO price. Anthropic raised $81B on Nov. 18, the largest IPO ever. A stock that stays above its IPO price makes it easier for the next big AI companies to go public, while one that falls below would make it harder. ([Upstox](https://upstox.com/news/market-news/us-stocks/dow-jones-s-and-p-500-nasdaq-futures-hint-gap-up-open-on-black-friday-us-tech-and-ai-stocks-to-watch-today/article-204109/), [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/markets/live-blog/anthropic-ipo-live-updates-rcna610159))
- **Several big tech names are lined up to go public:** Forge's IPO calendar, updated today, lists Blockchain.com, Cohesity, Discord and Strava as having filed privately, with no dates set. Marc Lore's Wonder filed on Nov. 5, and OpenAI filed privately earlier this year but has "not decided on timing." No IPOs are scheduled for this holiday week. ([Forge](https://forgeglobal.com/tech-ipo-calendar-2026/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/05/wonder-confidential-ipo-filing.html), [Renaissance Capital](https://www.renaissancecapital.com/IPO-Center/News))
- **ElevenLabs talks show private investors still pay up for AI apps:** According to TFN, the voice-AI startup's yearly revenue is now about $700M. It is in early talks to raise money at a $33B valuation, up from $22B, and aims to close in Q1 2027. Along with Sierra's recent $30B round, this shows private investors are still paying more for AI app companies even as public AI stocks fall. ([TFN](https://techfundingnews.com/report-elevenlabs-in-early-talks-to-raise-at-33b-valuation-as-annualised-revenue-hits-700m/), [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/25/voice-ai-startup-elevenlabs-reportedly-seeking-new-funding-at-33b-valuation/))

**Synthesis:** Thanksgiving brought almost no new deals, so price moves are the story today. The stocks that fell hardest in November's AI selloff (memory, optical networking, AI chips) are leading the rebound. On a half-day with few traders, moves like this often go too far and can reverse next week. Watch SNDK, MU, MRVL/LITE and NVDA to see whether the gains hold. Also watch ANT, because whether it stays above $83 will shape how eager other AI companies (OpenAI, Discord, Cohesity) are to go public. Many deals are still pending (CrowdStrike–Zscaler, BILL–TPG/Corpay, Palo Alto–Applied Compute, Expedia–Kiwi), so the stocks involved will move on approval headlines rather than new announcements. Expect deal activity to pick up again on Monday.

*Note: Fewer than usual new deals were announced in the past 24 hours because of the US holiday. Pre-market prices are early-morning snapshots and differ by source. All of today's bullets cover market moves, IPO plans and private funding talks, not newly signed deals.*
