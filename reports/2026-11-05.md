# 2026-11-05

- **Marvell / Lumentum:** Marvell agreed to buy optical components maker Lumentum in a deal reported at about $115B including debt (announced Nov 3; sources conflict on terms, reportedly mostly stock). It would be one of the biggest tech deals of 2026 and a major bet on AI data-center networking.
- **Anthropic IPO:** Anthropic's amended S-1 (Nov 2) still shows no price range. Reports point to a roadshow around Nov 9 and pricing in mid-to-late November, at a reported $1.5T–$2T valuation. IPOScoop shows no other IPOs pricing this week.
- **Meta / Conductor:** Meta bought Conductor, a Mac app for running coding agents in parallel (Nov 4). Terms were not disclosed; the cloud product shuts down Nov 20.
- **OpenAI / Pierre Computer:** OpenAI announced plans to buy Pierre Computer, a Git-based code storage and review startup, to strengthen Codex. Price and closing date are not disclosed, and the deal has not closed.
- **Onsemi / Ferric:** Onsemi agreed to buy Ferric, a voltage-regulator technology developer, for $600M cash plus up to $175M in earn-outs (Nov 2–4), targeting AI processor power delivery.
- **CrowdStrike / Fiddler AI:** CrowdStrike bought Fiddler AI (reported ~$400M by the WSJ) and Dreadnode on Nov 3, adding AI security and testing tools.
- **Amdocs / EIS:** Amdocs (DOX) acquired insurance software provider EIS, its first move into insurance. Terms were not found.

**Takeaway:** AI is driving almost every deal, from the AI infrastructure buildout (Marvell/Lumentum, Onsemi/Ferric) to AI coding tools (Meta, OpenAI) and AI security (CrowdStrike). Watch MRVL, LITE, optical and networking names, and power-chip makers like ON. The Anthropic IPO is the big event: a price range in an amended S-1 would be the next signal, and it could pull money from other AI stocks.
