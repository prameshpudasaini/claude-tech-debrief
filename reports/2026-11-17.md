# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-11-17

- **Anthropic holds its IPO range, and demand points higher:** An updated filing Tuesday kept the range at $70–$78 for 825M shares, which would raise more than $64B at about a $1.4T valuation. CNBC sources say it will likely price above the range on Wednesday, with Nasdaq trading as "ANT" on Thursday. New Street started coverage at Buy with a $105 target. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/17/anthropic-ipo-new-street-buy-rating.html), [CNBC guide](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/anthropic-ipo-date-price-range.html), [The Private Banker](https://leprivatebanker.com/2026/11/17/anthropic-signals-ipo-pricing-at-top-of-70-to-78-range-or-higher-raising-up-to-64-billion-ahead-of-wednesday-pricing/))
- **OpenAI signs a 457 MW AMD-based compute deal with TensorWave:** The six-year contract runs on AMD Instinct MI455X GPUs. Half the capacity comes online in H1 2027 and the rest by early 2028. TensorWave is the first neocloud publicly named under OpenAI's 6 GW AMD framework, a real-world test of AMD as an alternative to Nvidia. It didn't stop the chip sell-off intraday. ([Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4673150-tensorwave-enters-six-year-deal-to-provide-openai-with-amd-powered-cloud-compute), [moomoo](https://www.moomoo.com/community/feed/breaking-news-openai-tensorwave-amd-deal-fights-ai-capex-selloff-117627084537862))
- **Nvidia reports after the close tonight, its first quarter without Jensen Huang as CEO:** Guidance was for about $108B in revenue, and interim CEO Colette Kress leads the call. NVDA is down about 24% this month. The Nasdaq 100 fell nearly 2.7% Monday, and futures were soft Tuesday morning. ([Invezz](https://invezz.com/news/2026/11/17/nasdaq-slips-as-wall-street-awaits-nvidia-earnings-and-anthropic-ipo/), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stocks-extend-losses-traders-digest-030006305.html))
- **Nvidia-backed Genesis AI raises $500M at a $3.5B valuation:** Premji Invest, Khosla and Eclipse led the round for the French robotics startup, and Nvidia also took part. It adds to the steady flow of money into robotics and "physical AI," following AMD's World Labs deal. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-17/nvidia-backed-genesis-ai-raises-funds-at-3-5-billion-valuation), [MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/french-robot-maker-genesis-raises-500-million-at-3-5-billion-valuation-ce785edcdb8afe20))
- **AMD reportedly closes its Taalas acquisition:** Some outlets report that AMD has completed its purchase of the Toronto AI-inference chip startup, days after closing World Labs (about $11.1B across the two deals, per FourWeekMBA). AMD hasn't put out a closing release yet, so treat this as unconfirmed. Either way, it's another inference-silicon piece for AMD's challenge to Nvidia. ([FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-amd-closes-taalas-days-after-world-labs-11-1b-in-two-deals/), [AMD IR, original deal](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1296))
- **CCC Intelligent Solutions buyout financing tightens:** JPMorgan-led banks cut borrowing costs on $2.85B of debt funding GTCR and Elliott's take-private of CCCS. The strong debt demand makes it more likely the deal closes, and it shows private equity can still finance software buyouts. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-17/jpmorgan-cuts-borrowing-costs-on-2-85-billion-ccc-debt-deal))
- **Samsung–Modine: still talks, no signed deal:** Neither company has confirmed. Korean press estimates a $7.5–8B price, versus Modine's roughly $5.6B pre-report market value. MOD stays a live takeover play on data-center cooling. ([KED Global](https://www.kedglobal.com/mergers-acquisitions/newsView/ked202611130006), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/modine-stock-gets-potential-5-114103515.html))

**Synthesis:** It was a quiet day for new M&A. All attention is on two events that will set the AI trade's direction: Nvidia's earnings tonight and Anthropic's pricing Wednesday night. The deal flow keeps pointing to two themes. AMD keeps winning compute and buying chip startups to challenge Nvidia (TensorWave/OpenAI, Taalas), and money keeps moving into robotics and data-center infrastructure (Genesis, Modine). Stocks to watch: NVDA, AMD, AVGO, MU and TSM on earnings, and GOOGL, AMZN and MSFT on their Anthropic stakes and an above-range ANT pricing, plus MOD for any Samsung signing. A clean Nvidia beat followed by a hot ANT debut could set up a sharp relief rally after a month-long chip sell-off. A soft guide would likely make investors more cautious about the ANT debut too.
