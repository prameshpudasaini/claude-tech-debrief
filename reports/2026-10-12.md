# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-10-12

*Some items are press reports or talks, not signed deals; figures come from press releases and news reports.*

- **IonQ to buy Elisity (Oct 12):** The quantum computing company agreed to acquire the cybersecurity firm in an all-stock deal worth up to $1.3B, including up to $150M in earnouts. It is expected to close in Q4 2026.
- **Amazon in talks for Decart (Oct 11, reported):** The WSJ says Amazon is negotiating to buy the AI startup for about $7B to make AI cheaper to run. Not signed, and talks could collapse.
- **Schneider Electric to buy PTC (Oct 5):** All-cash at $205/share, about $22.6B equity value. PTC (NASDAQ: PTC) should trade near the offer price.
- **SpaceX buying Grain's low-band spectrum (~$8B):** Subject to FCC approval. CNBC reports the news hit AT&T, Verizon and T-Mobile shares.
- **SpaceX reportedly seeking ~$40B to buy Nvidia chips:** Still being negotiated per one report, which I could not confirm elsewhere. It would be a big order for NVDA if it goes through.
- **Broadcom in early talks on ~$30B AI chip financing (Oct 9, reported):** The debt would help OpenAI buy the custom chips the two are building.
- **Nscale IPO still pending:** The Nvidia-backed AI cloud firm filed an S-1 on Sep 18 for an NYSE listing (NSCL) at a reported valuation of up to $35B. Price and date are not set. The filing flags going-concern worries and a $3.1B Nvidia convertible loan.

**Takeaway:** Buyers keep paying up for software and AI assets (PTC, Elisity, Decart), and AI chip demand is being funded with more debt and vendor financing (Broadcom, Nvidia). Watch PTC for the merger-arb spread, AMZN and IONQ on Monday's open, and the big US carriers on the SpaceX spectrum move. The Nscale IPO is the next test of investor appetite for AI infrastructure, especially after the Firmus IPO was pulled last week.
