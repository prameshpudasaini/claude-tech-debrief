# August 1, 2026 — Tech & Markets Morning Debrief

- **IonQ completed its $1.8B cash-and-stock acquisition of SkyWater Technology** on July 31, merging IonQ's quantum computing roadmap with SkyWater's domestic semiconductor foundry — creating what IonQ calls the only vertically integrated, full-stack quantum platform company and reducing its reliance on third-party chip manufacturing.
- **A group of banks led by Morgan Stanley is in advanced talks to lend roughly $15B to Nexus Data Centers** for a 2,900-acre Anthropic-leased AI campus in Hubbard, Texas. Google is backstopping the debt with its investment-grade credit rating in exchange for a roughly 20% equity stake in the project, deepening its financial exposure to Anthropic beyond its direct stake in the company.
- **Nvidia struck a roughly $5B strategic investment and long-term partnership with Safe Superintelligence (SSI)**, the AI startup led by former OpenAI chief scientist Ilya Sutskever, giving SSI early access to Nvidia's next-generation Vera Rubin GPU platform and a tenfold compute increase over the next year.
- **Warner Bros. Discovery shares climbed 3.2% on July 31** after reports that California Governor Gavin Newsom is pushing to settle the antitrust suit over WBD's proposed sale to Paramount outside of court, narrowing the gap to Paramount's $31-per-share cash offer. A preliminary-injunction hearing on August 3 remains the key near-term catalyst for the deal's fate.
- **Schneider Electric agreed to acquire roughly 90% of AiDASH for $350M in cash**, one of the largest-ever exits in climate-adaptation software, as the French industrial giant builds out its AI-driven grid and infrastructure-resilience offerings.

Today's moves keep pointing to the same story: capital is flooding into AI infrastructure — chips, foundries, data centers, and power — faster than any single company can fund it alone, so hyperscalers are increasingly using equity stakes, credit backstops, and vertical M&A to lock in supply rather than just writing checks. Watch **IONQ** for follow-through on its foundry integration, **GOOGL** as its off-balance-sheet exposure to Anthropic keeps growing, and **NVDA** for how the market prices yet another Nvidia-backed AI lab. **WBD** is the name to watch into Monday's August 3 hearing, since a settlement path would remove a major merger-arb overhang that's weighed on the stock for weeks.
