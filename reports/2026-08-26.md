# 2026-08-26

## Tech & Market-Moving Deals

- **Nvidia reports fiscal Q2 FY2027 earnings today after the close** — consensus calls for ~$92.2B in revenue (~$85.7B from Data Center) and $2.09 adjusted EPS. Options markets are pricing an ~$280B market-cap swing on the print, making this the single biggest catalyst of the week for the entire AI trade. Watch NVDA directly and AMD, AVGO, MRVL, SMCI as read-throughs.
- **OpenAI unveiled its first custom inference chip, "Jalapeño," at the Hot Chips conference (Aug 25)**, with SemiAnalysis-verified benchmarks showing 1.5–1.9x better compute-per-watt and up to 3.6x lower latency than Nvidia's Blackwell on models like DeepSeek R1 and Kimi K2.5. It's inference-only and still low-volume in late 2026, but it lands as a direct "threat to Nvidia margins" narrative right before NVDA's earnings.
- **Anthropic is preparing to pitch IPO investors on a total addressable market exceeding $30 trillion** — topping SpaceX's own $28.5T pitch — while weighing raising as much as $100B at around a $2T valuation. Revenue more than doubled to $11.6B last quarter; a listing could come as early as September, setting the tone for OpenAI's own eventual IPO plans. Watch this as a bellwether for AI-sector valuation appetite.
- **Nayax (NYAX) signed a definitive agreement to acquire smart-parking tech firm IPS Group for $350M in cash** from Windjammer Capital — Nayax's largest acquisition ever, funded with cash on hand plus ~$150M of new debt. Shares jumped over 13% on the news as the deal expands Nayax into autonomous commerce/parking infrastructure across 250,000+ spaces.
- **SpaceX announced a second, $100 billion "Starbase" spaceport in Vermilion Parish, Louisiana** — its largest-ever single investment, with 10 launch pads, propellant production and an airport, expected to break ground in 2027. While SpaceX itself is now public (post its June 2026 IPO), the scale underscores how aggressively AI/space infrastructure capex keeps compounding, with ripple effects for suppliers and materials names.
- **Broader AI M&A backdrop stays hot**: SpaceX's $60B all-stock acquisition of Cursor-maker Anysphere continues moving toward its Q3 2026 close, and reports continue to swirl around Nvidia's talks to invest in Perplexity at a $30B+ valuation and a possible Hugging Face sale near $13B — both still pending confirmation.

## What it means

The AI infrastructure arms race keeps accelerating on two fronts simultaneously: capital (Anthropic's eye-popping $30T TAM pitch and SpaceX's $100B Louisiana spaceport show investors and operators are still willing to underwrite historic sums) and silicon competition (OpenAI's Jalapeño benchmarks landing the day before Nvidia's earnings is a pointed signal that the biggest AI labs are hedging their Nvidia dependency by building custom chips). The timing is no accident — it raises the bar for what Nvidia needs to show tonight to justify its valuation, and any softness in data-center guidance could hit the whole semiconductor complex (AMD, AVGO, MRVL) hard. Outside of AI megacaps, deal appetite is broadening into adjacent tech verticals too, as seen in Nayax's cash acquisition of IPS Group. Near-term, all eyes are on Nvidia's after-hours reaction tonight, followed by how Wall Street digests the custom-silicon threat narrative and whether Anthropic's IPO filing materializes in the coming weeks.
