# 2026-10-10

*Saturday, so US markets are closed. This covers deals announced Oct 8–9 that markets will digest Monday. News flow was thin; a few figures are from secondary sources and flagged.*

- **SpaceX buys wireless spectrum for Starlink mobile (Oct 8):** SpaceX agreed to acquire up to 14 MHz of paired 800 MHz spectrum, Grain Management's nationwide portfolio (originally from T-Mobile), subject to FCC approval. Price undisclosed (an August report put it near $6B). AT&T, Verizon and T-Mobile fell roughly 7% after hours.
- **Broadcom lines up ~$60B in chip financing (reported Oct 9):** Bloomberg reports the financing is meant to help AI labs such as Anthropic buy chips. TSMC shares rose on the news.
- **Microsoft + Nvidia team up on AI-agent Windows PCs (Oct 7–8):** Huang and Nadella said they are co-engineering hardware and software for agents running on Windows. Product, chip and launch details were not given.
- **Nvidia commits $1B to US research capacity:** Covers quantum, healthcare and energy security. It is a funding pledge, not a partner deal, so market impact is likely small.
- **ON Semiconductor / Synaptics (revised Oct 1, still live):** ON's bid was cut to about $5.7B in cash from about $7B in stock after an unsolicited rival proposal for Synaptics. The FTC has cleared it; other regulators are still reviewing.
- **Schneider Electric to buy PTC (Oct 5, still digesting):** Industrial software consolidation; a follow-through name for industrial-tech and software investors.
- **IPO setbacks and pipeline:** Oura's $2.1B US IPO was postponed, and Nvidia-backed Firmus scrapped a $5B Australian IPO amid AI scrutiny. Lambda is raising up to $4B ahead of a planned 2027 IPO. An Anthropic IPO date of Oct 23 appears on one calendar, but it is an unconfirmed placeholder.

**Takeaway:** The big story is connectivity. SpaceX is moving toward becoming a mobile carrier, which is a direct threat to the big three carriers (T, VZ, TMUS). Watch them at Monday's open, though the move could be sharp and then fade given the FCC approval step. AI chip demand stays strong, with fresh financing (Broadcom, TSMC) and Microsoft-Nvidia PC plans supporting semis. The weak IPO news suggests investors are getting pickier about AI valuations. Sources are thin for a weekend, so verify the Monday pre-market moves before trading.
