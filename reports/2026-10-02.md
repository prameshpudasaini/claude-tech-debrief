# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-10-02

- **Anthropic IPO:** Reuters-sourced reports say Anthropic's listing is shifting toward mid-October. The company filed confidentially in June and was last valued near $965B, with Morgan Stanley, Goldman, JPMorgan and Citi leading the deal.
- **Inseego completes Nokia FWA deal:** Inseego closed its purchase of Nokia's Fixed Wireless Access business, which should roughly double Inseego's revenue. Nokia takes about a 7% equity stake (plus warrants) and is putting in $10M more, for about 11% in total.
- **Nebius buys Inferize:** The AI cloud company (NBIS) acquired Inferize, an inference-optimization startup, to strengthen its Token Factory production stack. The deal is small but fits a push to compete on inference cost.
- **OpenWorld / VerifyMe merger closes:** The combined company began trading on NASDAQ on Oct 1 as "OPNW". It is a small-cap listing and likely to be volatile.
- **Lattice Semiconductor to buy AMI ($1.65B):** Lattice is moving into firmware and AI-infrastructure software. Search results did not give a firm announcement date, so check the timing.
- **Still in focus: AMD / World Labs ($8.2B):** AMD's agreed purchase of Fei-Fei Li's World Labs was announced in September. It is a big AI bet that investors are still digesting.

**Synthesis:** The AI buildout is spreading from chips into inference software and the infrastructure around it. Nebius, Lattice and AMD are all paying for software and AI talent. The biggest market event ahead is the Anthropic IPO. A mid-October listing at nearly $1T could pull money from other AI names, or lift them if it goes well. Watch NBIS, AMD, LSCC, Nokia and Inseego for deal-driven moves, and expect AI-heavy stocks to be sensitive to IPO news. Note that few large, brand-new deals surfaced in the past 24 hours, so this brief leans on smaller deals and recent context.
