# 2026-11-06

- **Anthropic IPO wobbles:** Bloomberg reports investors now talk of a $1.2T–$1.3T valuation (down from $1.4T–$1.5T) after Thursday's AI selloff, and Reuters says Anthropic may delay if AI assets stay under pressure. The S-1 is public; roadshow was expected the week of Nov 9 with pricing around Nov 16, and prediction-market odds of a November listing fell to about 54%.
- **Fox–Roku:** TechCrunch reports Fox completed its ~$22B Roku acquisition (announced June at $160/share in cash and stock), far earlier than the first-half-2027 target. Other coverage still calls it pending, so confirm via Fox's filings.
- **OpenAI to buy Pierre Computer (Nov 4):** The developer-tools startup focuses on Git-based code storage and will feed OpenAI's Codex. No price disclosed. Private deal, but it adds pressure on Microsoft/GitHub and the AI coding-tool field.
- **Marvell–Lumentum (Nov 2, still driving trading):** Marvell's ~$115B all-stock bid for the optical component maker sent MRVL down ~12.7% and LITE down ~15% on the day, erasing about $28B combined. Dilution and China regulatory risk are the worries; close is expected in 1H 2028.
- **Qualcomm joins $700M Hark round:** Qualcomm, Nvidia and AMD backed the AI-assistant startup at a ~$6B valuation, reported in recent days. It is a small check, but it shows chipmakers buying demand for their silicon.
- **Nvidia ($3B) and AMD ($1B) in Meta's equity raise (Oct 26–27):** Part of Meta's upsized ~$95–97.5B AI funding, oversubscribed about fivefold. Older news, but it still frames how chip vendors are financing their own customers.

**Takeaway:** Few brand-new deals landed in the last 24 hours, so this note leans on items still moving stocks. The theme is AI money getting pickier: acquirers get punished for dilution (Marvell/Lumentum), and the Anthropic valuation cut shows the IPO window is sensitive to the AI selloff. Watch MRVL, LITE, NVDA, AMD, META and ROKU/FOX, plus any Anthropic pricing news; a delay would likely weigh on AI-linked names, while a clean pricing would help sentiment. Note: some figures come from secondary sources and conflict, as flagged above.
