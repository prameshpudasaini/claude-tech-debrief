# Daily Tech Investment Brief — 2026-11-22

- **CrowdStrike is in talks to buy Zscaler:** Bloomberg reported after Friday's close that CrowdStrike (about $233B market cap) is negotiating to acquire Zscaler (about $32B). No price has been reported and neither company has confirmed. ZS rose about 18% after hours and CRWD fell more than 8%, which signals that investors expect a large premium and some dilution for CrowdStrike. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-20/crowdstrike-is-in-talks-to-buy-cybersecurity-firm-zscaler), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/zscaler-crowdstrike-deal.html))
- **Cybersecurity consolidation is speeding up:** A Saturday Bloomberg analysis describes the CrowdStrike talks as part of AI-driven consolidation in security. They follow Palo Alto Networks' Nov. 18 agreement to buy AI startup Applied Compute in an all-stock deal worth $4.5B, its second-largest deal after CyberArk. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-21/crowdstrike-s-talks-with-zscaler-show-ai-led-cyber-consolidation), [SecurityWeek](https://securityweek.com/palo-alto-networks-to-buy-ai-startup-applied-compute-for-4-5-billion))
- **Amazon is reportedly close to buying Decart for about $7–7.5B:** Reports dated Nov. 21 say talks are advanced for the Israeli AI startup, whose software moves AI workloads across Nvidia GPUs, Google TPUs and Amazon's Trainium chips. Earnouts could take the price to about $7.5B. One unconfirmed report says it could be unveiled at AWS re:Invent on Nov. 30. The deal would strengthen Trainium's position against Nvidia. ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/amazon-nears-deal-ai-startup-decart/), [Ground News](https://ground.news/article/amazon-nears-75-billion-deal-to-buy-ai-startup-decart), [Channel Insider](https://www.channelinsider.com/channel-business/mergers-and-acquisitions/news-amazon-decart-deal-aws-partners/))
- **More details on the SEC probe into Nvidia:** Follow-up reports say the inquiry looks at changes in how Nvidia reports revenue and at its financing deals with AI customers. The WSJ reportedly says it also covers whether Nvidia's disclosures misled investors. Nvidia has also delayed its 10-Q, and interim CEO Colette Kress says it will be "more selective" about financing deals for AI customers. NVDA lost about $900B in market value last week. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/nvidia-stock-extends-25-trillion-rout-as-sec-probe-reportedly-investigates-its-accounting-194945274.html), [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/11/21/after-its-worst-week-in-18-years-nvidia-now-faces-an-sec-investigation-what-it-means-for-investors/), [The AI Insider](https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/11/19/kress-reins-in-nvidias-ai-dealmaking-as-delayed-filing-deepens-scrutiny/))
- **Cognition has offers at a $70–80B valuation:** Business Insider reports that the maker of the Devin coding agent is considering a new round before year-end, only weeks after raising money at $48B. This shows private investors are still paying up for AI even as public AI stocks fall. ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/cognition-funding-talks-80bn-valuation-business-insider))
- **No IPOs are scheduled this week:** Renaissance Capital lists no deals for the short Thanksgiving week. That leaves Anthropic (ANT) and whether it holds its $83 IPO price as the main test of demand for new AI listings. ([Renaissance Capital](https://www.renaissancecapital.com/IPO-Center), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-ipo-ai-offering-price.html))

**Synthesis:** Even as AI hardware stocks fall, companies with strong balance sheets are using the drop to buy. CrowdStrike–Zscaler would be one of the largest cybersecurity deals ever, and Amazon–Decart is a clear bet on cutting its reliance on Nvidia. For Monday, watch ZS and CRWD, and peers like Palo Alto, Okta, SentinelOne and Cloudflare, since takeover expectations can lift the whole group. Also watch AMZN as a possible AI winner if the Decart deal is confirmed, and NVDA for any response from the company or the SEC, or a 10-Q filing date. The main point: while the SEC probe hangs over Nvidia, both money and deal-making are moving toward software, security and alternatives to Nvidia chips.
