# Tech Deals Morning Brief — 2026-09-30

- **AMD to buy World Labs (~$8.2B, all stock):** AMD agreed to acquire Fei-Fei Li's spatial-AI startup, with closing expected by year-end pending regulatory approval. Reports on the stock reaction conflict (one outlet says AMD fell ~4%), so the market is still digesting the dilution.
- **Long Lake Management completes $6.3B Amex GBT acquisition (Sept 29):** The travel-management software and services company is taken private; limited read-through for big-cap tech.
- **Optikos acquires Omega Optical (Sept 29, undisclosed):** A small private optical-components deal with little direct market impact.
- **SB Energy (Nasdaq: SBE) IPO in the pipeline:** The SoftBank-backed AI power and data-center developer is seeking to raise $5–7B at a valuation above $50B, with Nvidia's $3B investment and OpenAI warrants attached. Filed Sept 1–2 and still awaiting pricing, so it is a live catalyst rather than a fresh announcement.
- **Oura (Nasdaq: OURA) IPO pending:** The smart-ring maker's S-1 shows revenue up 74% to $1.21B over nine months and net income of $60.8M. Renaissance Capital expects up to eight roadshows to launch this holiday-shortened week.
- **Still driving coverage, but not new:** Nvidia–Hugging Face ($12.9B), Onsemi–Synaptics (~$7B, all stock) and Analog Devices–Alif ($1.35B cash) were announced earlier in September.

**Takeaway:** AI remains the deal theme. Chipmakers are buying AI software and talent (AMD, Nvidia, Onsemi, ADI), and the IPO pipeline is filling with AI-infrastructure names (SBE, NSCL) and one consumer-health-tech name (OURA). Watch AMD for dilution and execution risk, Nvidia for its exposure to SB Energy and Nscale, and small-cap chip names such as Synaptics for takeover premiums. Roadshow launches this week will show how much appetite investors have for big AI IPOs. Note: search coverage this morning was thin, and only a few items are confirmed as fresh within 24 hours.
